# The rebuilt packet keeps all primitive-power tests

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $D_1$ be the retained first-pass state with a [seed](the-first-pass-selects-disjoint-twist-supports-by-fixed-field-place.md), $D_2$ the transported state after enlargement, and $D_3$ the final rebuilt core. Suppose the Galois, twist and form data of $D_1,D_2$ agree, and the final core keeps the packet data of $D_2$ at the identified coefficient places. Suppose their prescribed exclusion sets correspond, the seed's $A_i$ contain all the design and recovery exclusions, and the final theta data include the entire theta input sets in each layer and on each side. Let $u(v)$ be the old place below a marked final coefficient place $v$. It must be marked in $D_1$. Assume that the rebuilt packet at $v$ is identified with the old packet at $u(v)$ with character raised to a power $a(v)$ prime to its residue characteristic. This last hypothesis is supplied on the retwisted route by the [Frobenius comparison](frobenius-comparison-recovers-a-single-primitive-power-of-a-twist.md).

Then every marked final place satisfies the full good-twist tests for all primitive powers on its chosen side and the extra test against the design exclusion set. The supports at places with different selected indices are disjoint. These are the clauses of `GoodO` on the rebuilt core.

First compose the two identifications of data. The index, support, root level and character at a final place can then all be compared with the seed at $u(v)$. Transport the seed's truncation exponents and finite basis lists. The full theta input assumption is used here: at any relevant layer the underlying set of allowed theta inputs is the entire set in both the seed and the rebuilt core. Thus the test on that set is unchanged by replacing its description with the full set and then transporting the lists, actions and coefficient rings.

Fix a marked $v$, let $\ell$ be its index characteristic, and write $\psi$ for the old character. The rebuilt one is $\psi^a$. If a final test raises it to a further power $c$ with $\gcd(|c|,\ell)=1$ and reads it on its chosen side, the tested character is $\psi^{ac}$ on one side and $\psi^{-ac}$ on the other. This holds because the other-side read inverts the character. Both exponents are prime to $\ell$. Hence the seed supplies the requisite rank and good-form tests for that very character. The tests with smaller exclusion sets follow by restricting the avoidance condition, and the corresponding ring and basis identifications carry them to the final core. Take $c=1$ with the design exclusion set to get the extra design test, and all $c$ with the recovery exclusion set to get the all-powers condition. Finally raising to a power leaves the support identical in the packet. If two final indices differ, their seed indices differ, so their supports are disjoint by the seed's construction.

In the application the simultaneous source of these identifications is the carried third level of the retwisted construction. The read there is set to the seed read pulled back along the coefficient primes. The finiteness step keeps this read in the selected level-three packet. One can then use the same normalizing data for the rebuilt core, with the good-twist certificate adjoined before passing to the next level.
