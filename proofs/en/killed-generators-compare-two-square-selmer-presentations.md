# Killed generators compare two square Selmer presentations

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $B$ be a commutative ring and $0\to U\to M'\to M\to0$ be exact. Suppose $U$ is generated by $k$ elements and $xU=0$, and that $M'$ and $M$ have square presentation matrices $\Phi'$ and $\Phi$. Then $x^k\det\Phi$ is a multiple of $\det\Phi'$. If $\Phi'$ has positive size, $x^k$ times each entry of ${\rm adj}(\Phi)$ belongs to the ideal generated by ${\rm adj}(\Phi')$; if $\Phi$ is empty, the corresponding assertion is $x^k\in({\rm adj}(\Phi'))$. The determinant of an empty matrix is $1$.

Choose $k$ generators of $U$, and lift the free presentation generators of $M$ to $M'$. These elements generate $M'$. Relations include $x$ times each chosen generator of $U$, and each column of $\Phi$ up to correction in the $U$-coordinates. They give the columns of a block matrix $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}xI_k&C\\0&\Phi\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ in the relation module of this new presentation of $M'$. Its determinant is $x^k\det\Phi$, and deleting a row and a column in the $\Phi$-block gives $x^k$ times a cofactor of $\Phi$, up to sign. When $\Phi$ is empty and $k>0$, this presentation yields $x^{k-1}$ in the first Fitting ideal, hence $x^k$ there. When $k=0$ and $\Phi$ is empty, $M'=0$. By presentation independence of Fitting ideals, the displayed minors lie in the determinant and first Fitting ideals of the original square presentation of $M'$. For the square presentation with positive size those ideals are generated by the determinant and the adjugate entries, respectively.

In the Selmer comparison, specialize the larger group ring by sending the new group generator to $1$, then reduce both presentations modulo $I_0=(\ell^\nu)\mathfrak m_O$. The two twists now coincide, and the two Selmer groups differ only by relaxing the conditions at a conjugate pair $v,\bar v$ of new places. Reduction makes the rank-$r$ untwisted Galois matrix the identity on both decomposition groups; the old twist is unramified there, and its Frobenius values at $v$ and $\bar v$ are reciprocal. Let $\theta$ be the value of the old group-valued character on Frobenius at $v$, and set $x=\theta-1$ in the reduced coefficient ring. The twisted Frobenius scalar at each place is then $\theta$ or $\theta^{-1}$, so its difference from $1$ is a unit multiple of $x$. The norms are $1$ in the coefficient ring. Inertia acts trivially and has pro-$\ell$ tame quotient generated by one element $\gamma$ at each place. If $c$ is a cocycle, the tame relation gives $(\rho_{\rm tw}({\rm Frob}_v)-Nv)c(\gamma)=0$ and the same equation at $\bar v$. Thus $x$ kills every coordinate of both inertia evaluations.

For coefficient modules $C$ over the reduction ring, the kernels of the two matrices on $C$-valued vectors are represented by their transposed cokernels; a kernel vector is precisely a list of images of the free generators compatible with the relations. Evaluate the relaxed universal class on the two inertia generators in its own representing module. The resulting $2r$ coordinates give $2r$ elements in that module, all killed by $x$. They evaluate to zero under a homomorphism to $C$ exactly when both local classes are unramified. Consequently the stricter representing module is the quotient by those elements. Applying the first paragraph with $k=2r$ gives the comparison of reduced determinants and adjugates. In the larger group ring coordinates restricted to the old set, $x$ lifts to $\prod_j(1+X_j)^{d_j({\rm Frob}_v)}-1$, with representatives $0\le d_j<\ell^M$. The distinguished unused first-block coordinate has Frobenius value $1$ in additive notation, so that variable has linear coefficient $1$ in this lift.

**Lean correspondence.** `exists_gens` obtains the killed generators by reading tame inertia and `cok_equiv_of_exact` makes the representing-module comparison. `fitting_exact` proves the block-minor assertion and `compare` specializes these results to the auxiliary prime.
