# A definite theta lift in the stable range

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $E/F$ be a CM extension with $F=E^+$ and complex conjugation $\sigma$. A base $B$ fixes one complex embedding $\tau_v$ above each real place $v$, an additive character $\psi$ of $\mathbb A_F/F$, and the split-place data for the lattice models. The base conditions in this theorem are the following.

- $E$ contains $x$ with $x^2=-D$ for a positive integer $D$, and $\mathcal O_E/\mathcal O_F$ is unramified.
- At infinity $\psi$ is $\exp(-2\pi i\operatorname{Tr}_{F_\infty/\mathbb R}(\cdot))$. Every inert finite place avoids residue characteristic $2$, has residue size greater than $3$, and has the local integers as their own annihilator for the pairing $(a,b)\mapsto\psi_v(ab)$. The last condition also holds at every place in the finite set $P$ of the base.
- In addition to $P$ and its chosen split embeddings, $B$ contains one distinguished finite split place and embedding, and two lists of integer exponents at the real places. The theorem quantifies over $B$ with these data. The angular exponents of its two input characters appear separately below.

Fix $n\ge1$ and a source datum $X$ of dimension $n$ with Gram matrix $I_n$ and signature $(n,0)$ everywhere. There is an integer $d_0$ such that for all $d\ge d_0$ the following holds. Choose a target datum $W$ of dimension $d$ with Gram matrix $\delta I_d$, $\delta\in E$, skew-Hermitian and of signature $(d,0)$ after multiplying its complex Gram matrix by $i$. The two data include full lattices and positive Cartan involutions at infinity. The lattice of $X$ is self-dual for the integrality test $h_X(y,L_X)\subset\mathcal O_E$. For $W$ the test is $\operatorname{Tr}_{E/\mathbb Q}(o h_W(y,x))\in\mathbb Z$ for every $x\in L_W$ and $o\in\mathcal O_E$. At each $v\in P$ each datum has a basis for the lattice after projecting onto the chosen split factor. The basis takes $\mathcal O_{F_v}^{\,n}$ or $\mathcal O_{F_v}^{\,d}$ bijectively to that projected lattice. [The two lattice conditions](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/BlockTheory/DivisorBase/WeilTensorModels.lean#L182) · [Split basis condition](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/Foundations/GLintEqKOAndPrincEqKOne.lean#L401) · [The theorem](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ThetaParseval/HalfIndexLattices/TLiftK.lean#L12)

Take unitary Hecke characters $\chi_X,\chi_W$ of $E$ whose restrictions to $\mathbb A_F^\times$ are $\eta_{E/F}^{\,n}$ and $\eta_{E/F}^{\,d}$ respectively, where $\eta_{E/F}$ is the quadratic character of the extension. Thus their values on the $F$-ideles are $1$ on $F^\times N_{E/F}(\mathbb A_E^\times)$, and $(-1)^n,(-1)^d$ respectively on the other coset. Write $m_X(v),m_W(v)$ for their angular exponents at $\tau_v$, in the convention $z\mapsto(z/|z|)^m$. Let $U$ be an irreducible automorphic representation of the standard definite unitary group of rank $n$. Here an automorphic representation is a nonzero irreducible right-translation subspace of the continuous left rationally invariant functions with open finite-adelic stabilizer and finite-dimensional span of their translates at infinity. Suppose $U$ contains the compact type of dominant integral weight $\lambda_v$ at $\tau_v$, for every $v$, and that
$$
d+m_W(v)\le 2\lambda_v(i)\quad(1\le i\le n).
$$
Then there is an irreducible automorphic representation $U'$ of the standard definite unitary group of rank $d$. At each $\tau_v$ it has weight
$$
2\lambda'_v(j)=
\begin{cases}
2\lambda_v(j)-d-m_W(v)+n+m_X(v),&1\le j\le n,\\
n+m_X(v),& n<j\le d .
\end{cases}
$$
For every finite split place $v$, each embedding $t:E\to F_v$ over $F$, and every pair of smooth irreducible components $(\tau,\tau')$ of $U,U'$ at the place of $E$ specified by $t$, there is a nonzero split theta intertwiner. More precisely, it is a map $\beta:\mathcal S(M_{n,d}(F_v))\times V_\tau\to V_{\tau'}$, linear in the Schwartz function and conjugate-linear in the vector, satisfying
$$
\beta(\omega_t(g,h)\Psi,\tau(g)x)=\tau'(h)\beta(\Psi,x)
$$
for all $g\in\mathrm{GL}_n(F_v)$, $h\in\mathrm{GL}_d(F_v)$, $\Psi$ and $x$. In the source normalization, with $\chi_{X,t},\chi_{W,t}$ the local characters at the specified place and $|\cdot|_v$ the modulus of multiplication on additive Haar measure,
$$
(\omega_t(g,h)\Psi)(Z)=
\chi_{W,t}(\det g)\chi_{X,t}(\det h)
|\det g|_v^{-d/2}|\det h|_v^{-n/2}\Psi(g^{-1}Zh^{-\mathsf T}).
$$

## Choosing the rank before the characters

Let $G=U(X)(\mathbb A_F)$. On the unitary group of the doubled space $X\oplus(-X)$ use the embedding $e(g_1,g_2)$ and Iwasawa height $a(pk)=|\det(t_p)|_{\mathbb A_E}$, where $p$ stabilizes the diagonal isotropic space, $t_p$ is its action there, and $k$ belongs to the Siegel stabilizer. The height construction gives positivity and continuity. There is a $t_0\in\mathbb R$ such that, for every $t\ge t_0$, the function $A(g)^t=a(e(g,1))^t$ is integrable against every Haar measure on $G$. The underlying estimate first treats $a(e(1,g))$. Since $e(g,g)$ is in the diagonal parabolic with determinant of absolute value $1$, the parabolic transformation law gives $a(e(1,g))=a(e(g^{-1},1))$. Inversion changes Haar measure by a scalar on this unimodular group, giving the required first-variable estimate. The construction uses only $B$ and $X$ at this stage. [Height and integrability](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ThetaParseval/ThetaKernels/ThkBaseHgtInt.lean#L15) · [Switching the two variables](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ThetaParseval/ThetaKernels/TPGHgtFst.lean#L30)

Choose an integer $N>2t_0$ and put $d_0=\max(N,2n)$. Then every $d\ge d_0$ satisfies both $d\ge2n$ and $d/2>t_0$. Fix the target and characters now. The signature guarantees $\delta\ne0$, and multiplying the Gram matrix by a nonzero scalar leaves its unitary group unchanged. This gives the identifications with the two standard groups, respecting rational points and archimedean and finite factors. Use the Weil-pair existence theorem to obtain a single global model $M$ and Weil action $\omega$ for these characters. ## From one test vector to one target representation

The [archimedean calculation](leading-minors-give-the-two-archimedean-types-in-theta.md) produces a nonzero polynomial Schwartz vector $f_\infty$ and a nonzero $\phi\in U$ from the same vector in a finite-dimensional irreducible source type. The vector $f_\infty$ also belongs, at each target archimedean factor, to the calculated type $\lambda'_v$. With that fixed pair, [the nonvanishing argument](finite-place-bumps-make-the-stable-range-theta-lift-nonzero.md) chooses finite Schwartz factors $\Phi_f$, equal to the model's vacuum factors at almost every place, for which the lift
$$
\Theta(\Phi,\phi)(h)=
\int_{U(X)(F)\backslash G}\theta_\Phi(g,h)\overline{\phi(g)}\,dg,
\qquad
\Phi=f_\infty\otimes\Phi_f,
$$
is nonzero. The kernel $\theta_\Phi(g,h)$ is the sum of $\omega(g,h)\Phi$ over the rational points in the model's Schrödinger coordinates. [Theta kernel](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/BlockTheory/DivisorBase/WeilTensorModels.lean#L286) · [Definition of the lift](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ThetaParseval/SchurStep.lean#L22) · [The nonzero lift in the proof](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ThetaParseval/ThetaKernels/GalTPDefs.lean#L159)

The [automorphy and projection argument](a-single-nonzero-theta-projection-keeps-the-local-types.md) then finds an irreducible target constituent $U'$ with nonzero projection of this vector. Varying one local Schwartz factor at a time and keeping that same projection gives the specified archimedean types and split-place intertwiners. This finishes the claim for every $d\ge d_0$. The [two-lift construction](two-theta-lifts-share-a-twist-and-have-disjoint-characters.md) uses the uniform threshold to take both lifts at one odd $d$, choosing the two character pairs only after $d$ is fixed.
