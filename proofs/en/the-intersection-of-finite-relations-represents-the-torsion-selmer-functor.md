# The intersection of finite relations represents the torsion Selmer functor

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $\Lambda$ be a complete Noetherian local ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$ and finite residue field $k$. Let $F$ be a linear left-exact functor on $\mathfrak m$-power torsion modules, where each element is killed by a power of $\mathfrak m$. Suppose $F(k)$ is finite and, for every torsion module $B$, every element of $F(B)$ comes from $F(S)$ for some finitely generated submodule $S\subseteq B$. Use the [compatible presentations](compatible-finite-selmer-presentations-have-one-free-source.md) $p_n:P=\Lambda^a\to M_n$ for $n\ge1$, with relation modules $N_n=\ker p_n$. Set $N=\bigcap_{n\ge1}N_n$.

**Proposition.** One has $N\subseteq\mathfrak mP$ and $N_n=N+\mathfrak m^nP$ for all $n\ge1$. There is a natural isomorphism $F(B)\simeq\operatorname{Hom}_\Lambda(P/N,B)$ on torsion modules.

**Proof.** The modules $N_n$ descend, contain $\mathfrak m^nP$, and satisfy $N_n=N_{n+1}+\mathfrak m^nP$. Given $x\in N_n$, repeatedly lift its relation to choose $x_j\in N_{n+j}$, starting at $x_0=x$, with $x_j-x_{j+1}\in\mathfrak m^{n+j}P$. In the complete free module these elements have a limit $y$. The tail from $x_j$ has constant image in $P/\mathfrak m^{n+j}P$, so $x_j-y\in\mathfrak m^{n+j}P\subseteq N_{n+j}$. Thus $y$ belongs to all the $N_{n+j}$, and by descent to every $N_i$. The first congruence now writes $x$ as an element of $N$ plus an element of $\mathfrak m^nP$. Both summands are contained in $N_n$. Also $N\subseteq N_1=\mathfrak mP$.

Given a map $v:P/N\to B$, its values on the finitely many free generators are killed by some common power $\mathfrak m^n$. Thus it induces a map $v_n:M_n=P/(N+\mathfrak m^nP)\to B$. Send it to $F(v_n)\xi_n$. Passing to a higher stage composes with the transition and leaves the value unchanged. Passage to a common higher stage proves linearity and naturality. If the value is zero, uniqueness for $(M_n,\xi_n)$ on torsion targets forces $v_n=0$.

For surjectivity, let $z\in F(B)$ come from $z_S\in F(S)$, with $S$ finitely generated. A common power $\mathfrak m^n$ kills the generators, so $S$ is finite because $\Lambda/\mathfrak m^n$ is finite. Representability at level $n$ gives $M_n\to S$ evaluating to $z_S$. Composition with the projection from $P/N$ and the inclusion into $B$ gives a preimage of $z$. The [obstruction argument](a-selmer-obstruction-bound-makes-the-presentation-square.md) now bounds the number of generators of $N$ and supplies the square matrix.
