# Split-place rigidity upgrades weak unitary descent to all split places

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $E$ be a CM field, $a\ge1$, $\ell$ prime, and $\pi$ a regular algebraic cuspidal automorphic representation of $\mathrm{GL}_a(\mathbb A_E)$. Assume the polarization character is $\chi_{E/E^+}^{\,a}$, where $\chi_{E/E^+}$ is the quadratic Hecke character of the extension $E/E^+$. The polarization condition requires this character to have value $(-1)^a$ on $-1$ at every real place, and $\pi^c\simeq \pi^\vee\otimes(\chi_{E/E^+}^{\,a}\circ N_{E/E^+})$. The norm pullback is trivial, so $\pi$ is conjugate self-dual. Assume $E$ contains $\sqrt{-d}$ for some positive integer $d$, $[E^+:\mathbb Q]$ is even, and every nonsplit finite place of $E/E^+$ is unramified over $E^+$, lies away from $\ell$, and is a spherical place of $\pi$. These are the hypotheses of `InScope .csd` with the stated polarization character. [Scope](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/GaloisGeometry/IsWeight.lean#L49) · [Polarization](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/GaloisGeometry/DualIsConjByOne.lean#L53)

There exists an irreducible automorphic representation $U$ of the standard totally definite unitary group of rank $a$ over $E^+$. At every nonsplit finite place $U$ has an integral fixed vector. At **every** split finite place $w$, its component identified with $\mathrm{GL}_a(E_w)$ is the component $\pi_w$. At each archimedean embedding $\tau$, if $\lambda_\tau=(\lambda_0,\ldots,\lambda_{a-1})$ is the dominant algebraic weight of $U$, the infinitesimal parameters of $\pi$ agree with $\operatorname{infOfWeight}(\lambda_\tau)$, the multiset of entries $\lambda_i+(a-1)/2-i$. The formal variant `DescOffT` asks, given any finite forbidden set $Z$, for these matches and a finite nonsplit exceptional set $T$ disjoint from $Z$. The proof gives the full `Desc` assertion and takes $T=\varnothing$. ## Obtaining matching spherical data at almost every split place

First use [the trace-formula theorem `RunTh`](a-finite-hecke-separator-forces-a-term-in-the-unitary-trace-formula.md). For a regular algebraic conjugate self-dual cuspidal $\pi$ over a finite-unramified CM extension $E/E^+$, spherical at all nonsplit places, it gives the following alternatives.

- There is an automorphic $U$ as above, unramified at all nonsplit places and with the same Satake multiset as $\pi$ at all but finitely many split places.
- There are finitely many cuspidal $\mathrm{GL}_{a_i}$ classes $P_i$ with $a_i>0$, $\sum_i a_i=a$ and none equal to $\pi$ as a rank-labeled cuspidal class. Their combined infinitesimal multiset is a dominant-weight parameter at each complex embedding. Almost everywhere at nonsplit places, their combined Satake multiset is invariant under inversion. At almost every split place the union of their Satake multisets is the Satake multiset of $\pi$.

Here and below multisets count multiplicity. In the second alternative [the normalization argument `PartNorm`](a-half-integral-norm-twist-vanishes-under-strict-satake-bounds.md) makes each $P_i$ unitary. In its exact form, any finite list of this rank with the weight and split matching conditions of the second alternative has normalized unitary cuspidal representatives for every entry. The conjugate self-dual $\pi$ is unitary as well. Apply [the split-place rigidity theorem](many-anticyclotomic-twists-make-the-nonsplit-rankin-selberg-share-small.md), which says that over a CM extension unramified at every finite place, if a unitary cuspidal $\mathrm{GL}_a$ class has its Satake multiset equal to the union of finitely many positive-rank unitary cuspidal classes at all but finitely many split places, then one of those classes is the original class with the same rank. Thus the second alternative contradicts the exclusion of $\pi$. This proves the weaker existence assertion `WeakAll`. ## Removing the Speh lengths

The [base-change result `BcSpeh`](discrete-base-change-blocks-give-the-local-speh-product.md) gives a description of the split components of $U$ as products of Speh factors. More precisely, for a definite unitary representation of rank $a$ over such an unramified CM extension, it supplies finitely many positive integers $b_i,d_i$ with $\sum_i d_i b_i=a$, and conjugate self-dual cuspidal representations $P_i$ of $\mathrm{GL}_{b_i}(\mathbb A_E)$. At each split place, the unitary component is isomorphic to the normalized parabolic product of the length-$d_i$ Speh representations formed from the $P_{i,w}$. At each archimedean embedding the infinitesimal multiset of the unitary weight is the union of the infinitesimal multisets of the same factors. For a length-one factor this multiset and the local representation are the original ones of $P_i$. These are the clauses of `BcSpeh` used here. [Speh description](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/NonGaloisDescent/IsUnitarisableTrivChar.lean#L37) · [Length one comparison](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/WeilBound/CoreStatements/PadicHodgeComparison/SaturatedBaseChange/SpehSingleFactorEquiv.lean#L1)

At a split place where $U$ and $\pi$ have the common Satake multiset $B$, each $P_{i,w}$ is spherical. Write $S_i$ for its Satake multiset and $q=|\mathcal O_E/w|$. The spherical Speh product formula gives
$$
 B=\bigsqcup_i\bigsqcup_{k=0}^{d_i-1} q^{(d_i-1)/2-k}\, S_i .
$$
Each $S_i$ has $b_i>0$ members. The Jacquet–Shalika bound for the unitary cuspidal $\pi$ is $q^{-1/2}<|\alpha|<q^{1/2}$ for every $\alpha\in B$. Suppose $d_i\ge2$, and take $z\in S_i$. Then $zq^{(d_i-1)/2}$ and $zq^{(d_i-1)/2-1}$ both belong to $B$. The modulus of the second is $q^{-1}$ times the first. The strict lower bound on the second forces the first to be larger than $q^{1/2}$, contradicting the strict upper bound. There is a split place available outside the finite exceptional set, so this argument gives $d_i=1$ for every $i$. [Spherical Speh calculation](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/InertiaEigenvalues/SatakeParameterSphericalProductSpehQuotients.lean#L616) · [Jacquet–Shalika bound in Lean](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/PublishedTheorems/GaloisCohomology₂/StrongMultiplicityOne.lean#L197) · [Length calculation](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/BaseChangeDescent/Strong.lean#L35)

Now the almost-everywhere split matching reads $B=\bigsqcup_i S_i$. The $P_i$ are unitary because they are conjugate self-dual. Apply `SplitRigid` again to get one $P_j$ of rank $a$ isomorphic to $\pi$. Since all $b_i>0$ and their sum is $a$, there can be only one factor. Cuspidal multiplicity one of $\mathrm{GL}_a$ identifies the two cuspidal subspaces representing this class. ## All split places and the weights

The original description `BcSpeh` was valid at every split finite place, including those excluded from the almost-everywhere spherical matching. It now has one factor of length one, so the local representation there is $\pi_w$. At infinity the same single-factor rule gives the infinitesimal parameters of $\pi$ from the archimedean weight of $U$. The nonsplit unramifiedness was already built into `WeakAll`. All hypotheses of `RunTh` and `BcSpeh` apply because the original nonsplit places are unramified and a split extension is also unramified. This proves `Desc` and then `DescOffT`.
