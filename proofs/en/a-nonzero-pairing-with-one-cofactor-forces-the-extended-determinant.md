# A nonzero pairing with one cofactor forces the extended determinant

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $\pi:B\to A$ be a surjection of commutative rings with kernel $(x,y)$, $x^2=y^m=0$, $m>0$, and $xy^{m-1}f=0\Rightarrow\pi(f)=0$. Let $\Phi'$ be a square matrix of size $a'$ over $B$, let $\Phi$ be square of size $a$ over $A$, and fix a row index $b_0$ of $\Phi$. Write $\mathcal R(C)$ for the row span of a matrix and use bars to denote reduction by $\pi$. Suppose $u_j,t_j\in B^{a'}$, $\eta_j\in B$ with $\bar\eta_j=1$, $U_j\in A^a$, $k_j\in A$ for $1\le j\le m$, and $E\in{\rm Mat}_{a\times a'}(A)$ satisfy the following conditions.

- The rows of $E,\bar\Phi'$ and all $\bar t_j$ together generate $A^{a'}$.
- Each row of $\Phi E$, and each difference $\bar u_j-U_j E$, lies in $\mathcal R(\bar\Phi')+\sum_j A\bar t_j$.
- For each $j$, $x u_j-y\eta_j t_j\in\mathcal R(\Phi')$, and $\sum_j k_j\bar t_j\in\mathcal R(\bar\Phi')$.
- The scalar $\sum_j k_j (U_j\,{\rm adj}(\Phi))_{b_0}$ is nonzero.

Then $\det\Phi'\ne0$.

Set $G=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}\widetilde E\\(\eta_jt_j)_j\end{smallmatrix}\right)$, where $\widetilde E$ is any lift to $B$. Its rows generate $B^{a'}/\mathcal R(\Phi')$. To see this, they generate after reduction, and $(x,y)$ is nilpotent, so iterating the statement modulo $(x,y)$ gives generation over $B$. Moreover any prescribed representation of a reduced row by $\bar G$ modulo $\bar\Phi'$ lifts to a representation over $B$. Lift the coefficients and the $\bar\Phi'$ combination arbitrarily. The remaining error lies in $(x,y)B^{a'}$ and is represented by $G,\Phi'$ with coefficients in $(x,y)$, which can be added to the first lift.

For each row $b\ne b_0$ of $\Phi$, choose $C_b\in A^m$ so that $(\Phi E)_b+C_b\bar t$ lies in $\mathcal R(\bar\Phi')$. Let $D$ be a matrix over $B$ with $a$ rows of relations on $G$ modulo $\Phi'$, whose reductions have first block $\Phi$ with row $b_0$ replaced by zero, and second block $C$ with row $b_0$ equal to $(k_j)_j$. Such lifts exist by the preceding paragraph and the relation on $\sum_j k_j\bar t_j$. Likewise choose a row $W_j$ with $\bar W_j=(U_j,\sigma_j)$ for a suitable $\sigma_j\in A^m$, and $u_j-W_jG\in\mathcal R(\Phi')$. The $m$ lower rows $y(0,I_m)_j-xW_j$, as well as the $a$ upper rows $D$, are relations on $G$. The lower-row assertion follows by combining $x u_j-y\eta_jt_j\in\mathcal R(\Phi')$ with the relation for $u_j-W_jG$.

Call the resulting square relation matrix $T$. Its determinant belongs to the zeroth Fitting ideal of $B^{a'}/\mathcal R(\Phi')$, since its rows are relations on a list of generators of that module. This ideal is $(\det\Phi')$. Suppose $\det\Phi'=0$. By multilinearity, $\det T$ is $xy^{m-1}$ times the sum of determinants of matrices with upper block $D$ and lower block $(0,I_m)$ with exactly one row $j$ replaced by $-W_j$. The all-$y$ term and the terms using $x$ twice vanish. Since $\det T=0$, the sum has zero image in $A$. In each summand after reduction, expand along the lower identity rows with labels other than $j$. In the remaining block the row $b_0$ vanishes in the first $a$ columns and has entry $k_j$ in the last column. Expanding there gives $k_j(U_j{\rm adj}(\Phi))_{b_0}$: moving the row $-U_j$ from the bottom to row $b_0$, with the sign from the expansion and the sign in $-U_j$, gives a positive cofactor expansion. Therefore this sum is the scalar assumed nonzero, a contradiction.

The application takes $m=2r$ rows indexed by place and vector coordinate at the new conjugate pair. The rows $u_j,t_j$ record Frobenius and inertia evaluations of the larger universal cocycle; $U_j$ are old Frobenius rows. The coefficients $k_j$ come from a dual Selmer cocycle, with opposite signs at the conjugate places. Global reciprocity supplies its relation on $\bar t$. The new Frobenius element must have nonzero pairing with the old adjugate-column class. That arithmetic choice is the remaining input to this argument.

**Lean correspondence.** `generate` and `lift_exact` lift generation and relations through the nilpotent ideal. `det_relations` compares the determinant with the zeroth Fitting ideal, `det_expand` isolates the mixed term, and `det_ne_zero` combines them. `NewPrime.det_ne_zero` supplies the algebraic data from cocycles and reciprocity.
