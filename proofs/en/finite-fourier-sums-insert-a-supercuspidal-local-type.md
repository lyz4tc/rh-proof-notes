# Finite Fourier sums insert a supercuspidal local type

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Fix a CM field $E$, $a\ge1$, a prime $\ell$, an isomorphism $\iota\colon\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\simeq\mathbb C$, and a split place $w_0\mid\ell$ of $E$ over $E^+$. Let $G$ be the standard totally definite unitary group of rank $a$, whose local factor at this split place is $\mathrm{GL}_a(E_{w_0})$. Let $K$ be a compact open level on the finite factors away from that pair of places, and fix an algebraic coefficient representation of weight $W$. Suppose the algebraic automorphic form $f$ of weight $W$ is fixed by $K$. For every $\delta>0$ there is a finite sum $F$ of forms of weight $W$, fixed by $K$ and lying in automorphic representations with the local type `TyEll` at $w_0$, such that
$$
 f(v)(g)-F(v)(g)=e(g)f(v)(g),\qquad
 |\iota^{-1}e(g)|_\ell\le\delta
 \quad(g\in G(\mathbb A),\ v\in V_W).
$$
Here `TyEll` means that at $w_0$ some nonzero vector transforms by a nontrivial character of a torsion-free compact open subgroup's cyclic quotient of $\ell$-power order, with the property that every irreducible smooth local representation containing the character is supercuspidal. At the level of eigenvalues this density can be combined with the [lacunary weight change](projecting-a-weight-family-gives-new-hecke-eigenvalues.md). ## Producing the character functions

Smoothness gives an identity neighborhood at the split local factor within which all elements fix $f$. Choose $m_0$ with $\ell^{-m_0}<\delta$ and apply the [local type construction](an-irreducible-residue-matrix-forces-a-supercuspidal-type.md) to this neighborhood and $m_0$. It gives $m\ge m_0$, a torsion-free compact open $J\subseteq\mathrm{GL}_a(E_{w_0})$ and a surjective $\theta\colon J\to A=\mathbb Z/\ell^m\mathbb Z$, with all nontrivial characters of $A$ having the stated type property. Let $C=KJ$, where $J$ is supported at $w_0,\bar w_0$. The form $f$ is fixed by $C$. There is a homomorphism $\lambda\colon C\to A$ given by $\lambda(c)=\theta(c_{w_0}(c))$, trivial on $K$, and trivial on every element of finite order in $C$ since its $w_0$-coordinate then has finite order in $J$. [Adjoining the local subgroup](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/SeparatingAlgebras/LevFacts.lean#L9) · [Local type and fixedness](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CongruenceOddLevel/NotApartOfOverPair.lean#L87)

Write $G_\infty$ for the archimedean subgroup and extend $\lambda$ to $C G_\infty$ with trivial archimedean component. Use one representative $g_*$ per double coset under the rational points on the left and $C G_\infty$ on the right. For $g=\gamma g_* c x_\infty$, set $r(g)=\lambda(c)$. This is independent of the factorization. If two factorizations give $cx_\infty$ and $c'x'_\infty$, their quotient belongs to the intersection of $C G_\infty$ with a conjugate of the rational points. That intersection is finite since $C G_\infty$ is compact and the rational subgroup is closed and discrete. Hence the quotient has finite order, and its image under the extended $\lambda$ is trivial. The map $r$ is left rational invariant, right archimedean invariant, and satisfies $r(gc)=r(g)+\lambda(c)$. It is locally constant on finite factors since the kernel of $\lambda$ is open. [Abstract double-coset construction](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/SeparatingAlgebras/EllGrp.lean#L8) · [Application to the definite unitary group](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/SeparatingAlgebras/EllPf.lean#L8)

For each character $\chi$ of $A$ set $F_\chi(v)(g)=\chi(r(g))f(v)(g)$. The character factor is smooth on the finite side, right archimedean invariant and left rational invariant, so multiplication preserves algebraic forms and the weight. For $c\in C$,
$$
 F_\chi(v)(gc)=\chi(\lambda(c))F_\chi(v)(g).
$$
The algebraic automorphic decomposition writes $F_\chi$ as a finite sum of forms in irreducible automorphic representations with the same character law. To see why the law survives, use projections onto the summands of an invariant direct sum. At each nonzero summand choose a coefficient vector $v$ giving a nonzero scalar-valued form. It is a $K$-fixed vector transforming by $\chi\circ\theta$ at $J$. Thus when $\chi\ne 1$ this summand has local type `TyEll`. [Irreducible decomposition preserves the character](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/SeparatingAlgebras/TwistPart.lean#L9)

## Removing the trivial character with small error

The finite sum $F=-\sum_{\chi\ne1} F_\chi$ has the desired local types. We have $f-F=e f$, where
$$
 e(g)=\sum_{\chi\in\widehat A}\chi(r(g))
     =\begin{cases}\ell^m,&r(g)=0,\\0,&r(g)\ne0.\end{cases}
$$
Indeed, list the characters as powers of one primitive $\ell^m$-th root of unity evaluated on $r(g)$. Their sum is the geometric series shown. Since $|\ell^m|_\ell=\ell^{-m}\le\ell^{-m_0}$, the asserted error bound follows. ## The effect on Hecke systems

Start with the same two split pairs $w_0$ and $v'$, levels $K$ and $K'$, embedding $\tau_0$ above $v'$, automorphic representation of weight $\lambda$, and family $T_j$ as in the [weight-changing statement](projecting-a-weight-family-gives-new-hecke-eigenvalues.md). Here `StepSt` assumes both `IntW` and `IntL`, the global and local versions of the $\ell$-integrality bound. Weight change alone gives finite polynomial approximations by representations of weights $\mu$ that differ from $\lambda$ only at $\tau_0$ and its conjugate, and have lacunary jumps there. The local bound `IntL` charges only coefficients at archimedean embeddings whose induced $\ell$-adic local coordinate is nonidentity on the operator support. Since $T_j$ has trivial coordinates at the $v'$-pair, no changed weight contributes to the bound. Therefore the same operators admit an integral evaluation frame at every new weight $\mu$. [Local integrality survives the weight change](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CongruenceOddLevel/NotApartOfOverPair.lean#L61)

Now fix one such weight $\mu$ and one of the approximating representations at that weight. Take a nonzero form in it and normalize one frame evaluation to $1$. There is a common bound $R>0$ on its frame evaluations. The construction above produces an error at most $(\varepsilon/R)|f|$ pointwise, hence at most $\varepsilon$ on the frame. Irreducible decomposition gives a finite sum of $K$-fixed forms of weight $\mu$ in representations with `TyEll`. Every summand has the prescribed scalar $T_j$-action, since $K'\subseteq K$. The [common contraction estimate](a-small-vector-error-gives-one-approximation-for-all-hecke-polynomials.md) turns this into one approximation for all integer polynomials in the eigenvalues. The coefficients are the evaluations at the same coordinate. Composing with the weight-changing approximation yields finite polynomial approximations by systems with both the lacunary weight and the local type. This is `StepSt`. [Density to polynomial approximation](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/SeparatingAlgebras/ToPolyX.lean#L11) · [Combined weight and type](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CongruenceOddLevel/NotApartOfOverPair.lean#L76)
