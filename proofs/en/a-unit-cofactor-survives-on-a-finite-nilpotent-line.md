# A unit cofactor survives on a finite nilpotent line

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $O$ be a DVR with finite residue field $k$, let $\lambda\in O\setminus\{0\}$ have valuation $e$, and let $w\ge0$. Fix a finite variable set $J$ and excluded coordinates $K\subseteq J$. Suppose $F\in O[X_j:j\in J]$ has a unit coefficient in total degree $\le w$ on a monomial involving no coordinate in $K$. Set $q=(e+1)w$ and $H=2q+2$. There exists a finite local $O$-algebra $A$ and $t,a_j\in A$ with
$$
(\lambda)\mathfrak m_O A=0,\qquad t^H=0,\qquad \lambda t^{H-1}\ne0,\qquad
F(a_jt:j\in J)\mid t^q.
$$
Here $a_j=0$ for $j\in K$ and $a_j$ is a unit for $j\notin K$.

Indeed, reduce the polynomial after setting the excluded variables to zero. Its homogeneous component at the indicated degree is nonzero. Over the algebraic closure of the finite field, choose a point where that component and all remaining coordinates are nonzero. For example, the product of the component and the coordinate functions is a nonzero polynomial over an infinite field, so it cannot vanish at every point. The coordinates are contained in a single finite extension $k'/k$. Choose a primitive element of $k'/k$ and lift its monic minimal polynomial to a monic polynomial over $O/(\lambda\mathfrak m_O)$. Adjoining a root gives a finite free extension $S$ of $O/(\lambda\mathfrak m_O)$, with residue quotient $k'$ and kernel of reduction generated by the uniformizer $\varpi$ of $O$. Indeed quotienting the lifted polynomial by $\varpi$ gives that irreducible polynomial over $k$. Since $\varpi^{e+1}=0$ in $S$, this kernel is the unique maximal ideal. Lift the chosen coordinates to $a_j\in S$, with zero on $K$; the retained ones are units.

Take $A=S[t]/(t^H)$, again finite local since $t$ is nilpotent and $S$ is finite local. The basis $1,t,\ldots,t^{H-1}$ and injectivity of constants $O/(\lambda\mathfrak m_O)\hookrightarrow S$ show $\lambda t^{H-1}\ne0$, as $\lambda$ has valuation $e$. Specialize $F$ to a one-variable polynomial $P(t)$ over $S$. Let $d$ be the least degree at which a coefficient is nonzero in $k'$. Then $d\le w$ by the choice of the point, and $P=\varpi Q+t^d V$, where $V(0)$ is a unit. In $A/(P)$, $t^d V=-\varpi Q$. Raising to the power $e+1$ proves $t^{(e+1)d}=0$ there, since $V$ is invertible in $A$. It follows that $P\mid t^{(e+1)w}$ in $A$.

In the new-prime argument $\lambda=\ell^\nu$. If every coefficient below degree $H$ of the old determinant lift lies in $(\lambda)\mathfrak m_O$, that lift evaluates to zero in $A$. The cofactor still divides $t^q$. In particular, for $c$ equal to that cofactor, $\lambda t^{q+1}c\ne0$, since multiplying by a witness for $c\mid t^q$ gives the nonzero $\lambda t^{2q+1}$.
