# Artin reciprocity and a finite field enlargement carry the twist seed

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $B$ be a base with a pinned preliminary datum and fields $K_s\subseteq L_0\subseteq L$, as in the [exact-field construction](an-exact-arithmetic-field-survives-two-coefficient-enlargements.md). Fix its frame $f$, a finite set $\mathcal P$ of rational primes, and a set $W$ of coefficient places above $\mathcal P$ at which new twists are to be inserted. Suppose $W$ is invariant under conjugation and is a union of fibres over primes of $L_0$. Start with a [seed of integral twist packets](the-first-pass-selects-disjoint-twist-supports-by-fixed-field-place.md) on these places. It assigns $i=s(v)$ to $v\in W$, constant on the two kinds of fibres, with $\ell_i$ the residue characteristic of $v$. The packet has support $Q_i$, a root level $k_i$, and a ray-quotient character $\psi_i$ into the $\ell_i^{k_i}$-th roots of unity of its integral ring.

Assume global Artin reciprocity with the source's convention at unramified Frobenius. For each packet use the exported good-twist conditions that its Artin character has open kernel, conjugation sends its values to their inverses, and its support is $Q_i$ in the local ramification tests. The support primes have prime absolute norms and avoid both the index characteristic and the prescribed sets of forbidden host places. In particular the exclusions $A_i$ include every prime of the host above the original off-level rational primes and every prime above the requested forbidden norms at $\ell_i$. For any further finite list of algebraic coefficients depending on the new finite character set, there is a base enlargement with the same preliminary datum, frame, $K_s,L_0,\mathcal P$, and with the following properties.

- the Hecke twists at new coefficient places have the same supports and root levels as the corresponding seed packets, with integral-to-complex Frobenius readings as in (1) of the [primitive-power comparison](frobenius-comparison-recovers-a-single-primitive-power-of-a-twist.md);
- the enlarged ambient field contains the requested coefficients and all the needed roots of unity;
- the pulled-back twists are coherent on each fibre over $L_0$ and obey the required norm avoidance.

## Construct the twist datum before choosing the field

For each used index choose an isomorphism from its cyclic group of integral roots to the complex roots of the same order. Apply this isomorphism to $\psi_i$ composed with the Galois-to-ray quotient map. The character factors through the abelianization and is continuous because its kernel is open. Composing with global Artin reciprocity gives a Hecke character $\chi_i$. Its Frobenius value at a prime away from $Q_i$ is the inverse of the complex image of the integral Artin value, by the unramified convention. The ray packet conditions then give the order dividing $\ell_i^{k_i}$, the conjugation condition and the prescribed ramification away from the excluded primes. In particular a forbidden rational prime $p$ cannot occur as a norm of a support prime: such a support prime would lie above $p$ and hence in $A_i$.

At a marked place $v$ take $(Q_{s(v)},k_{s(v)},\chi_{s(v)})$. Elsewhere take the empty support, exponent $0$ and trivial character. The marked set, its indices and the assignment are conjugation invariant. The finite rational-prime set remains exactly $\mathcal P$. This choice now fixes the finite set of frame-and-twist characters at which the requested coefficient list is to be evaluated.

## Enlarge and pull back

To that list add all $\ell_v^{k_v}$-th roots of unity for places above $\mathcal P$. There are finitely many such places in the original coefficient field and finitely many roots at each. At all other places $k_v=0$, so only $1$ is required. The [second field enlargement](an-exact-arithmetic-field-survives-two-coefficient-enlargements.md) adjoins this finite collection in a finite Galois field while preserving the two smaller embedded fields. Pull back the twist datum from each old coefficient prime to all the new primes above it. Contraction of primes preserves residue characteristic and commutes with conjugation. It is onto by lying-over for integral extensions, so the set of frame-and-twist characters on which the requested list was evaluated is unchanged. All support, exponent, and Frobenius identities therefore remain valid.

If two new coefficient primes lie over the same prime of $L_0$, their contractions in the old ambient field do as well. Saturation makes them both marked or both unmarked. In the marked case they have the same seed index, hence the same support, exponent and Hecke character. In the unmarked case all three entries are trivial by construction. This is the first coherence clause. The other clause requires a primitive root of the recorded order in the enlarged ambient field at each place, which was included in the finite enlargement. Thus the rebuilt datum satisfies the coherence required for the second pass.
