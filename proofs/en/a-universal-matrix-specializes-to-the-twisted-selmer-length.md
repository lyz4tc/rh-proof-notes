# A universal matrix specializes to the twisted Selmer length

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Fix a residual prime $\ell$, an auxiliary exponent $M\ge1$, a finite set $J$ of auxiliary coordinates, and one member of the finite family of lattices in the Selmer problem. The group of auxiliary coordinates is $G=(\mathbb Z/\ell^M\mathbb Z)^J$. Write $\Lambda=O_0[G]$, where $O_0$ is the original coefficient valuation ring, and let $g_j$ be the coordinate generator. The universal Galois twist acts through the inverse of the group element specified by the auxiliary coordinate homomorphisms. The local data include a finite support $Q$ and the prescribed finite conditions at places over $\ell$.

The [universal presentation](a-selmer-obstruction-bound-makes-the-presentation-square.md) gives a square matrix $\Phi$ over $\Lambda$. For each torsion $\Lambda$-module $B$, the kernel of $\Phi$ on $B^a$ is identified with the universal twisted Selmer group with coefficients in $B$. This identification is natural in maps of torsion coefficient modules. In particular its formula commutes with an endomorphism applied to all entries of a kernel vector and to every coefficient of a representing cocycle. The local conditions over $\ell$ in this presentation are generated by the original finite-condition cocycles with coefficient vectors ranging over $B$.

**Proposition.** Write $F\in O_0[X_j:j\in J]$ for any polynomial lifting $\det\Phi$ under $X_j\mapsto[g_j]-1$. For every coefficient extension with valuation ring $O'$ and fraction field $K'$, choose roots $u_j\in O'^\times$ of order dividing $\ell^M$. Let $\psi$ be the character of the Galois group obtained by evaluating the $j$-th coordinate at $u_j$, and assume that $\psi$ is trivial on the decomposition groups over $\ell$. With the original integral lattice and all the local conditions fixed as above,

$$
\frac{\operatorname{length}_{O'}\operatorname{Sel}_{Q}(\psi)}
     {v_{O'}(\ell)}
=
\frac{v_{O'}\bigl(F((u_j-1)_j)\bigr)}{v_{O'}(\ell)} .
$$

Here $\operatorname{Sel}_{Q}(\psi)$ uses the $\psi^{-1}$ twist of the Galois action and torsion coefficients $K'/O'$. We set the valuation of zero equal to infinity. The image of each coefficient of $F$ in $O'$ is understood.

**Proof.** First, a lift always exists. Each basis element of $\Lambda$ has the form $\prod_j [g_j]^{a_j}$, with $0\le a_j<\ell^M$, so it is the image of $\prod_j(1+X_j)^{a_j}$. Such elements span the group ring as an $O_0$-module.

Define $\chi:\Lambda\to O'$ by extension of the coefficient map and $[g_j]\mapsto u_j$. The root relations make this a ring homomorphism. On universal inverse-twist scalars it gives $\psi^{-1}$, and $K'/O'$ becomes a torsion $\Lambda$-module through $\chi$. The universal Galois action on this module is exactly the action for $\operatorname{Sel}_{Q}(\psi)$. At the places over $\ell$, triviality of the auxiliary character on the decomposition groups identifies the transported finite conditions with the original ones after extension of coefficients. The spans agree over $O'$: multiplying a generator of the local condition by $t\in O'$ is achieved by multiplying its chosen torsion coefficient vector by $t$, which is still allowed in the original description of the generators.

The presentation now gives a bijection from the kernel of $\chi(\Phi)$ on $(K'/O')^a$ to $\operatorname{Sel}_{Q}(\psi)$. For $t\in O'$, multiplication by $t$ on $K'/O'$ commutes with the $\Lambda$ action. Naturality therefore makes this bijection $O'$-linear. Thus it preserves composition lengths over $O'$.

To calculate the length, make invertible row and column operations over $O'$ to put $\chi(\Phi)$ in Smith normal form. These operations induce automorphisms on the torsion module. Each nonzero diagonal entry is a unit times $\varpi^m$ for the uniformizer $\varpi$, and its kernel is $\varpi^{-m}O'/O'$, of length $m$. If a diagonal entry is zero, its kernel is all of $K'/O'$, which has infinite length since it contains these submodules for arbitrary $m$. The length of the full kernel is therefore $v_{O'}(\det\chi(\Phi))$. Formation of the determinant commutes with $\chi$, and the lifting map followed by $\chi$ is precisely substitution $X_j=u_j-1$. Dividing by $v_{O'}(\ell)$ gives the normalized statement.

The same polynomial works for all choices of the roots at this fixed auxiliary level. The separate residual Selmer bounds give the small coefficient of $F$ that enters the [common twist argument](a-single-ray-character-satisfies-all-the-exported-twist-bounds.md).
