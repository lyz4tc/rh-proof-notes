# Finitely many Frobenius tests preserve the Selmer pairing

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $G$ be a profinite group, $\sigma$ a continuous involutive automorphism, and $L$ an open normal subgroup stable under $\sigma$. Let $T,T^D$ be discrete continuous representations on $K^r$, both trivial on $L$, whose dot product transforms by the same nonzero scalar $\epsilon(g)$ at $g$ and at $\sigma g$. For finite subspaces $S\subseteq H^1(G,T)$, $S'\subseteq H^1(G,T^D)$ of dimensions $n,n'$, choose cocycles $x_i,y_j$ for bases. On $h\in L$ define $M(h)_{ij}=\langle x_i(h),y_j(h)\rangle-\langle x_i(\sigma h),y_j(\sigma h)\rangle$. These are well-defined evaluations on classes because both actions are trivial on $L$.

**Proposition.** Let $B$ be any bilinear form on $S\times S'$ whose matrix is in the span of the $M(h)$. There is a list of at most $nn'$ elements $h_a\in L$ and an open normal subgroup $U\subseteq L$ with the following property. Given elements $\phi_b\in G$ such that each $h_a$ is conjugate to some $\phi_{b(a)}$ modulo $U$, the evaluations at those $\phi_b$ realize $B$ by a linear combination on the same spaces.

**Proof.** Choose a basis of the span from the family of matrices $M(h)$. At most $nn'$ matrices are needed, so write $B=\sum_a d_a M(h_a)$ in coordinates. Each restricted cocycle is an additive map on $L$. Its kernel in $L$ is open by continuity and normal in $G$, since $x(g h g^{-1})=T(g)x(h)$ on $L$. Take $U$ to be the intersection in $L$ of the kernels of the finitely many cocycles and of their pullbacks by $\sigma$. It is open, normal and $\sigma$-stable.

Choose $g_a$ with $g_a\phi_{b(a)}g_a^{-1}h_a^{-1}\in U$. Then $\phi_{b(a)}\in L$ since $U\subseteq L$ and $L$ is normal. On the chosen quotient equality, every basis cocycle agrees on $h_a$ and $g_a\phi_{b(a)}g_a^{-1}$, and also on their images under $\sigma$. The bilinear evaluation at the conjugate is $\epsilon(g_a)$ times the evaluation at $\phi_{b(a)}$. The same factor occurs for the $\sigma$ evaluation because $\epsilon(\sigma g_a)=\epsilon(g_a)$. Hence $M(h_a)=\epsilon(g_a)M(\phi_{b(a)})$. Summing the coefficients $d_a\epsilon(g_a)$ that land at each $b$ gives the asserted combination. By bilinearity it realizes $B$ on every pair of classes in $S\times S'$, not just the basis.

In the inequivalent case the [full cocycle span](polarized-cocycle-evaluations-span-when-the-representations-differ.md) realizes every form. When $n=n'$, choose the pairing with identity matrix to get left corank zero. In the equivalent case the intertwiner carries $S'$ into the cohomology space of $T$. Work on the sum of this transported space with $S$. Its evaluation matrices span the [alternating forms](a-conjugate-dual-intertwiner-makes-the-cocycle-forms-alternating.md). Choose an [alternating form of left corank at most one](two-equal-dimensional-subspaces-admit-an-alternating-pairing-of-corank-one.md) on the two equal-dimensional subspaces. Applying the same transport argument on the sum furnishes the desired pairing on $S\times S'$.

**Lean correspondence.** `exists_comb` extracts the finite spanning list, and `kerOn_normal` and `val_of_mem` give conjugacy stability and congruence of the cocycle values. `core` combines the evaluations. `abs_odd` and `abs_two` use the nonsingular and corank-one target forms, respectively.
