# A unit linear term preserves low-degree determinants under growth

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Fix a twist, a good signature, coefficient DVR $O$, residue characteristic $\ell$, rank $r$, and positive cyclotomic exponent $\nu$. Write $I_0=(\ell^\nu)\mathfrak m_O$. Take a localized family of $R$ first-block places and $n<R$ second-block places, and extend it by one second-block place while keeping the prescribed Frobenius coordinates. These conditions mean that the second-block decomposition groups lie in the common small subgroup, on which the untwisted matrix is $1\bmod I_0$, and that the first-block character values at the $j$-th second-block Frobenius give the $j$-th standard basis vector. Assume the auxiliary family and its characters have the admissibility and ramification conditions of `PiLocalization₂`. Let $M\ge\nu+2$, and let $c+2r\le\ell^{M-\nu-1}$.

The determinant property at cutoff $c$ says that every polynomial lift of the determinant of every square Selmer presentation, for every allowed relaxation set, has a coefficient outside $I_0$ of degree $<c$. The cofactor property says that the presentation is empty or one adjugate entry has a unit coefficient of degree $<c$ in every polynomial lift, on a monomial avoiding the unused first-block coordinates $j\ge n$. Each property persists at cutoff $c+2r$ after the extension, now with unused first-block coordinates $j\ge n+1$.

Here is the multiplication step. Let $I$ be an ideal of $O$, let $K$ be excluded variables, and let $P$ have no constant term and have unit linear coefficient in a variable $X_q$ outside $K$. If $F$ has a coefficient outside $I$ of degree $d<c$ avoiding $K$, then $P^hF$ has such a coefficient of degree $<c+h$. Indeed, reduce modulo $I$ and set all excluded variables to zero. In this polynomial ring the lowest nonzero homogeneous component of $F$ has degree $d_0\le d$. The linear form of $P$, viewed in $X_q$ with coefficients in the other variables, has unit leading coefficient. Therefore multiplication by its $h$-th power is injective even if $O/I$ has zero divisors. The lowest nonzero homogeneous part of $P^hF$ has degree $d_0+h$.

For the determinant, choose a presentation for the old family using a relaxation set inside the new one. Apply the [comparison of square presentations](killed-generators-compare-two-square-selmer-presentations.md). If all new coefficients below $c+2r$ lie in $I_0$, the new determinant lift still has this property after the new variable is set to zero. The old determinant multiplied by $P^{2r}$, for the polynomial $P$ in that comparison, lies modulo $I_0$ in the ideal generated by this restricted polynomial. By the [truncated coefficient test](a-truncated-polynomial-reads-the-low-coefficients-modulo-an-ideal.md), every coefficient below $c+2r$ of that product lies in $I_0$. This contradicts the multiplication step applied to an old determinant coefficient.

For cofactors one can assume the new matrix has positive size. Choose an old presentation of positive size, replacing an empty one by the $1\times1$ identity if necessary. In the comparison take $I=\mathfrak m_O$, and let $K$ be the first-block coordinates $j\ge n+1$. Every old cofactor monomial given by the hypothesis avoids $K$. The distinguished variable $X_n$ is outside $K$. If the new property fails, for each new adjugate entry choose a polynomial lift all of whose $K$-free coefficients of degree $<c+2r$ belong to $\mathfrak m_O$. After restriction these lifts generate a low-coefficient ideal, and the comparison puts $P^{2r}$ times the chosen old cofactor in its image modulo $I_0$. Apply the truncated coefficient test with $I_0\subseteq\mathfrak m_O$ and then the multiplication step. The resulting contradiction proves the cofactor property with its required quantification over lifts.
