# Discrete base-change blocks give the local Speh product

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $E/E^+$ be a CM extension unramified at every finite place, $n\ge1$, and $U$ an irreducible automorphic representation of the standard totally definite unitary group of rank $n$. The theorem `BcSpeh` gives positive integers $a_i,d_i$ with $\sum_i d_i a_i=n$ and normalized conjugate self-dual cuspidal representations $P_i$ of $\mathrm{GL}_{a_i}(\mathbb A_E)$. At each finite place $w$, take their local components $\rho_{i,w}$ and the length-$d_i$ Speh representations $\sigma_{i,w}$ on $\mathrm{GL}_{d_i a_i}(E_w)$. The factors are unitarizable. At every split place, the component of $U$ is the normalized parabolic induction of the product of these $\sigma_{i,w}$.

At an embedding $\tau$, if $m_i(\tau)$ is the infinitesimal multiset of $P_i$, the infinitesimal multiset corresponding to a weight of $U$ is
$$
 \bigsqcup_i\bigsqcup_{k=0}^{d_i-1}
   \bigl(m_i(\tau)+(d_i-1)/2-k\bigr).
$$
The pairs $(P_i,d_i)$ are distinct. At each nonsplit unramified place, in the unramified unitary model and for each Satake parameter of $U$ there, the expected base-change parameter is the union of the spherical Speh strings from the $P_i$. This statement supplies the factors used in the [upgrade from almost all to all split places](split-place-rigidity-upgrades-weak-unitary-descent-to-all-split-places.md). ## From subquotient to the full local product

Apply the [isobaric base-change theorem](single-place-tests-upgrade-a-weak-isobaric-base-change-datum.md) to $U$. It gives discrete representations $V_i$ of positive ranks $N_i$ with $\sum_iN_i=n$, their local components $\sigma_{i,w}$ and infinitesimal multisets $m'_i(\tau)$, and an irreducible subquotient of their normalized parabolic product $I_w$ at each $w$. These subquotients already have the desired split and inert comparisons.

An irreducible discrete automorphic representation is unitary in its normalized realization; its finite local components are unitarizable. Apply Bernstein's irreducibility theorem for normalized parabolic induction on $\mathrm{GL}_n(F)$ over a nonarchimedean local field $F$: the induction of an irreducible unitarizable representation of the Levi product is irreducible. All $\sigma_{i,w}$ are irreducible and unitarizable, so $I_w$ is irreducible. The irreducible subquotient is therefore $I_w$ itself up to isomorphism. Hence the split and nonsplit comparisons apply to the full product. [Bernstein, sections 0.2 and 8.2](https://www.math.tau.ac.il/~bernstei/Publication_list/publication_texts/Bernstein-P-invar-SLN.pdf) · [Passing to the irreducible product](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/BaseChangeIdentities/Narrow.lean#L47)

## Reading the cuspidal data of each discrete block

The ascending condition for each $V_i$ is part of the input datum. It supplies a normalized conjugate self-dual cuspidal representation $P_i$, of rank $a_i$, and a positive length $d_i$, with $V_i$ identified by an intertwining embedding into the corresponding ascending induction. The rank relation is $N_i=d_i a_i$. The discrete-spectrum comparison for an ascending constituent identifies its local factors with the Speh quotients formed from the local components of $P_i$. It also identifies its infinitesimal multiset with the string of length $d_i$ obtained by shifting the parameters of $P_i$ by $(d_i-1)/2-k$. This is the form of the residual-spectrum theorem `AscComp` used by the project. Its local assertion refers to the specified ascending datum, so the same $P_i$ is used at every place. The classical residual classification describes the discrete representations of $\mathrm{GL}_N$ by such Speh strings from a unitary cusp form of rank $a$, $N=ad$. [Mœglin–Waldspurger, theorem in the introduction](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.24033/asens.1595.pdf#page=2) · [Precise Lean input](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/BaseChangeIdentities/Narrow.lean#L38) · [Its construction](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/BaseChangeIdentities/NarrowAsc.lean#L7)

The local components of each $P_i$ are unitarizable from the length-one version of the same comparison, since a normalized cusp form itself lies in the discrete part. This proves the claimed local Speh description and unitarizability of all factors. The infinitesimal multiset of $U$ is the union of the $m'_i(\tau)$ by the weight comparison of the base-change datum, giving the shifted-string formula above.

To see distinctness, suppose $P_i$ and $P_j$ are isomorphic and $d_i=d_j$. Then the two nonempty multisets $m'_i(\tau)$, $m'_j(\tau)$ are equal at every embedding. But their combined contribution is a submultiset of the infinitesimal multiset of a dominant integral weight, whose entries $\lambda_0+(n-1)/2,\lambda_1+(n-1)/2-1,\ldots$ are strictly decreasing. For $i\ne j$ an entry would occur at least twice. Thus $i=j$. At a nonsplit spherical place the Satake formula for a spherical parabolic product of Speh quotients gives the parameter strings, as in [the split comparison](split-place-rigidity-upgrades-weak-unitary-descent-to-all-split-places.md#removing-the-speh-lengths).
