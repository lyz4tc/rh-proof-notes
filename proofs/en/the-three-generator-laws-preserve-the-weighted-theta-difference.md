# The three generator laws preserve the weighted theta difference

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Use the data and average-count assumption of [the theta difference](cuspidal-cancellation-bounds-the-weighted-theta-difference.md), with dimension $2M$ and weight $W(\tau)=\prod_\sigma(\operatorname{Im}\tau_\sigma)^{M/2}$. Let $D_\phi(\tau)=\Theta_\phi(\tau)-E_\phi(\tau)$ be the average theta function minus the comparison series. For any $\gamma\in\mathrm{SL}_2(F)$, let $T$ be any unitary finite operator implementing its Heisenberg action, in the sense of [the operator uniqueness lemma](heisenberg-covariance-determines-the-finite-weil-operators-up-to-a-phase.md). Then
$$
 W(\gamma\tau)|D_{T\phi}(\gamma\tau)|=W(\tau)|D_\phi(\tau)|
\tag{1}
$$
for every test $\phi$ and every $\tau$ in the product of upper half-planes. The statement also applies separately to $\Theta$ and $E$. [Construct a weighted implementation](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/CompanionTheory/AutomorphicTheory/ThetaDilations/ProdRho.lean#L57) · [Invariance for every implementation](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/CompanionTheory/AutomorphicTheory/ThetaDilations/ProdRho.lean#L121)

By [Poisson summation](poisson-summation-matches-the-finite-fourier-and-real-theta-factors.md) the theta summand has the three generator laws for each adelic isometry. The count bound makes the Gaussian series integrable in the average for each parameter and each test, as in the theta difference proof. Integrating the laws therefore preserves them. The [Eisenstein reciprocal calculation](a-nonzero-theta-value-matches-the-eisenstein-inversion-phase.md) gives exactly the same laws for $E$. Both are linear in the finite test, so their difference has those laws as well.

Under $n(b)$ every height is unchanged, and its operator $U_b$ gives the required equality. Under $m(a)$, the parameter is $a^2\tau$, and the weight becomes $|\mathrm N a|^M W(\tau)$. Its unitary operator is $|\mathrm N a|^{-M}D_a^0$. The dilation law for $D_a^0$ and linearity cancel the two factors. Under $w$, the parameter becomes $S\tau$, with height $y_\sigma/|\tau_\sigma|^2$. Thus the weight is $|c(\tau)|W(\tau)$, which cancels the multiplier in the inversion law for $\mathcal F$. [Rational adelic modulus](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/CompanionTheory/AutomorphicTheory/ThetaDilations/ProdRho.lean#L45) · [Weight factors for the generators](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/CompanionTheory/AutomorphicTheory/ThetaDilations/ProdRho.lean#L57)

Every matrix is a product of these generators. If its entries are $\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}$ with $c=0$, use $n(ab)m(a)$. If $c\ne0$, use $n(a/c)m(-c^{-1})w n(d/c)$, as follows by multiplying and using $ad-bc=1$. Composition gives (1) for the product operator in each case. Every other implementation differs by a scalar of modulus one, which leaves the left side unchanged. ## Keep one uniform constant after reduction

The [cusp reduction](ideal-classes-and-units-reduce-the-hilbert-upper-half-plane.md) puts any $\tau$ into the reduced region using a matrix $rk$, with $r$ from a fixed finite set of rational matrices and $k\in\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathcal O_F)$. Use the corresponding product $T_r T_k$ in (1). For fixed $\phi$, [finite integral covariance](finite-integral-operators-give-the-denominator-decay-of-the-quadratic-integral.md) places every $T_k\phi$ in a common finite-dimensional space of coset indicators, with uniformly bounded coefficients. Each $T_r$ maps this finite list to another finite list of Schwartz–Bruhat tests. The already proved reduced-region estimate, applied to all these tests and summed with the uniform coefficient bounds, gives a single bound independent of $\tau$. This is the weighted estimate used to extract the Gaussian counting error.
