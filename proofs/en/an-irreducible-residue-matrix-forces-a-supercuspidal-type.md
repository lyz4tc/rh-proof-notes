# An irreducible residue matrix forces a supercuspidal type

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $F$ be a finite extension of $\mathbb Q_\ell$, with $\ell$ prime, and let $a\ge1$. Fix a neighborhood $N$ of the identity in $\mathrm{GL}_a(F)$ and an integer $m_0\ge0$. There are $m\ge m_0$, a torsion-free compact open subgroup $J\subset N$ and a continuous surjective homomorphism $\theta\colon J\to\mathbb Z/\ell^m\mathbb Z$ with the following property. For every nontrivial complex character $\chi$ of the target and every smooth irreducible complex representation $\sigma$ of $\mathrm{GL}_a(F)$, if there is $v\ne0$ with $\sigma(j)v=\chi(\theta(j))v$ for every $j\in J$, then $\sigma$ is supercuspidal. The notion used here means that all its Jacquet modules for unipotent radicals of proper parabolics are zero. In the formal statement $F=E_{w_0}$ for a number field $E$ and $w_0\mid\ell$, and the later global application takes $E$ CM and $w_0$ split over $E^+$. ## The cyclic quotient of a small level

Write $\mathcal O$ for the valuation ring and $k$ for its finite residue field. For sufficiently large $m\ge\max(m_0,2)$, the subgroup
$$
 J=1+\ell^m M_a(\mathcal O)
$$
lies in $N$ and is compact open. The powers of $\ell$ tend to zero, and these ideals define the same topology as the powers of a uniformizer. This also gives arbitrarily small such levels. Inversion preserves $J$ since an integral matrix congruent to the identity modulo the maximal ideal has unit determinant. To see torsion-freeness it suffices to exclude elements of prime order $p$. For a nonzero matrix $Y$ of minimum entry valuation $s\ge 2v_F(\ell)$, the term $pY$ of $(1+Y)^p-1$ has minimum entry valuation $v_F(p)+s$. If $p\ne\ell$ then the remaining terms have entry valuations at least $2s$. If $p=\ell$, the intermediate terms have valuations at least $v_F(\ell)+2s$ and the last term has valuations at least $\ell s>v_F(\ell)+s$. In either case the minimum cannot cancel. [Principal congruence subgroup properties](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/DivisorProofs/ExistsTorsionFreeSurjZmod.lean#L185) · [Torsion argument used by Lean](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/SatakeParameters/PrincipalCongruencePrimePowTorsionFreeStatement.lean#L1)

There is an additive map $\phi\colon\mathcal O\to\mathbb Z/\ell^m\mathbb Z$ with $\phi(1)=1$ and $\phi(\ell^m\mathcal O)=0$. Indeed, $\mathcal O$ is free of finite rank over $\mathbb Z_\ell$ and $1$ is primitive modulo $\ell\mathcal O$. Choose a basis containing $1$, project to that coordinate and reduce modulo $\ell^m$. For any integral matrix $B$, the function
$$
 \theta(1+\ell^m X)=\phi(\operatorname{tr}(BX))
$$
is a group homomorphism to the additive cyclic group. Multiplication of $1+\ell^m X$ and $1+\ell^m Y$ adds $X+Y$ and the extra term $\ell^m XY$, which disappears in $\phi$. The kernel contains $1+\ell^{2m}M_a(\mathcal O)$, so the homomorphism is continuous. [Additive coordinate map](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/SatakeParameters/ExistsAddHomZmodPrimePowOneOfNotIsUnitStatement.lean#L1) · [Trace character](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/SatakeParameters/ExistsCongruenceKernelTraceCharacterStatement.lean#L1)

For $a=1$, take $B=1$. The element $1+\ell^m$ maps to $1$. There is no proper parabolic of $\mathrm{GL}_1$ with nontrivial unipotent radical, so the desired local type property is automatic. For $a\ge2$ we choose $B$ differently. Take multiplication by a generator of the degree-$a$ finite field extension of $k$, viewed as an endomorphism of the $k$-space of dimension $a$. Such an endomorphism $B_0$ has no nonzero proper invariant subspace, since the algebra $k[B_0]$ is itself the extension field. Lift its entries to a matrix $B$ over $\mathcal O$. [Rank one](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/DivisorProofs/ExistsTorsionFreeSurjZmod.lean#L80) · [Irreducible residue matrix](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/DivisorProofs/ExistsTorsionFreeSurjZmod.lean#L198)

## A transvection in every conjugate unipotent radical

Let $S\subset F^a$ be any nonzero proper subspace. Its lattice $S\cap\mathcal O^a$ is a direct summand of $\mathcal O^a$ of rank $\dim_F S$: the quotient is torsion-free because $S$ is an $F$-subspace, hence free over the discrete valuation ring. Its reduction $\overline S\subset k^a$ is a nonzero proper subspace. Choose $\bar w\in\overline S$ with $B_0\bar w\notin\overline S$. Choose a linear form $\bar f$ on $k^a$ that vanishes on $\overline S$ but has nonzero value on $B_0\bar w$. Lift $\bar w$ to $w\in S\cap\mathcal O^a$, and lift $\bar f$ to an integral linear form $f$ vanishing on $S$, using a basis adapted to the direct summand. The scalar $u=f(Bw)$ is a unit. Let $X=u^{-1}wf$, a rank-one matrix. Then
$$
 X^2=0,\quad X(F^a)\subset S,\quad X|_S=0,\quad
 \operatorname{tr}(BX)=u^{-1}f(Bw)=1.
$$
In particular $j=1+\ell^m X$ lies in $J$ and $\theta(j)=1$. The construction for even one $S$, for instance a coordinate line, proves surjectivity. [Integral unit pairing](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/SatakeParameters/ExistsIntegralUnitPairingOfIrreducibleReductionAStatement.lean#L1) · [Surjectivity calculation](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/DivisorProofs/ExistsTorsionFreeSurjZmod.lean#L228)

Now fix a proper parabolic with unipotent radical $U$, and let $W$ be any nonzero proper subspace in its defining flag. The radical contains every $1+Z$ with $Z(F^a)\subset W$ and $Z|_W=0$. These maps act trivially on each successive quotient of the flag. Given $g\in\mathrm{GL}_a(F)$, apply the preceding construction to $S=g^{-1}W$. The resulting $j\in J$ has $gjg^{-1}\in U$ and $\theta(j)=1$. [Flag criterion](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/SatakeParameters/UnipotentRadicalFlagStabilizerStatement.lean#L1) · [Construction in each conjugate](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/DivisorProofs/ExistsTorsionFreeSurjZmod.lean#L289)

In the $U$-coinvariant quotient of $\sigma$, the class of $\sigma(g)v$ equals the class of $\sigma(gjg^{-1})\sigma(g)v$, since $gjg^{-1}\in U$. The latter is $\chi(1)$ times the former, where $1$ denotes the generator of the *additive* cyclic group. Since $\chi$ is nontrivial, $\chi(1)\ne1$. Thus the class is zero. The $G$-translates of the nonzero $v$ span $\sigma$ by irreducibility, so the Jacquet module is zero. This works for every proper parabolic and proves the assertion. [Jacquet module criterion](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/SatakeParameters/JacquetModuleCoinvariantsSubsingletonOfCharConjStatement.lean#L1)
