# One new auxiliary prime per signature gives a uniform dyadic bound

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Fix a twist with $\nu>0$, coefficient DVR $O$, residue characteristic $\ell$, rank $r$, and $e=v_O(\ell^\nu)$, using the additive valuation that assigns $1$ to a uniformizer. Let $T$ be any subset of the finite set of good signatures, with the Galois hypotheses for every $s\in T$. Assume the two-block prescribed-Frobenius result and common residual corank-$\le1$ selection described below, and the starting, growth, and new-prime steps. Then there are integers $R,D,M_0$, depending on the twist and $T$, such that at each $M\ge M_0$ and for every admissible character family, tame inertia element, open normal Frobenius congruence subgroup and finite avoidance set, $R$ places in each block can be chosen with the required coordinates and congruences. For every $s\in T$, every compatible relaxed Selmer presentation and every polynomial lift of its determinant, some coefficient of degree $\le D$ has valuation $\le e$.

More explicitly, put $I_0=(\ell^\nu)\mathfrak m_O$. A determinant test at $c$ is the assertion that there is a coefficient outside $I_0$ of degree $<c$ in every such lift. A cofactor test at $c$ asks for an adjugate entry with a unit coefficient of degree $<c$ in every lift, avoiding the unused first-block coordinates, or for an empty presentation. The starting bound at a balanced core of size $R_0$ with $R-R_0$ extra first-block places gives the cofactor test at $4rR_0+1$. The growth step raises both cutoffs by $2r$ when a second-block place is appended. The new-prime step says that a cofactor test at $w+1$ allows the choice of the next second-block place so that the extended family has the determinant test at $2(e+1)w+2+2r$ for the signature at hand. It preserves all previous places and localization, provided an unused first-block coordinate remains and $M\ge\nu+2$ and $2(e+1)w+2+2r\le\ell^{M-\nu-1}$. This last assertion is the arithmetic ingredient discussed separately.

Choose the $R_0$ elements of the common small subgroup for which the mixed residual Selmer forms have common radical of dimension at most one on every signature in $T$; this bound survives conjugating the elements and changing them within a smaller open normal subgroup. Enumerate the signatures $s_0,\ldots,s_{t-1}$. Use the two-block Chebotarev construction with the $R_0$ elements and $t$ copies of the identity, working in the intersection of the smaller subgroup and the prescribed congruence subgroup. Keep all $R=R_0+t$ first-block places, with their Frobenius test modulo the prescribed subgroup, and retain only the first $R_0$ second-block places. The second-block decomposition groups lie in the common small subgroup: Frobenius belongs to it by the congruence prescription and normality, inertia belongs to it by construction, and the decomposition group is topologically generated by inertia and Frobenius. Their character coordinates are the first $R_0$ standard basis vectors. All good signatures therefore have the starting cofactor bound.

Set $b_0=4rR_0$, $b_{j+1}=2(e+1)b_j+2+2r$, and take $M_0=b_t+\nu+3$. These choices ensure that every cutoff needed is within the indicated powers of $\ell$, and $4rR_0+2<\ell^M$. Suppose $j$ places have been appended. Maintain the cofactor test at $b_j+1$ for all signatures and the determinant test at $b_j+1$ for the first $j$. For $s_j$, use its cofactor test to choose a new place, which gives its determinant test at $b_{j+1}$. Apply growth to the extension for all signatures: $b_j+1+2r\le b_{j+1}+1$, so all earlier tests remain true at $b_{j+1}+1$. After $t$ steps the blocks both have length $R$. For each signature its determinant test at $b_t+1$ gives a coefficient outside $I_0$ of degree $\le b_t$. In a DVR that ideal consists precisely of elements of valuation at least $e+1$. Thus $D=b_t$ works, including the case $T=\varnothing$, when no test is needed.
