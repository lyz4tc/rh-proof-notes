# Geometric seeds and Hecke approximations give one odd-rank representation

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $E$ be a CM field, $\ell$ prime, $a\ge1$ odd, $\overline E$ an algebraic closure, and $\iota\colon\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\simeq\mathbb C$. Assume there is a positive integer $d$ such that $E$ contains $\sqrt{-d}$, $-d$ is a square in $\mathbb Q_\ell$, $[E^+:\mathbb Q]$ is even, and every nonsplit finite place of $E/E^+$ lies away from $\ell$ and is unramified over its restriction. These are the field conditions `FieldOK`. A finite place of $E$ is called usable if it is split in $E/E^+$ and $-d$ is a square in $\mathbb Q_q$ for the prime $q$ below that place. Let $T$ be a finite set of nonsplit places and $U$ an irreducible automorphic representation of the standard totally definite unitary group of rank $a$, unramified at nonsplit places outside $T$. Fix $w_0,v'\mid\ell$ above distinct places of $E^+$. By `FieldOK` both are split. [Field and automorphic conditions](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/GaloisGeometry/LatticeDataThicknessBound.lean#L16) · [Usable places](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/GaloisGeometry/ApartAllEmpty.lean#L24)

There is a continuous semisimple representation $r\colon\operatorname{Gal}(\overline E/E)\to\mathrm{GL}_a(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell)$ with the following properties.

- At each usable $w\nmid\ell$ where $U$ is spherical, it is unramified and the characteristic polynomial of the inverse Frobenius, transported to $\mathbb C$, has roots the algebraically normalized Satake multiset of $U_w$.
- At each $w\mid\ell$ outside the two reference pairs where $U$ is spherical, it is crystalline and de Rham. Its jumps at each local embedding are the integers obtained by subtracting the infinitesimal parameters of $U$ at the induced complex embedding from $(a-1)/2$.
- At each place of the second clause, for every local period setup of the crystalline comparison and every local Weil element $\sigma$, there is a family of continuous representations $\rho$ crystalline at $w$ with the same jumps. Given $\varepsilon>0$ and $N\ge0$, one finite coefficient family $c_\rho$ on these representations approximates $\operatorname{tr}r(g)$ by $\sum_\rho c_\rho\operatorname{tr}\rho(g)$ to within $\varepsilon$, uniformly for all $g\in\operatorname{Gal}(\overline E/E)$. With the **same coefficients**, the first $N+1$ power traces of $\sigma$ in the unramified Weil–Deligne parameter of $U_w$ are approximated within $\varepsilon$ by the corresponding linear combination of power traces in the crystalline Weil–Deligne parameters of $\rho$. These local traces and errors are transported by $\iota^{-1}$.

This is the conclusion `LimFinOdd` of the joint geometric seed and congruence theorem. The third clause supplies the local Frobenius comparison through the subsequent trace-closure argument. [Exact conclusion and period setup](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/GaloisGeometry/LimWU4OfLimWU3.lean#L57) · [Odd-rank proof](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TraceLimits/LimFinOddOf.lean#L197)

## Preparing a single trace coordinate set

Use the [odd-rank congruence theorem](one-approximation-preserves-hecke-coordinates-and-adds-generic-satake-data.md) to choose the adapted local levels $K_w$. Let $S$ be the finite set of split places where $U$ is nonspherical. Both members of a conjugate pair are spherical together, since the local identifications of the unitary factor at the two split places are related by conjugate duality. A split place is thus outside the pairs in $S$ exactly when it is spherical for $U$. Every congruence neighbor $U_i$ is spherical at each split place outside the pairs in $S$ and the $w_0$-pair. At the $v'$-pair this is a separate output of the congruence theorem. At all other such places it follows from the hyperspecial $K_w$-fixed vector. [Spherical setup](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TraceLimits/LimFinOddOf.lean#L21)

Take an index set $J$ that includes each triple consisting of a spherical split $w\nmid\ell$ of $U$, a choice of place of $\overline E$ above $w$ and associated Frobenius element $s$, and a positive integer $n$. At index $j$ let $x_j=s^{-n}$. The spherical Hecke operator has scalar equal to the sum of $n$-th powers of the algebraically normalized Satake roots. Its rational coefficients have denominators that are powers of the residue cardinality $q_w$, so it is $\ell$-integral. The definite unitary local component is unique at a split place, and its $K_w$-fixed subspace is nonzero. The scalar therefore defines a common eigenvalue coordinate $\theta_j$ of $U$ and $\eta_{i,j}$ of each $U_i$, transported by $\iota^{-1}$. The rationality clause of the congruence theorem places all $\theta_j$ in one finite extension of $\mathbb Q_\ell$. [Frobenius power-sum operators and rational coefficients](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/SatakeParameters/FrobOpsIdx.lean#L20) · [Construction of the eigenvalue system](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TraceLimits/LimFinOddOf.lean#L37)

## Geometric seeds for each finite test set

The [geometric seed theorem](conjugate-reference-places-give-one-odd-rank-geometric-seed.md) gives a finite set $Z_0(Y)$ of usable places outside $S$ and away from $\ell$ for each finite set $Y$ of requested local comparisons. To its tests add all split spherical places of $U$ in $Y$ away from $\ell$. Also take $W(Y)$ to be the spherical places of $U$ in $Y$ over $\ell$ outside the two reference pairs. The congruence theorem applies simultaneously to these finite sets, imposing generic Satake data there and controlling the scaled power sums at $W(Y)$. In particular its neighbors $U_i$ meet the seed theorem's local type, weight, and sphericity hypotheses. Index the resulting family by $i=(Y,U_i,\eta_i)$ and choose a continuous semisimple seed $\rho_i$ from the seed theorem for each such $i$. The index set includes every finite $Y$, so the approximation can later be restricted to any one $Y$. [Preparing the test sets and seeds](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TraceLimits/LimFinOddOf.lean#L55) · [Exact seed statement](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/GaloisGeometry/LimWU4OfLimWU3.lean#L16)

The seed theorem gives each $\rho_i$ the characteristic polynomial equality at usable spherical places outside a finite exceptional set $X_i$, whenever the Frobenius element belongs to an open dense set $V_i$ of the Galois group. Let $D_i\subseteq J$ be the set where $\eta_{i,j}=\operatorname{tr}\rho_i(x_j)$. For any finite subset of seeds, avoid $S$ and their finitely many sets $X_i$, and require the Frobenius elements to lie in the intersection of their open dense sets $V_i$. The [density of usable Frobenius elements](degree-one-frobenius-elements-are-dense-at-the-usable-split-places.md) shows these Frobenius elements remain dense. Taking $n=1$ in the coordinate set gives a dense set of $x_j$ with $j$ in all the corresponding $D_i$, since inversion is a homeomorphism. Characteristic polynomial comparison gives the required trace comparison there. [Common density verification](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TraceLimits/LimFinOddOf.lean#L94)

The [limit-trace construction](constructing-the-limit-trace-from-dense-frobenius-values.md) now gives a continuous function $t$ on the Galois group with values in the finite field containing the $\theta_j$. For each fixed $Y$, the family restricted to that $Y$ still polynomially approximates $\theta$ and therefore polynomially approximates $t$. The [trace identity and representation construction](limit-traces-retain-the-polynomial-identities-of-representations.md) gives a continuous semisimple $r$ of dimension $a$ with trace $t$. Every such restricted approximation yields uniform linear trace approximation of this **same** $r$ by the seeds with that $Y$. [Constructing $r$ and retaining each subfamily](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TraceLimits/LimFinOddOf.lean#L121)

## Recovering the three local conclusions

Fix a usable spherical $w\nmid\ell$ and restrict the seed family to $Y=\{w\}$. Then $w$ was included in the finite tests, so every seed in this subfamily is unramified at $w$ and has its full characteristic polynomial comparison there, including all inverse Frobenius powers, without the individual finite exceptional set or open-set restriction. The [common inertia-kernel argument](the-limit-trace-preserves-the-common-inertia-kernel.md) makes $r$ unramified at $w$. Every index $j$ over $w$ belongs to all $D_i$ in this subfamily, so the interpolation property gives $t(x_j)=\theta_j$. Equality for the positive powers of every inverse Frobenius gives equality of the characteristic polynomials by Newton's identities. [Frobenius and inertia conclusion](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TraceLimits/LimFinOddOf.lean#L136)

Fix a spherical $w\mid\ell$ outside the two reference pairs and again take $Y=\{w\}$. The seed theorem now makes every seed crystalline and de Rham there, and their jumps agree with those of $U$ because the congruence theorem keeps the weights at every embedding over $w$. Use the closure theorem `closedAtL` for this local condition, applied to the linear trace approximation of $r$ by these seeds. Its input is a continuous semisimple $r$ uniformly approximable in trace by continuous semisimple representations satisfying the specified crystalline and de Rham conditions at $w$, with the same fixed jump multiset at each embedding. It gives the same conditions and jumps for $r$. [Crystalline and de Rham closure in the proof](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TraceLimits/LimFinOddOf.lean#L163) · [Closure theorem](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/SatakeParameters/ClosedAtL.lean#L25)

For this same $w$, the joint congruence approximates the entire trace-coordinate system together with the scaled Satake power sums at $w$. If the algebraically normalized Satake roots are $b_i$, the unramified Weil–Deligne power trace of $\sigma$ at power $n$ equals $\sum_i b_i^{-n\deg\sigma}$. Each seed has this formula with the roots of $U_i$, and the seed theorem identifies that power trace with the one in its crystalline Weil–Deligne parameter, since $w\in Y\cap W(Y)$ is a generic test. To remove the nonzero scales on the first $N+1$ such coordinates, choose a tolerance divided by the largest norm of the inverse scales or $1$, whichever is larger. The [joint approximation lemma](one-set-of-coefficients-approximates-global-and-local-traces.md) preserves the global trace bound and produces the local bound with the same coefficients. This uses finite sets of local powers with arbitrary precision.
