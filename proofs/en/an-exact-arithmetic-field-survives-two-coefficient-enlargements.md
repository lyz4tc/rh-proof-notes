# An exact arithmetic field survives two coefficient enlargements

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

The base used in the [two construction passes](two-passes-give-layered-objects-over-the-same-numerical-context.md) has three fields. In their chosen complex realizations write them as $K_s\subseteq L_0\subseteq L$, where $L_0/\mathbb Q$ and $L/\mathbb Q$ are finite Galois. $K_s$ is the field of arithmetic coefficients in the base. $L_0$ is the specified field for coefficients that must stay fixed during a later enlargement. $L$ is the ambient field and may be enlarged at that later step. A base also specifies the rational preliminary data $P$. The [frame $f$ over $P$](the-host-hecke-frame-comes-from-four-explicit-character-choices.md) includes its CM type and Hecke characters with their prescribed infinite types and tame local data.

Take one of the good scale contexts on the eventual range where its diagonal preliminary datum $P$ and a frame $f$ are available. In the diagonal datum the standard coordinate vectors of the smaller distinguished space map to the correspondingly indexed standard coordinate vectors of the final column space. This is the pin condition. Use existence of the ray class fields, capitulation of all ideals in the Hilbert class field, and the existence of the CM curve with the field and good reduction conditions of the host. Then there is a base with precisely this $P,f$ and with $K_s$ equal to the explicit compositum below. Moreover, for arbitrary finite-set functions $S_1(F)$ and $S_2(F,F_0)$ whose values consist of algebraic complex numbers, one can obtain
$$
 S_1(K_s)\subseteq L_0,\qquad S_2(K_s,L_0)\subseteq L .
 \tag{1}
$$
Here $F,F_0$ range over subfields of $\mathbb C$ containing $\mathbb Q$. This order matters: $S_2$ can depend on the field $L_0$ chosen for the first inclusion. Both the exact field $K_s$ and the pin condition persist under all later base enlargements in the sense of the source, which fix $P,f,K_s,L_0$ in their specified realizations and enlarge $L$.

## The exact field

Write $E$ for the Galois CM field of the host in its fixed embedding. Let $g_0,g_1$ be its two generator primes and $p_d,p_d'$ its two distinguished split primes. Take the ray field $E_{\mathfrak m}$ for $\mathfrak m=(g_0g_1p_d p_d')$. Put $h_*=[E_{\mathfrak m}:E]$ and let $V_{h_*}$ be the [finite Kummer field](a-finite-kummer-extension-contains-the-normalized-hecke-values.md). The field datum also specifies a CM curve over a number field $F_j$, and a class-group generating list for $E$. Define $M'$ as the compositum of $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-d})$, $F_j$, the field generated by the coordinates of the curve's $12$-torsion and the field generated by the $12$-th roots of its discriminant in its fixed Weierstrass model. It is finite since there are finitely many torsion points and their coordinates are algebraic over the field of definition. The discriminant roots are algebraic as well.

The field $K_s$ is the compositum of the following fields.

- $E$, $M'$, and $E_{\mathfrak m}$;
- the cyclotomic fields of respective orders $D_{\rm row}$, $g_0(g_0-1)$, $g_1(g_1-1)$, $3^4$ and $5^4$. Here $D_{\rm row}$ is the order of the finite product of the row groups $\operatorname{GL}_r(\mathbb F_{g_i})$, with the multiplicity of real places used in the preliminary datum;
- the fields of unramified values of the frame characters $\mu_{0,j},\eta,\delta,\delta'$, with normalization exponent $1/2$ for $\eta$ and $0$ for the others, together with the chosen residue character values;
- for every layer $1\le j\le m$, the field $K_r$ in the [type-norm generator construction](class-group-radicals-give-generators-of-prescribed-cm-type-norm.md) for the exponent pair of that layer.

All these fields lie in
$$
 M'\,E_{\mathfrak m}\,\mathbb Q(\zeta_\nu)\,V_{h_*},
 \qquad \nu=\operatorname{lcm}(D_{\rm row},3^4 5^4).
 \tag{2}
$$
For the cyclotomic fields, $g_i(g_i-1)$ divides the order of the corresponding general linear group by the factor $g_i^r-g_i^{r-1}$, since $r\ge2$. The residue values have orders dividing $g_i-1$. For the value fields, $\mu_{0,j}$ has even angular exponents because the underlying weight exponents are integral, $\eta$ has angular exponent $1$, and the finite-order determinant characters have exponent $0$. Their ramification is tame and restricted to the specified primes of $\mathfrak m$. The ray-value argument gives the inclusions with the stated norm factors. For the type fields use that the class number divides the ray class degree. This proves finiteness of $K_s$, including those value fields defined using all primes of unramified evaluation.

The field $K_s$ contains $E$, the prescribed roots of unity, and the needed ideal generators. The roots of unity of orders $p_d,p_d'$ lie in $E_{\mathfrak m}$: the cyclotomic extensions of $E$ of these orders are abelian, with their Frobenius on roots of unity given by the ideal norm, so the principal ray congruence forces trivial Frobenius. The class-number root of unity lies in the type field for the first layer, which exists since $m\ge2$. Every ideal of $E$ becomes principal in $K_s$ because $E_{\mathfrak m}$ contains the Hilbert class field, in which ideals capitulate. The layer type norms admit the stronger generators with all conjugate absolute values from (1) of the type-norm note. These properties are exactly the arithmetic field conditions of the base.

## Making the two inclusions in order

Take the normal closure of the finite extension generated by $K_s$ and the finitely many further roots of unity prescribed for the ambient field. This gives an initial $L$. Complex conjugation restricts to it by normality. Regard $K_s$ as the specified embedded subfield and initially take $L_0=L$. All the rational data, ideal generators and norm equalities can be transported by the chosen embedding, so this constructs the starting base.

Now evaluate $S_1(K_s)$, form the normal closure of the compositum of this finite list with the starting ambient field, and take that larger field both as the ambient field and as $L_0$. This first adjustment keeps $K_s$ exact but is allowed to set the eventual fixed field. Next evaluate $S_2(K_s,L_0)$, take a further normal closure including that finite list, and carry $L_0$ into it as the same embedded subfield. This gives (1). Throughout, the diagonal coordinate inclusions and the frame $f$ are transported with the same preliminary datum. An enlargement $\widetilde L$ of the latter kind changes only the ambient complex image, so the same field-data witness still proves $K_s$ is the prescribed compositum. This is the preservation assertion needed when the twists are changed in the second pass.
