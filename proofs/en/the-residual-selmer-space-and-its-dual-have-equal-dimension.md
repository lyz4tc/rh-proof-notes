# The residual Selmer space and its dual have equal dimension

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $F$ be the CM host of degree $2f$, $k$ the finite residue field of the coefficient ring, $A=k^r$ the residual representation and $A^D$ its cyclotomic $k$-linear dual. At the places over $\ell$ take the reduced Bloch–Kato finite conditions. At the other specified exceptional places, all local $H^0,H^1,H^2$ groups on both sides are assumed zero. Away from these places and $\ell$, assume the representations and the permitted classes are unramified. Suppose that the residual representation matrices span the full matrix algebra and $r\ge2$. Suppose also that the rational de Rham dimensions over the places above $\ell$ obey the conjugate pairing as in the [product of local sizes](conjugate-hodge-filtrations-fix-the-product-of-local-selmer-sizes.md).

**Proposition.** The two unrelaxed residual Selmer spaces are finite-dimensional of equal dimension over $k$. Their dimensions remain equal over any extension of the residue coefficient field equipped with the same local conditions generated by the reduced integral cocycles.

**Proof.** The span hypothesis yields zero global invariants on either side. In the ordinary representation a nonzero invariant would be an eigenvector of every representation matrix, and then an eigenvector of the whole matrix algebra by linearity. For $r\ge2$, no line is stable under all matrix units. For the dual, inversion permutes the group image, transpose preserves matrix span, and nonzero scalar twists do not change the span. Thus the same argument applies.

Use [descent to restricted ramification](unramified-selmer-classes-descend-to-one-finite-set-of-places.md) to work with the finite set consisting of the exceptional and $\ell$-adic places. Over $\ell$ the local conditions are [exact annihilators](residual-bloch-kato-conditions-are-exact-annihilators.md). At the other allowed places the local $H^1$ groups are zero, so exact annihilation also holds there. For the finite-group pairing in Poitou–Tate, compose the $k$-bilinear pairing with the trace $k\to\mathbb F_\ell$ and identify $\mathbb F_\ell(1)$ with $\mu_\ell$. The trace pairing $(a,b)\mapsto\operatorname{Tr}(ab)$ is nondegenerate because finite fields are separable. It gives the requisite finite cyclotomic dual, and annihilation for a $k$-subspace is preserved because one may multiply a test class by every $a\in k$. Equivalently, using a basis and its trace-dual basis recovers a $k$-valued cup from its $\mathbb F_\ell$-valued images. Reversing the order of two degree-one cups changes sign up to a coboundary and so has the same zero condition.

The [Greenberg–Wiles count](the-poitou-tate-sequence-counts-the-selmer-dual-pair.md) now applies. Both global invariant groups have order one. The local factors at the exceptional places away from $\ell$ are all one. At the places over $\ell$ the product of finite-condition sizes equals
$\prod_{v\mid\ell}\#H^0(F_v,A)\,(\# k)^{fr}$. This equals exactly the remaining invariant factors times $(\#A)^{r_2(F)}$, since $\#A=(\#k)^r$ and $r_2(F)=f$. The ratio is one. Thus the finite groups have equal size, hence equal $k$-dimension.

For extension $K/k$, choose a $k$-basis of $K$. Each continuous cocycle into the discrete $K$-module has finite image because the Galois group is compact. Its finitely many coordinate values involve finitely many basis elements of $K$. Thus it is a finite $K$-linear combination of its coefficient projections into $k$. Those projections preserve the cocycle equation because the representation has entries in $k$. They preserve the local conditions: they commute with restriction and take a scalar multiple of a reduced integral cocycle to another such multiple. Conversely coefficient inclusion preserves the same conditions. If a finite $K$-linear relation among extended classes holds, applying the $k$-linear coordinate projections to a witnessing coboundary shows that every $k$-component of the relation is zero. The Selmer space is therefore extended by scalars and its dimension is unchanged. Apply the argument to both sides.

**Lean correspondence.** `card_eq` combines local annihilation, vanishing, signature count and the Greenberg–Wiles identity. `greenbergWiles_dualPair` converts the $k$-pairing to finite additive duality through trace and changes the Galois group by inflation. `selOn_baseChange` uses the coefficient projections on cocycles; `eqDim` concludes equality over all residual coefficient fields.
