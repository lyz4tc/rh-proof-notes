## Statement: the input actually used by rank extraction

Let $E$ be a number field, $\Omega$ an algebraic closure, and $\rho:G_E\to\mathrm{GL}_N(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell)$ a continuous representation. Use the following datum, denoted `HTRichEx` in the caller: at one $w\mid\ell$ fix a place above it, a compatible local bridge identifying its decomposition group with the absolute Galois group of a finite $\ell$-adic field $K$, the tied period data, and an induced embedding $\tau:E\hookrightarrow\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$. Suppose a finite local coefficient model of the restricted representation is de Rham and has filtration-jump multiset $J$ in the component belonging to $\tau$. Suppose that a set $J_0$ of $a$ distinct integers in $J$ is lacunary: no two disjoint nonempty subsets have the same average. The entire multiset $J$ need not be distinct or lacunary.

Then there is an endomorphism $X$ over $\mathbb C_\ell$ in the tangent space at $I$ cut out by the entry-polynomial equations of $\rho(G_E)$ over $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$, and its characteristic polynomial has the $a$ distinct marked roots $J_0$, still lacunary in $\mathbb C_\ell$. This is the tangent input to `DivWCoreLac` with ambient dimension $N$ and target rank $a$. [Local hypothesis](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/ZetaCharacterDivisibility/InsideRefl.lean#L54-L60) · [Tangent condition](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/WeilBound/CoreStatements/PadicHodgeComparison/CocharacterLieDivisors/MeetsOpenCosets.lean#L16) · [The cocharacter-to-vector assembly](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/ZetaCharacterDivisibility/HtCocharOf.lean#L20-L44).

The proof is not that the original representation has a diagonal family of global Galois elements with eigenvalues $t^j$. The diagonal family will lie in the **Zariski closure** after extending coefficients. The difficult implication is that equations vanishing on the Galois image vanish on this family, even though the Hodge–Tate change of basis is only semilinearly equivariant.

## 1. Where the marked weights come from

For the geometric caller, the model is the semisimple Hecke-tower representation twisted by an algebraic character whose local jump is $-m_\tau$. The Hodge comparison of the reference tower supplies the desired jumps $j+m_\tau$, so in the twisted model all required $j$ occur. This is the `JumpsOcc` part of `Pack.rep`. The earlier [geometric-seed note](https://github.com/lyz4tc/rh-proof-notes/blob/main/proofs/en/an-algebraic-twist-turns-a-hecke-tower-into-a-geometric-seed.md) explains the twist and the geometric hypotheses. The remaining choice in `RichOf` matters: it uses **one** complex embedding $\tau_c$ and one weight $\lambda$ at which the automorphic datum supplies a lacunary jump set. Transport $\tau_c$ back by $\iota^{-1}$, where $\iota:\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\simeq\mathbb C$ is fixed. Extend that embedding of $E$ to $\Omega$, take its valuation ring, and choose the resulting local bridge. Its induced local embedding in the chosen completion is the identity. So `JumpsOcc` puts all of the $a$ integers of `wtJumps`$(\lambda)$ inside one multiset $J$ at that same place and embedding; it does not collect jumps from different embeddings. This produces `HTRichEx`. Listing $J_0$ in increasing order gives an injective $e:\{1,\ldots,a\}\to\mathbb Z$; pulling index subsets back to $J_0$ gives the arithmetic mean inequalities in `HTLac`. [Twisted comparison](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/GeometricFlagBases/MapInvMul.lean#L61-L77) · [Embedding and existence of the marked set](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/GeometricFlagBases/RichOf.lean#L50-L77) · [Weight indexing](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/ZetaCharacterDivisibility/PLacBridge.lean#L16-L34).

It is necessary to check separately that the ambient multiset satisfies $|J|=N$. `IsJumpsAt` records a finite coefficient model and the full multiplicity of each graded piece at an induced embedding, not just the fact that $J_0$ occurs. Enlarge its coefficient field together with the field used to specify the embedding. In the enlarged de Rham component the filtered comparison gives dimension $N$, and the total of the graded-piece dimensions is that component dimension. The change-of-model count formula identifies those dimensions with the original multiplicities of $J$. Thus $J$ accounts for all $N$ columns of a Hodge–Tate basis, while the $a$ selected jumps are just an injectively indexed part of that list. [Finite model and jump multiplicities](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/AutomorphicMembership/FiniteDimensionalWdSpace.lean#L189-L205) · [Dimension and comparison of counts](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/ZetaCharacterDivisibility/PJumpCard.lean#L17-L36) · [Count comparison](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/IwasawaDecomposition/FiniteDimensionalFilDR.lean#L246).

## 2. From filtered de Rham coordinates to semilinear eigenvectors

Work first at the chosen local bridge. The embedding of $K$ inducing $\tau$ need not be the inclusion into the finite coefficient field $L$. Extend that embedding to an automorphism of $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ over $\mathbb Q_\ell$, conjugate the local Galois group accordingly, and transfer the representation by precomposing with inverse conjugation. The de Rham dimension and the component multiplicities are unchanged when the embedding is transported as well. This makes the chosen component the one indexed by inclusion. Lean constructs this change of group and uses `multV_conj` for the multiplicity equality. Then work on $H=G_{K_1}$ for a further finite extension $K_1$ containing the conjugated $K$ and the coefficients $L$. This guarantees that elements of $H$ fix the entries of **every** representation matrix in the finite model, not just the entries of their own matrices. A finite local coefficient model at the chosen place is all that is required; no finite global coefficient field is assumed. [Conjugating the induced local embedding](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/InertiaEigenvalues/MemClosureOfJumps.lean#L69-L124) · [Transport of the graded pieces](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/IwasawaDecomposition/LocalIndepBridge.lean#L296).

Here is the filtered-basis calculation behind the local eigenbasis. Write $F^j B_{\mathrm{dR}}$ for the filtration, choose its period parameter $\varpi$ satisfying $g(\varpi)=\chi_\ell(g)\varpi$, and write $\theta:F^0B_{\mathrm{dR}}\twoheadrightarrow\mathbb C_\ell$ for residue reduction, with kernel $F^1$. In the chosen component the filtered de Rham comparison and an adapted basis give columns $u_i$ fixed for the semilinear action $u\mapsto\rho(g)g(u)$, with degrees $k_i$ whose multiset is $J$. The coordinates of $u_i$ lie in $F^{k_i}$, and any column $y$ with entries in $F^j$ can be written uniquely as $\sum_i f_i u_i$ with $f_i\in F^{j-k_i}$. Ordinary linear independence and this filtered spanning property are both used.

Let the $i$th column of $P$ be $\theta(\varpi^{-k_i}u_i)$. These columns are independent over $\mathbb C_\ell$. In fact, lift the coefficients of a relation to $a_i\in F^0$. The column $\sum_i a_i\varpi^{-k_i}u_i$ then has entries in $F^1$. By uniqueness and the filtered spanning property at $j=1$, each coefficient $a_i\varpi^{-k_i}$ belongs to $F^{1-k_i}$, so $a_i\in F^1$ and the original coefficients are zero. Thus $P$ is invertible. Reducing the semilinear transformation of $\varpi^{-k_i} u_i$ gives
$$
 \rho(g)\,g(P)=P D(\chi(g)),\qquad
 \chi=\chi_\ell^{-1},\qquad D(t)=\operatorname{diag}(t^{k_i})
 \quad(g\in H).
$$
The inverse cyclotomic character in this equation accounts for the residue normalization; the algebraic cocharacter below uses the **positive** exponents $k_i$. [Adapted frame and count](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/InertiaEigenvalues/DeRhamExistsHodgeTateEigenbasis.lean#L17-L121) · [Invertibility after reduction](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/InertiaEigenvalues/HodgeTateSymbolMatrixIsUnitDet.lean#L12) · [Sign in semilinear equivariance](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/InertiaEigenvalues/HodgeTateSymbolMatrixEquivariant.lean#L13).

## 3. Why the semilinear matrix relation implies Zariski containment

Use the local Tate vanishing in the following form: for $m\ne0$, an element $c\in\mathbb C_\ell$ satisfying $g(c)=\chi(g)^m c$ for every $g\in H$ must be zero. This is a statement for the absolute Galois group of the *finite* local extension $K_1$ and for the genuine action on the completion. It is the input that will separate integer weights even when coefficient polynomials are not fixed by $H$. [Tate no twisted invariants](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/InertiaEigenvalues/TateNoTwistedInvariantsComplexPadic.lean#L245).

Let $\mathcal I$ be the vector space of $\mathbb C_\ell$-coefficient polynomials in a matrix variable $Z$ that vanish on all $\rho(h)P$ for $h\in H$. Give the variable in column $j$ weight $k_j$, and each monomial $M$ the resulting total weight $w(M)$. For a polynomial $q$ and $g\in H$ write
$$
 (L_gq)(Z)=g(q)(Z D(\chi(g))),
$$
where $g(q)$ means applying $g$ to the coefficients only. The space $\mathcal I$ is stable under $L_g$. Indeed $\rho(h)P D(\chi(g))=g(\rho(hg)P)$ by the matrix relation and the fixed matrix entries. Thus $(L_gq)(\rho(h)P)=g\big(q(\rho(hg)P)\big)=0$. On the coefficient of $M$, $L_g$ acts by $c_M\mapsto \chi(g)^{w(M)}g(c_M)$. [Semilinear stability](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/InertiaEigenvalues/VanishAtHodgeTateCocharacter.lean#L76).

**Weight projections preserve $\mathcal I$.** To see this, induct on the number of monomials of a polynomial in $\mathcal I$. Normalize a nonzero coefficient at a monomial $M_0$ to $1$ and call the polynomial $q$. Put
$$
 q_g=\chi(g)^{-w(M_0)}L_gq-q .
$$
This is still in $\mathcal I$, has no new monomials, and loses the $M_0$ term. If all $q_g=0$, every nonzero $c_M$ satisfies $g(c_M)=\chi(g)^{w(M_0)-w(M)}c_M$ for every $g$. Tate vanishing forces its weight to be $w(M_0)$, so the polynomial is homogeneous. Otherwise take $g$ with $q_g\ne0$ and a monomial $M_1$ with a nonzero coefficient in $q_g$. Subtract a scalar multiple of $q_g$ from $q$ to cancel $M_1$. Both the difference and $q_g$ have strictly fewer terms than $q$. The induction hypothesis applied to each proves that every weight component of $q$ lies in $\mathcal I$. Undoing the normalization finishes the induction. [Finite-support weight argument](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/InertiaEigenvalues/TateWeightGraded.lean#L5).

Now if $f$ vanishes on $\rho(H)$, take $q(Z)=f(ZP^{-1})$, which lies in $\mathcal I$. Each weight component $q_d$ vanishes at $P=\rho(1)P$. Under the column scaling by $D(t)$ it satisfies $q_d(PD(t))=t^d q_d(P)$ for $t\ne0$. Summing over the finitely many $d$ gives
$$
       f(PD(t)P^{-1})=0 \qquad(t\in\mathbb C_\ell^\times).
$$
This proves containment by vanishing equations, not by lifting $t$ to a Galois element. At the actual caller a polynomial over $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ vanishing on $\rho(G_E)$ still vanishes after extension to $\mathbb C_\ell$, restriction to the local decomposition group and all the subsequent finite restrictions and changes of group. The matrix values are identified by the bridge and finite model. In fact the source verifies the local containment even for equations on a prescribed deeper local group: conjugate that finite extension along with $K$, then increase $K_1$ to its compositum with the coefficient field and the field already used for the eigenbasis. The same basis still satisfies the matrix relation on the smaller group. [Vanishing on the cocharacter](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/InertiaEigenvalues/VanishAtHodgeTateCocharacter.lean#L145) · [Local finite restriction and coefficient inclusion](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/InertiaEigenvalues/MemClosureOfJumps.lean#L12-L67) · [From local to global equations](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/ZetaCharacterDivisibility/HtCocharOf.lean#L29-L44).

## 4. Differentiate and use the result in `DivSet`

For a global equation $f$, the expression $f(PD(t)P^{-1})$ is a Laurent polynomial over $\mathbb C_\ell$. It vanishes at all nonzero scalars and therefore is the zero Laurent polynomial: multiply by a power of $t$ and use infinitude of the field. Substitute $t=1+\epsilon$ in the dual numbers, where $\epsilon^2=0$. For any integer $k$, including negative $k$, $(1+\epsilon)^k=1+k\epsilon$. Thus
$$
 PD(1+\epsilon)P^{-1}=I+\epsilon X,\qquad
 X=P\operatorname{diag}(k_i)P^{-1}.
$$
The coefficient of $\epsilon$ in $f(I+\epsilon X)$ is $df_I(X)$. It is zero for every global equation by the Laurent identity, which is exactly `InLieZar`. Because $X$ is conjugate to the displayed diagonal matrix, its characteristic polynomial includes all marked jumps $e(i)\in J_0$ as roots. The embedding $\mathbb Q\hookrightarrow\mathbb C_\ell$ preserves the distinctness and the cross-multiplied inequalities defining lacunarity. No topological logarithm, local analytic derivative or choice of an eigenbasis over $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ is necessary. [Laurent polynomial and dual numbers](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/WeilBound/CoreStatements/PadicHodgeComparison/CocharacterLieDivisors/CocharacterLieAlgebra/PCocharToLie.lean#L74-L177) · [Lacunarity and identified roots](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/WeilBound/CoreStatements/PadicHodgeComparison/CocharacterLieDivisors/CocharacterLieAlgebra/PCocharToLie.lean#L182-L205).

The resulting tangent witness is the one passed by `divSet_of` to `goal_holds`. The same call separately supplies monic annihilators of degree $a$ on a dense set and the determinant-character condition; these come from the away-from-$\ell$ Satake/Frobenius and central-character arguments, not from the Hodge cocharacter. `DivSet` retains the two embeddings and openness and density of the separable characteristic polynomial locus. `DivOne` uses those inclusions to recover the specified inverse-Frobenius polynomials on that locus, and `DivAll` compares the extracted representations among the allowed models. In particular the Hodge argument supplies a rank test but does not identify a unique global representation by itself. ## Inputs not reproved here

The substantive period-theory inputs used above are: the concrete tied local bridge and filtered de Rham comparison (including scalar extension and independence of counts), the existence and properties of the period parameter and residue map, and Tate's vanishing of nontrivially twisted invariants in $\mathbb C_\ell$. These are referenced results with proofs in the pinned Lean source, not added axioms or a presumed group-theoretic analogue. This chapter does not reconstruct the Hecke tower, prove its Hodge-trace occurrence or the automorphic gap estimate, reproduce Chebotarev or the central-character construction, or redo the subsequent local–global transfer in `DivAll`. The semilinear ideal, the weight separation, and the passage from the cocharacter to the tangent witness are proved here, not assumed.
