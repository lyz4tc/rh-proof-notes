# The auxiliary group ring supports one universal Selmer matrix

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $O_0$ be the valuation ring of a finite extension of $\mathbb Q_\ell$, with uniformizer $\pi$ and residue field $k_0$. Fix $M\ge1$, a finite set $J$, and continuous characters $d_j:G_F\to\mathbb Z/\ell^M\mathbb Z$ on the Galois group of the CM host field. Assume each $d_j$ is trivial on the decomposition groups over $\ell$ and on inertia away from the indicated auxiliary place and its conjugate. Write $Q$ for the union of the auxiliary pairs, disjoint from the places over $\ell$. Set $D=(\mathbb Z/\ell^M\mathbb Z)^J$ and $\Lambda=O_0[D]$. If $[d(g)]\in\Lambda^\times$ is the basis element for the tuple of character values at $g$, the universal twist is $\kappa(g)=[d(g)]^{-1}$.

Fix one integral Galois representation of rank $r$ and the local conditions of the Selmer setup, with unrestricted classes at the allowed places in $Q$ away from $\ell$. For each discrete coefficient module $B$ whose elements are killed by powers of $\ell$, form $F(B)$ using global cocycles for $B^r$ with the given representation multiplied by $\kappa$. Assume its coefficient maps preserve the local conditions, it is left exact, each element of $F(B)$ comes from $F(B_0)$ for some finitely generated submodule $B_0\subseteq B$, and for extensions with kernel the residual field the lifting quotient is finite and bounded in cardinality by the finite residual Selmer group.

**Proposition.** There is one square matrix $\Phi$ over $\Lambda$, with every entry in its maximal ideal, whose kernel on $B^a$ is naturally $F(B)$ for all such $B$. If a coefficient map sends all components of a cocycle to those of a second cocycle, it also sends the corresponding kernel vector componentwise.

**Proof.** The group ring is finite free over $O_0$ with basis indexed by $D$ and is Noetherian. The reduction $k_0[D]$ has nilpotent augmentation ideal. In fact it is generated by $x_j=[e_j]-1$ for the coordinate generators $e_j$, and $x_j^{\ell^M}=0$ in characteristic $\ell$. Products of more than $|J|(\ell^M-1)$ generators are therefore zero. Modulo $\pi$, the ideal is nilpotent with quotient $k_0$, so it is the unique maximal ideal of $k_0[D]$. Every maximal ideal of $\Lambda$ lies over the maximal ideal of $O_0$ by finiteness. This proves that $\Lambda$ is local with residue field $k_0$. Its maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$ contains $\pi\Lambda$, and some power of $\mathfrak m$ is contained in $\pi\Lambda$. Consequently the two adic topologies are equivalent. Finite freeness makes $\Lambda$ complete for the $\pi$-adic topology and therefore for the $\mathfrak m$-adic topology. The ideals $(\ell)$ and $(\pi)$ have cofinal powers, so $\ell$-power torsion is equivalent to $\mathfrak m$-power torsion.

The twist has open kernel because the tuple of continuous characters has finite image. Its values have order dividing $\ell^M$. Triviality on the decomposition groups over $\ell$ and on inertia outside $Q$ follows coordinatewise. Thus the universal character satisfies the auxiliary hypotheses for the chosen integral Galois representation. For coefficient maps the induced map on cocycles is componentwise application, and coboundaries map to coboundaries. It is linear on cohomology and on the local-condition submodules. The Selmer assignment with these maps is a linear functor.

The [finite-stage assembly](the-intersection-of-finite-relations-represents-the-torsion-selmer-functor.md) gives a presentation by the homomorphisms out of $\Lambda^a/N$ with $N\subseteq\mathfrak m\Lambda^a$. The [residual obstruction estimate for relations](a-selmer-obstruction-bound-makes-the-presentation-square.md) gives at most $a$ generators of $N$. Arrange them as rows and pad with zero rows. This is the matrix $\Phi$, and every entry lies in $\mathfrak m$. Naturality is part of the homomorphism description and coincides with the cocycle coefficient maps described above. This matrix is the input for [specializing the determinant to a twisted Selmer length](a-universal-matrix-specializes-to-the-twisted-selmer-length.md).
