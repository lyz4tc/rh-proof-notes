# One approximation preserves Hecke coordinates and adds generic Satake data

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $E$ be a CM field, $a\ge1$ odd, $\ell$ prime, and $\iota\colon\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\simeq\mathbb C$. Use the $\ell$-adic norm transported by $\iota$. Let $U$ be an irreducible automorphic representation of the standard totally definite unitary group of rank $a$, unramified at every nonsplit place of $E/E^+$ outside a finite set $T$ of nonsplit places. Let $w_0,v'\mid\ell$ be split by $E/E^+$ and lie over distinct places of $E^+$. The theorem `CongFinOdd` constructs congruence approximations to $U$ that preserve the fixed levels outside those two pairs, keep $v'$ spherical, and impose a supercuspidal type at $w_0$ and lacunary jumps at an embedding above $v'$. The approximation also retains test coordinates at all requested spherical places, even when some spherical places divide $\ell$. ## The quantifiers and the approximating systems

There are levels $K_w$ with nonzero fixed vectors in the local components of $U$ at split places $w$ away from the two pairs. The groups $K_w$ at the split places outside $w_0,\bar w_0$ are compact open. At a spherical split place outside both pairs one may take $K_w=\mathrm{GL}_a(\mathcal O_{E_w})$. These levels are fixed once, before prescribing any of the following data.

- Choose finite sets $Z,W$ of split places outside both pairs where $U$ is spherical, with $w\mid\ell$ for every $w\in W$. At all places in $Z\cup W$ require the new Satake multisets to be generic. Here generic means that the specified integer polynomial $D_a$ has nonzero value for every ordering of the multiset. In the application $D_a$ is the nonzero [polynomial `DGen`](a-deep-weight-shift-forces-the-required-gap-and-generic-tests.md).
- There are nonzero scaling factors $c_{w,n}\in\mathbb C$ for all places $w$ and integers $n$, chosen now, before choosing any extra Hecke operators. For each $w\in W,n\in\mathbb Z$, the coordinate to be approximated is $\iota^{-1}(c_{w,n}\operatorname{PowSat}(U,w,n))$, where $\operatorname{PowSat}(V,w,n)=\sum_i b_i^n$ for the algebraically normalized Satake roots $b_i$ of $V$ at $w$.
- Let $J$ be any index set. For each $j\in J$ choose a split place $s_j\nmid\ell$ and a finite complex linear combination $t_j^{\rm loc}$ of elements of $\mathrm{GL}_a(E_{s_j})$, all coefficients of $\ell$-adic norm at most $1$. Assume it acts by a scalar on the $K_{s_j}$-fixed subspace of each irreducible smooth representation, with a specified function of the representation giving that scalar. Take $\theta_j$ to be the transported scalar on the local component of $U$.

For every $\varepsilon>0$ there is a finite $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$-linear combination of systems of these coordinates for representations $U'$, which approximates evaluation on the original system $(\theta_j,\iota^{-1}(c_{w,n}\operatorname{PowSat}(U,w,n)))$ to within $\varepsilon$ for **every** integer polynomial in the joined coordinates. Each $U'$ is unramified at the same nonsplit places as required of $U$, has the same adapted split levels $K_w$ outside both pairs, and is spherical at $v'$. Its weights agree with $U$ away from the embeddings over the two pairs, and its shifted infinitesimal parameters at one embedding inducing $v'$ have the lacunarity of `GapWt`. The construction changes the weights only at that embedding and its conjugate. Every $U'$ has the [local type `TyEll`](finite-fourier-sums-insert-a-supercuspidal-local-type.md) at $w_0$, and at every place in $Z\cup W$ its Satake multiset is generic. If the coefficients of all $t_j^{\rm loc}$ are rational, there is also one finite extension of $\mathbb Q_\ell$ containing all original $\theta_j$. ## Combining three classes of operators

At each finite local factor take a compact open subgroup fixing some nonzero vector of $U$. Take the integral subgroup wherever such a fixed vector exists, and otherwise use the intersection of the integral subgroup with the stabilizer of a fixed nonzero smooth vector. These choices equal the integral subgroup almost everywhere, by smoothness. Since local factors commute and $U$ has irreducible local components, their product away from the $w_0$-pair has a nonzero fixed vector in $U$. At $v'$, enlarge the chosen subgroup to the full hyperspecial subgroup in the product $K$, and keep a separate record $K_v$ of the original subgroup. The subgroup $K'=K\cap c_{v'}^{-1}(K_v)$ is the original product. A nonzero $K'$-fixed vector gives $K_w$-fixed vectors locally away from the two pairs. A $K$-fixed vector forces sphericality at $v'$ and the required nonsplit unramified conditions. [Construction and fixedness of the two levels](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CongruenceOddLevel/OpenCompactMap.lean#L77)

Use the split local-factor maps to place three classes of operators in the global space of finite-support sums of adelic right translations. The operators $t_j^{\rm loc}$ supply the first class, acting by the given scalars on $K'$-fixed vectors. For the second and third classes use the spherical Hecke operations at the relevant places.

1. A spherical operator at $w\in W$ can act by the $n$-th power sum of the normalized Satake roots for any $n\in\mathbb Z$, including negative $n$, since the roots are nonzero. Rescale by $c_{w,n}\ne0$ to make the operator $\ell$-integral in both senses `IntW` and `IntL` for the original weight $\lambda$.
2. At $z\in Z\cup W$, realize the symmetric polynomial $\prod_{\sigma\in S_a}D_a(X_{\sigma(0)},\ldots,X_{\sigma(a-1)})$ as a spherical operator. The product is a nonzero polynomial and vanishes on exactly the unwanted Satake multisets. Denote its operator by $A_z$, again rescaled to satisfy both integrality bounds. On any nonzero $K'$-fixed space its scalar is nonzero exactly when the Satake multiset is generic.

The rescalings exist because each operator uses finitely many group elements. First bound all denominators at places over $\ell$ in the matrices and their inverses on this finite support. Then a sufficiently large power of $\ell$ in the coefficients compensates for every denominator in the weight representation bounds. This is done separately for each $w,n$ and each test operator. The operators indexed by $j\in J$ already satisfy the bounds, since their support is trivial at every place above $\ell$ and their coefficients have norm at most $1$. All three classes are supported away from the $w_0$- and $v'$-pairs. ## First impose genericity, then impose weight and type

The [density input `DenseGen`](a-half-dimensional-good-space-gives-dense-generic-hecke-systems.md) is the following precise statement. At a compact open level away from a split $w_0\mid\ell$ and at any fixed algebraic weight, take a finite set of split spherical test places away from $w_0,\bar w_0$ at which the level is hyperspecial, with a nonzero polynomial in the Satake coordinates at each. For any form $f$ of that weight fixed by the level and any $\delta>0$, there is a finite sum $F$ of forms of the same weight and level in irreducible automorphic representations satisfying every test, and $f(v)(g)-F(v)(g)=e(g)f(v)(g)$ for a function with $|\iota^{-1}e(g)|_\ell\le\delta$. Apply it at $K'$, to the set $Z\cup W$. The [contraction argument](a-small-vector-error-gives-one-approximation-for-all-hecke-polynomials.md) with the integral evaluation frame converts this into polynomial approximation of the original system along **all three classes of coordinates**, just as in the local type argument. At this stage the intervening systems have generic Satake data and the original weight. [Density to polynomial approximation](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/SeparatingAlgebras/ToPolyX.lean#L11) · [Application of density](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CongruenceOddLevel/NotApartOfOverPair.lean#L208)

For each intervening system, apply `StepSt` from the [weight and type construction](finite-fourier-sums-insert-a-supercuspidal-local-type.md) using the same operators and $K\supseteq K'$. This gives further approximating systems of $K$-fixed forms with the local type at $w_0$ and lacunarity at $v'$. To retain genericity, let $Q=\prod_{z\in Z\cup W}X_z$ be the product of the genericity coordinate variables. Its value on every intervening system is nonzero. For such a system $\xi$, approximate the polynomial $P Q$ to whatever precision is needed and use the coefficients multiplied by $Q$ evaluated at each new system, then divide all coefficients by $Q(\xi)$. This gives an approximation for $P$ and all summands with $Q=0$ drop out. The coefficients used in this modification depend on the systems and $Q$, independently of $P$. Composition of finite linear combinations, with smaller tolerances in the second stage to absorb their coefficients and the divisions, proves approximation by systems satisfying all tests. Finally discard the auxiliary test coordinates by taking polynomials in just $J$ and the power-sum coordinates. [Cut by the product of test coordinates](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CongruenceOddLevel/NotApartOfOverPair.lean#L217) · [Use of the common polynomial cut](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/SeparatingAlgebras/Combine.lean#L94)

Every representation retained at this point has a nonzero $K$-fixed vector. It therefore inherits the required split levels, sphericality at $v'$, and nonsplit unramified conditions. A single weight assignment at each archimedean embedding is forced by its local irreducible component. Thus the weight agreement and lacunarity from `StepSt` are those of the resulting representations, not just of the chosen forms. The scalar identities in the first two operator classes identify their coordinates with the same $\theta_j$-variables and the same scaled power sums. ## One finite field for the rational test eigenvalues

Assume now that the $t_j^{\rm loc}$ have rational coefficients. The lifted operators have rational coefficients too. Choose a nonzero $K'$-fixed automorphic eigenfunction $f$ in $U$. Restrict $f$ to the finite adeles, apply an automorphism $\sigma$ of $\mathbb C$ pointwise there, and extend the result as an automorphic form. The conjugation result for definite-unitary forms asserts that all these extensions exist and span a finite-dimensional space. They are nonzero because the original function is detected by its restriction to finite adeles, using density modulo rational points. The rational operators commute with this conjugation on finite adeles, so the conjugate form has system $(\sigma(\iota(\theta_j)))_{j\in J}$. Distinct full systems have linearly independent simultaneous eigenvectors. In a finite relation of shortest nonzero length, applying an operator minus the eigenvalue on one vector kills that vector and leaves at least one term, a contradiction. Hence the full system has only finitely many conjugates. [Finite orbit argument](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/SatakeParameters/LinearIndependentSimulEigen.lean#L49)

In particular each coordinate is algebraic, since a transcendental complex number has infinitely many images under automorphisms of $\mathbb C$. For every finite subset of the coordinates the field it generates has degree bounded by the number of conjugate full systems: every embedding of a finitely generated algebraic subfield extends to an automorphism of $\mathbb C$. A uniform bound on finite subfield degrees implies that the union is a number field. Indeed a finite subfield of greatest possible degree cannot increase on adjoining any further coordinate. Transport this field to $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ with $\iota^{-1}$ and adjoin it to $\mathbb Q_\ell$. The resulting extension is finite and contains all $\theta_j$.
