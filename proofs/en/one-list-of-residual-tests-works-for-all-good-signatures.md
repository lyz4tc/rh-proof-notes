# One list of residual tests works for all good signatures

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Fix a twist index with coefficient valuation ring $O_0$, residue field $k$, residual characteristic $\ell$, representation rank $r$ and cyclotomic exponent $t$. Write $\epsilon:G_F\to O_0^\times$ for the $\ell$-adic cyclotomic character. The index parameter $\nu$ is the least $\ell$-adic order of $\epsilon(g)^{tr}-1$ occurring in the $\mathbb Z_\ell$-valued character, before extending coefficients to $O_0$. The minimum is finite, and it is attained by an element with $\epsilon(g)^{tr}-1=\ell^\nu u$, $u\in O_0^\times$. Fix the finite list of signatures satisfying the Galois hypotheses. At each such signature the source has an integral identity

$$
 T^D(\sigma g)=\epsilon(g)^t A_0 T(g)A_0^{-1}
$$

for an integral invertible intertwiner $A_0$, where $T^D$ denotes the cyclotomic dual. Assume that the ordinary and dual unrelaxed residual Selmer spaces of every signature are finite and have equal dimensions. Also assume the scalar inertia and matrix-span hypotheses used in the two cocycle-form cases.

**Proposition.** There is an integer $R$, depending only on the finite list of signatures and its residual spaces, with the following property. For any designated tame inertia element and common open normal subgroup $L$ small enough to satisfy those scalar and matrix hypotheses, one may choose $R$ elements $h_a\in L$ and an open normal $U\subseteq L$. A list of Frobenius elements and their conjugates realizing these classes modulo $U$ supplies the Frobenius rank condition for every signature. The permitted left corank is zero if $\nu=0$, and at most one if $\nu>0$.

**Proof.** A residual conjugate-dual intertwiner over $k$ would imply $\det\bar T^D(\sigma g)=\det\bar T(g)$ for all $g$. Taking determinants in the displayed integral identity and reducing gives
$\det\bar T^D(\sigma g)=\bar\epsilon(g)^{tr}\det\bar T(g)$. Cancelling the nonzero determinant would make $\bar\epsilon(g)^{tr}=1$ identically. The element attaining the minimum has nonzero $\epsilon(g)^{tr}-1$ modulo the maximal ideal if $\nu=0$. Therefore residual equivalence can occur only when $\nu>0$.

For an inequivalent signature, use the [finite transport of the full form](finitely-many-frobenius-tests-preserve-the-selmer-pairing.md) to realize a nonsingular form. For an equivalent signature use the same note on the sum of the ordinary and transported dual spaces to realize an alternating form of left corank at most one on the two spaces. The number of chosen elements is bounded by the square of the appropriate ambient dimension. These dimensions are determined by the fixed signature, its Selmer spaces and its chosen residual intertwiner before $L$ and the prime choices. Pad with identities when necessary to use that fixed bound.

Concatenate the finitely many lists and let $R$ be their combined length. Intersect their open normal subgroups with $L$. A Frobenius list meeting each prescribed class for the resulting subgroup meets every individual prescription for its larger subgroup. Each signature can then use the combination for its own form, with zero coefficients at unused indices. The rank bound thus holds simultaneously. The version quantified over residual coefficient fields receiving a surjective map from $O_0$ follows as well: any such map killing the maximal ideal identifies its target with $k$. Transport through that field isomorphism preserves cocycle evaluation, the local conditions, and linear independence.

**Lean correspondence.** `nu_pos_of_resIso` uses determinants to exclude residual equivalence at $\nu=0$. `single_field` chooses the form, `single` transfers to any surjective residue field, and `chooseH` concatenates the tests. The cyclotomic minimum and its attained value are expressed in `exists_eps_eq_pow_nu`.
