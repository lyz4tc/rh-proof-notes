# Class-group radicals give generators of prescribed CM type norm

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $E/\mathbb Q$ be a Galois CM field with embedding $\iota:E\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$. Let $r_\tau\ge0$ be an integer exponent at each embedding $\tau:E\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$ and suppose $r_\tau+r_{\bar\tau}=n_0$ is constant. For $x\in E^\times$ put $P_r(x)=\prod_\tau\tau(x)^{r_\tau}$. Write $H=|\mathrm{Cl}(E)|$ and choose a finite list of fractional ideals $\mathfrak c_i$ generating the class group. Let $d_i$ be the order of their classes, and choose $\gamma_i\in E^\times$ with $\mathfrak c_i^{d_i}=(\gamma_i)$. Let $K_r\subset \mathbb C$ be generated over $\iota(E)$ by a primitive $H$-th root of unity and all solutions of $z^{d_i}=P_r(\gamma_i)$ for every $i$. For each embedded number field $F\supseteq K_r$ and every nonzero integral ideal $\mathfrak a$ of $E$, there is $\beta\in F^\times$ with
$$
 (\beta)=\prod_\tau (\tau(\mathfrak a)\mathcal O_F)^{r_\tau},
 \qquad
 |\sigma(\beta)|^2=(N\mathfrak a)^{n_0}\quad\text{for every }\sigma:F\hookrightarrow\mathbb C .
 \tag{1}
$$
In the product, $\tau(\mathfrak a)\mathcal O_F$ means the $\mathcal O_F$-ideal generated by the image of $\mathfrak a$. The field $K_r$ is the source's `kPsiField` for the exponent pattern that assigns one exponent to the chosen CM type and the other to its conjugate.

Choose integers $a_i$ expressing the class of $\mathfrak a$ in the given generators. There is an $\alpha\in E^\times$ such that $\mathfrak a=(\alpha)\prod_i\mathfrak c_i^{a_i}$ as fractional ideals. With $b_i=H/d_i$, this gives
$$
 \mathfrak a^H=(\gamma),\qquad \gamma=\alpha^H\prod_i\gamma_i^{a_i b_i}.
$$
Choose one $\psi_i$ with $\psi_i^{d_i}=P_r(\gamma_i)$, and set $\beta=P_r(\alpha)\prod_i\psi_i^{a_i}$. These factors are nonzero and belong to $F$, the first since $E/\mathbb Q$ is Galois. By multiplicativity and $d_i b_i=H$ we have $\beta^H=P_r(\gamma)$. The $H$-th power of the ideal product in (1) is also $(P_r(\gamma))$, because ideal extension commutes with products and powers. Raising to $H$ is injective on nonzero fractional ideals of $F$, by the unique prime exponents. Thus the first claim of (1) holds.

For an embedding $\sigma:F\to\mathbb C$, expand
$$
 |\sigma(\beta)|^{2H}
   =\prod_\tau |(\sigma\circ\tau)(\gamma)|^{2r_\tau}.
$$
The compositions permute the embeddings of $E$, and every conjugate pair goes to a conjugate pair because the CM involution is central. The values in a pair have equal absolute value, and the sum of their exponents is $n_0$. The product is therefore $|N_{E/\mathbb Q}\gamma|^{n_0}=(N\mathfrak a)^{Hn_0}$. Taking positive $H$-th roots proves the second claim. This uses the specified conjugate-pair sums exactly. Arbitrary choices of roots $\psi_i$ only multiply $\beta$ by roots of unity.

The generating field is compatible with the [single ambient Kummer extension](a-finite-kummer-extension-contains-the-normalized-hecke-values.md). For each $i$, the principal ideal of $\gamma_i$ is a $d_i$-th power. The elements of $E$ with such principal ideals form a subgroup stable under automorphisms. Thus the element of $\iota(E)$ given by $P_r(\gamma_i)$ corresponds to another such element of $E$. Every $\psi_i$ lies in $V_{d_i}\subseteq V_H$. The other generators lie there as well. Since the ordinary class group is a quotient of the ray class group, $H$ divides its order $h$, and $K_r\subseteq V_h$.
