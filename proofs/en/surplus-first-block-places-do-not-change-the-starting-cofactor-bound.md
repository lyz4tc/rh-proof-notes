# Surplus first-block places do not change the starting cofactor bound

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Fix a good signature of rank $r$, residue characteristic $\ell$, and a localized family with $R\ge n$ first-block places and $n$ second-block places at auxiliary level $\ell^M$, where $4rn+2<\ell^M$. Assume the first-block Frobenius is conjugate to a fixed tame inertia element modulo a common small subgroup, the second block has the first $n$ dual Frobenius coordinates, and the residual Selmer forms at the second block have common radical of dimension at most one. The residual representation, characters, decomposition subgroups, and auxiliary family satisfy the `GalHyp`, `Small` and `PiLocalization₂` conditions. Then for every admissible relaxation set and every square Selmer presentation, either the matrix is empty or one adjugate entry has a unit coefficient of degree at most $4rn$ in every polynomial lift, on a monomial using only the first $n$ first-block variables and the $n$ second-block variables.

First consider a local representation on an $\ell$-primary torsion coefficient module at $v\nmid\ell$, where inertia acts trivially. A continuous cocycle restricts to a continuous homomorphism on inertia with finite $\ell$-group image. Hence it factors through the pro-$\ell$ part of tame inertia, generated topologically by a generator $\gamma$. Its value $m$ on $\gamma$ satisfies $\rho(\phi)m=Nv\cdot m$, by the tame Frobenius relation and the cocycle identity. If the untwisted Frobenius matrix over $O$ is residual scalar $u\ne1$, $Nv\equiv1\bmod\mathfrak m_O$, and the twist character has order dividing $\ell^M$, raise the equation to the $\ell^M$-th power in the sense of iterating Frobenius. This gives $(\rho_{\rm untw}(\phi^{\ell^M})-(Nv)^{\ell^M})m=0$. The matrix in parentheses is residual scalar $(u-1)^{\ell^M}$ in characteristic $\ell$ and is invertible over $O$. Consequently $m=0$, even on torsion modules without any finite-length assumption. Every local class is unramified.

Now set the surplus first-block variables to zero, equivalently delete those group coordinates. A place above a deleted first-block place is unramified for the retained characters by admissibility. The untwisted representation is also unramified there and has nontrivial residual scalar Frobenius: normality and the small subgroup compare it with the scalar of the tame element, and conjugate self-duality gives the inverse scalar at the companion place because the cyclotomic character is residually $1$. The local norms are $1\bmod\ell$. The preceding calculation makes the relaxed and unrelaxed local conditions equal there. Consequently after specialization, the original presentation computes the Selmer functor for the balanced family of $n$ places in each block. This equality holds on every torsion coefficient module and commutes with coefficient maps, since the Galois actions coincide and only those redundant local conditions are removed.

Apply the [residual cofactor bound](a-dual-number-line-gives-the-residual-selmer-coefficient-bound.md) along the chosen Frobenius tests, using the dimension bound of one on the common radical and the stated inequality at level $M$. This gives an adjugate entry of the specialized presentation whose every polynomial lift has a unit coefficient of degree at most $4rn$. Determinant minors commute with specialization. In any lift of the original entry, the same coefficient is obtained by giving deleted variables exponent zero. This yields the assertion for the original presentation.

**Lean correspondence.** `unrAt_eq_top` and `eq_zero_of_scalar` give the local argument. `computes_map` changes the scalar ring and `start_of_galHyp` deletes the surplus places, applies the residual bound, and lifts the coefficient back. `start_of_resBound` is its entry point in the dyadic assembly.
