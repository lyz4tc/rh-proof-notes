# Keeping the lower origin lets the second pass rebuild the same level data

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

The [two-pass family construction](two-passes-give-layered-objects-over-the-same-numerical-context.md) starts with an untwisted core at a fixed good numerical context, exports the necessary twist tests, then constructs a core at the same context on an enlarged coefficient base. This note gives the comparison across the intermediate levels. Fix that context, base, frame and pinned columns, and use the following construction inputs on a common eventual range.

- From the starting pinned columns there is an unlocalized state with zero auxiliary characters. Its marked weight-place set is saturated over the fixed coefficient field for the untwisted input. There is a level with the same Galois data where the local Langlands, multiplicity, rationality and cuspidality conditions can be imposed together. At this level the prescribed list carried by the state is still available.
- Three successive choices attach the settings of the forms, the series, and the moduli points. Their assertions take the existing state and list to a next state with a transported list. The next strictly Henselian condition provides a compatible character reading. The finiteness assertion for that level then gives a later column datum and a level-three packet keeping the weight-place set and the character reading of the state. These are the first-pass level-existence inputs.
- After coefficient enlargement and twist pullback, the pinned columns can be carried to the new base, with the required extra avoidance and algebraic coefficients. The initial state there can be chosen with the same Galois data. Carrying the lower state to the rebuilt intermediate level preserves its exports and embedded coefficient data, and transports its list without changing the weight-place test. This is the move-to-base assertion used in the source. On the carried strictly Henselian state, any compatible conjugation-invariant place reading may be prescribed, and the same finiteness assertion applies to it.

Under these inputs, every untwisted built core with a valid export can be retwisted with any specified finite avoidance and coefficient requests. It yields a level-three packet over the enlarged base for that same context, with the original normalizing data and the good-twist certificate adjoined. In particular the packet has the original construction's validity requirements and the strengthened all-powers input needed to finish the second core.

## Retain the lower origin during the first pass

Take the initial saturated state. Choose the same-Galois local Langlands level, adjoin its multiplicity, rationality and cusp clauses, and make the three successive settings, series and moduli choices. The composite of their list implications yields the list at the final state. Taking its strictly Henselian lift and then the finiteness output gives the level-three packet. Keep with the packet a witness of the old state, its strictly Henselian level, its list, the saturation, and the two equalities relating its weight-place set and character read to those of the output. Keeping this witness does not change any entries of the packet. It records the state on which the exported good-character assertion can later be used. The level-four and core constructions use this extended packet.

## Carry the same tests through the second pass

Given an export from the built core, extract the retained state and its character read. The export and the retention equality give a [seed](the-first-pass-selects-disjoint-twist-supports-by-fixed-field-place.md) on that state for every finite exclusion. Combine the caller's exclusions with those needed for rebuilding the pinned columns, and combine the two finite coefficient lists as well. Use [Artin reciprocity and field enlargement](artin-reciprocity-and-a-finite-field-enlargement-carry-the-twist-seed.md) on the enlarged requests. The resulting base supports the rebuilt column witness, and the left-hand pieces of the joint requests still give the caller's avoidances and inclusions.

Now take the promised initial state over the rebuilt columns, retaining the numerical context and Galois data. Use the same-Galois local Langlands step there, fill its multiplicity, rationality and cusp fields, and apply the move-to-base assertion to the old strictly Henselian state and the new intermediate level. The carried state has the corresponding list. A new coefficient place belongs to its marked set exactly when its old contraction belongs to the original marked set. Thus the saturation statement follows on the carried fibres from the old saturation and the equality of restrictions to the fixed field.

On this carried state prescribe the read at each new prime to be the old seed read at its contraction. The agreement of the state data makes this a legal read, and contraction commutes with conjugation, so this choice is conjugation invariant. The strictly Henselian and finiteness inputs give a level-three packet with that read, keeping the carried weight-place set and character data. The [primitive-power comparison](frobenius-comparison-recovers-a-single-primitive-power-of-a-twist.md) identifies each actual integral packet with the corresponding old packet to one exponent prime to its residue characteristic. The [good-twist transfer](the-rebuilt-packet-keeps-all-primitive-power-tests.md) makes the required certificate on the rebuilt state. Attach it to the packet. The subsequent level-four and core constructions are the same construction with the stronger input, and the [finite-requirement rule](finite-coefficient-and-prime-requirements-survive-the-retwisting-pass.md) places all their additional requirements in the suitable fields at the right time.
