# Unitary pullback gives integral moments for a normalized theta translate

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

## Statement

Use the host $D$ with its imaginary quadratic base $K$ of discriminant $-d$ for $d$ prime and $d\equiv7\bmod16$, and its CM field $E\supset K$ unramified at finite places over its maximal real subfield. Write $\Phi$ for the host CM embeddings of $E$ extending the chosen embedding of $K$. Let $\mathfrak c\subseteq\mathcal O_K$ be a nonzero ideal of norm prime to $2d$, take $\delta^{12}=\Delta(\mathfrak c)$, and let the host theta base $B$ use the elliptic period lattice $\Lambda_{\mathfrak c}=\delta\mathfrak c$. Let $\chi$ be an admissible determinant-ideal character and assume the covariance `EqP` of the natural theta family $e_{\mathrm{nat},\chi}$ under rational unitary transformations, with the host base conditions. The precise unitary equality used here is given below. In the Lean theorem `EqP` is supplied uniformly for all such data. [The host reconstruction of the theta equations](square-and-triangle-paths-give-the-host-theta-equations.md) describes the full equality package.

Take an odd prime $q$ split in $K$, with $q\nmid N(\mathfrak c)$, and a field isomorphism $\iota:\mathbb C\simeq\mathbb C_q$. Use the principal-lattice period data `Per`: $\theta$ is the normalized even theta of $\mathcal O_K$, $\delta_0^{12}=\Delta(\mathcal O_K)$ with $\delta_0\ne0$, and $\Omega\in\mathbb C_q^\times$ has norm one. At scale $\omega=\Omega/\iota(\delta_0)$ there is a good integral multiplicative chart, and the theta chart laws hold. In detail these are the unit coefficient bound for every prime-to-$q$ Heisenberg translate, the multiplicative shift with the bracket root of unity of order dividing $q^n$ for each good lift at level $n$, and preservation of every coefficient bound $B_0\ge0$ when all terms of a finite sum of powers of prime-to-$q$ translates are acted on by the same prime-to-$q$ shift before taking their powers. In the shift law the good lift is $N_0v/q^n$ with $v\in\mathcal O_K$, $|\iota(\bar v)|_q\le q^{-2n}$, and $N_0t\in\mathcal O_K$ for the initial translate $t$, with $q\nmid N_0$. The period data also give a unit $\Omega'$ satisfying the same theta laws after replacing $\iota$ by any field isomorphism $\iota'$. The period laws are detailed in [the theta chart integrality proof](a-split-cm-isogeny-forces-integrality-of-the-even-theta-series.md), [its shift extension](the-torsion-shift-extends-to-prime-to-p-theta-translates.md), and [the simultaneous translation bound](an-elliptic-quotient-keeps-cancellations-in-a-theta-translate-sum.md). [Principal-lattice period data `Per`](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/GlobalTwisting/LatIntZero.lean#L33) · [The theta laws](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/Jets/CmLatticeCommutatorPairingPowEqOneOfGoodLift.lean#L109)

For every pair of even ranks $N,N'\ge0$ and every pair of full standard-Hermitian self-dual lattices $L\subseteq E^N,\ L'\subseteq E^{N'}$, form the complex matrix period lattice $\Gamma(L,L')$ in $V=\prod_{\tau\in\Phi}\operatorname{Mat}_{N'\times N}(\mathbb C)$. It is the additive lattice generated by $w\,(\tau(A))_\tau$ for $w\in\Lambda_{\mathfrak c}$ and $A$ in the product of the inverse half-different ideal of the host with $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal O_E}(L,L')$. The latter consists of $E$-linear matrices carrying $L$ into $L'$. If $t\in V$ satisfies $nt\in\Gamma(L,L')$ for an integer $n>0$ prime to $q$, then
$$
           U_t e_{\mathrm{nat},\chi}(L,L')
       \quad\text{satisfies }\mathrm{LatInt}(\iota,\omega,\Gamma(L,L')).
$$
Here $U_t$ is translation by $t$ multiplied by $\exp(-\pi H(z,t)-\pi H(t,t)/2)$, with $H$ the sum of coordinate Hermitian products divided by $\operatorname{area}(\Lambda_{\mathfrak c})$. The conclusion means precisely the norm-at-most-one lattice moment property for every finite list of period directions from [the binomial jet criterion](binomial-jet-integrality-survives-products-pullbacks-and-tensor-descent.md). ## Proof

### Move the pair to standard lattices at $q$

Put $S_N=\mathcal O_E^N$, $S_{N'}=\mathcal O_E^{N'}$. Use [unitary approximation of Hermitian lattices](simultaneous-unitary-approximation-of-hermitian-lattices.md) at every finite place above $q$. That proof uses the local split form or the unramified quadratic form with odd residue characteristic, so it applies also at the odd split-in-$K$ prime of this theorem, including $q=3$. It gives unitary matrices $u,u'$ over $E$ and self-dual $M,M'$ with $L=uM,\ L'=u'M'$, where $M$ and $S_N$, and $M'$ and $S_{N'}$, agree upon localization at $q$. Equivalently there are positive integers $a,b$ prime to $q$ with
$$
  aM\subseteq S_N,\quad aS_N\subseteq M,\qquad
  bM'\subseteq S_{N'},\quad bS_{N'}\subseteq M'.
$$
The approximation over the host CM field applies without assuming that every place above $q$ splits over the maximal real subfield. [Weak approximation at the chosen prime](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/GlobalInterpolation/WeakApproximationUnitaryLatticesSplitPrime.lean#L14)

On complex matrix variables set $g(z)_\tau=\tau(u')^*z_\tau\tau(u)$, with star the conjugate transpose. This map is complex linear and preserves $H$, by unitarity and the trace formula for the matrix inner product. On period lattices it sends $\Gamma(L,L')$ to $\Gamma(M,M')$: a matrix $A$ sending $uM$ to $u'M'$ becomes $u'^{-1}Au$, which sends $M$ to $M'$. The inverse map gives the reverse inclusion. The inverse half-different scalars and elliptic periods commute with this operation. In particular $gt$ is a prime-to-$q$ torsion point of the $M,M'$ lattice. Covariance in `EqP` is
$$
 e_{\mathrm{nat},\chi}(L,L')=e_{\mathrm{nat},\chi}(M,M')\circ g.
$$
For any entire $F$, $U_t(F\circ g)=(U_{gt}F)\circ g$, because $g(z+t)=gz+gt$ and $H(z,t)=H(gz,gt)$. It remains to obtain an integral bound for the translate on the $M,M'$ pair. [The isometry and covariance of translation](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/GlobalInterpolation/UnitaryPairPullbackLatticesTranslates.lean#L39)

### Integral combination of standard translates

Let $\Gamma_0=\Gamma(S_N,S_{N'})$. The normalized vector at $M,M'$ is the character factor times two successive normalized finite theta averages from the standard vacuum $\theta_{\mathrm{std}}$. The exact calculation of [finite theta averages at a standard local pair](finite-theta-averages-at-a-standard-local-pair.md) applies at this $q$. Its conditions for the calculation are the two prime-to-$q$ mutual inclusions just obtained, the integral alternating form on the self-dual pair, and the admissible determinant-ideal character. The calculation uses no restriction on how $q$ splits. In particular the quotient orders in its two averages are prime to $q$, from the annihilators $a,b$. The phases are roots of unity by rationality of each shift relative to the lattice, and the determinant character is a unit under $\iota$ by the norm-one generator computation. Each summand has a total shift killed modulo $\Gamma_0$ by $nab$. Thus there is a finite representation
$$
       U_{gt}e_{\mathrm{nat},\chi}(M,M')=\sum_j c_j U_{s_j}\theta_{\mathrm{std}},
       \qquad |\iota(c_j)|_q\le1,\quad nab\,s_j\in\Gamma_0 ,
\tag{1}
$$
with the $c_j$ algebraic. Equally, the source theorem allows a separate prime-to-$q$ annihilator for each $s_j$. [Finite integrated coefficient expansion](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/GlobalInterpolation/TranslateNormalizedVectorIntegralCombTensorPower.lean#L15)

Use the standard-tensor moment input in the following form. For the same host curve and principal period data, every translate $U_s\theta_{\mathrm{std}}$ with $s$ prime-to-$q$ torsion on $\Gamma_0$ is integral at scale $\omega$ on $\Gamma_0$. Its derivation starts with the corresponding bound for the base function on the single-entry host period lattice. The standard vacuum is the product of that base function over all matrix entries. The standard period lattice is the product of the single-entry period lattices, and the Hermitian form is their orthogonal sum. Thus the translation factors entry by entry. Each entry of $s$ has the same allowed torsion denominator. Pull back the integral bound on each entry to the entire lattice using the corresponding coordinate projection, and multiply the bounds using the binomial jet criterion. This gives the precise standard-tensor input used here. [The single-entry proof](integral-elliptic-translates-give-the-single-entry-theta-moments.md) uses the fourfold isometry and elliptic translate theorem. [Single-entry host bound](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/GlobalInterpolation/CmThetaBaseVectorTranslatesIntegralStatement.lean#L7) · [Standard matrix tensor bound](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/GlobalInterpolation/CmBaseThetaTensorPowerTranslatesIntegral.lean#L32)

All functions in (1) are entire, so applying the integral sum operation to the standard-tensor bounds makes (1) integral on $\Gamma_0$.

### Return to the original period lattice

For $x\in\Gamma(L,L')$, its image $gx$ lies in $\Gamma(M,M')$ and $ab\,gx\in\Gamma_0$. To verify this last containment on generators, take an $E$-linear matrix $A$ sending $M$ into $M'$. For $v\in S_N$, $av\in M$, so $A(av)\in M'$ and $bA(av)\in S_{N'}$. Thus $ab A$ carries $S_N$ into $S_{N'}$. The fractional-ideal and elliptic-lattice scalar factors preserve the inclusion. Since $ab$ is prime to $q$, the pullback operation from the binomial jet criterion applies to $g$ and (1). The covariance identity above identifies this pullback with $U_t e_{\mathrm{nat},\chi}(L,L')$, which proves the stated bound.
