# A common prime of the imaginary field pins the column local choices

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $E/\mathbb Q$ be the host's finite Galois CM field, $F$ its maximal real subfield, and $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-d})\subseteq E$ its fixed imaginary quadratic subfield. For each rational prime $p$, choose one prime $\mathfrak p_K(p)$ of $K$ above $p$. For every finite prime $v$ of $F$ above $p$, there is a prime $P(v)$ of $E$ above both $v$ and $\mathfrak p_K(p)$. Thus one can choose a section of the restriction of primes to $F$ in which all primes selected above the same rational prime restrict to the same prime of $K$. The two distinguished split primes used in the column input can use the very same section.

To prove the simultaneous extension, take by lying-over primes $w,u$ of $E$ above $v,\mathfrak p_K(p)$, respectively. They both lie over $p$. Galois transitivity gives $\sigma\in\operatorname{Gal}(E/\mathbb Q)$ carrying $u$ to $w$. Its restriction to $K$ either fixes $\sqrt{-d}$ or negates it. In the first case $w$ already restricts to $\mathfrak p_K(p)$, since contraction to a field fixed by $\sigma$ is unchanged. In the second case, compose $\sigma$ with the CM involution $c$, which negates $\sqrt{-d}$ and fixes $F$. Now $c(w)$ still restricts to $v$, and the composite fixes $K$, so $c(w)$ restricts to $\mathfrak p_K(p)$. The unique rational prime below $v$ makes the construction consistent when choosing all the $P(v)$.

## Use in the initial column datum

Fix a good context with preliminary record $P$, coefficient-field base, frame and twist datum. The arithmetic input gives generator elements of the specified norms and congruence classes, one word-prime generator $\pi_u$ for each word $u$, and the diagonal coordinate spaces described in the [exact arithmetic field construction](an-exact-arithmetic-field-survives-two-coefficient-enlargements.md). In the column input, orient the two generator elements using the two chosen primes of $K$ in the frame. Exchanging a generator with its conjugate preserves its norm and rational congruence. Use the word generators from the context itself, with their prescribed ratios $c_u=\pi_{u_0}/\pi_u$ for the fixed reference word $u_0$. This ensures $c_u\pi_u=\pi_{u_0}$ and ties all the subsequent column realizations to the same numerical context. The source’s column-input existence result then applies with the specified prime section and frame. Its canonicalization preserves these choices and fixes the two local modulus exponents in each tuple to $m+1$, where $m$ is the number of layers. The canonicalization requires the original lower bounds $m+1$ on these exponents.

The extra coefficient requirement for the column jets can be imposed before choosing this column input. For a fixed preliminary record and its finite set of twist characters, the set of bad primes is finite. The source considers every word, layer $i<m$, and two subsets $S_1,S_2$ of these bad primes. For each such tuple the step jet set uses the fixed ranks at $i,i+1$, its degree fixed by the weight letters, and the prescribed modulus
$$
 T_u\Bigl(\prod_{\mathfrak p\in S_2}N\mathfrak p\Bigr)^{2(m+1)} .
$$
Include any step set that lies in a field generated by finitely many algebraic numbers. The union has the same property since the family of tuples is finite. Adjoin coefficients generating a field containing this union in the large-base list for the ambient field. This list depends on the frame and its available characters. The actual jet set of every later canonical column is included in the union. Indeed its local subsets are among the bad primes and its exact local modulus and degree are among those enumerated. The fixed-step algebraicity theorem gives its inclusion among the sets considered. Thus all its jet coefficients land in the chosen field. This argument requires finite generation of each relevant coefficient field and allows an infinite set of jet values.

The pinned column witness combines that canonical column, its prime section, the synchronized generators, the coordinate inclusion and the extra local roots of unity. The first pass starts from this same witness with the untwisted support convention and the trivial twist coherence. The individual column-existence and fixed-step algebraicity inputs are used at the stated points.
