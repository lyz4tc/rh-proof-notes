# A finite Hecke separator forces a term in the unitary trace formula

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $E$ be a CM field unramified over $E^+$ at all finite places, $n\ge1$, and $\pi$ a regular algebraic conjugate self-dual cuspidal representation of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb A_E)$, spherical at every nonsplit finite place. The trace formula argument proves the following alternative, called `RunTh`.

1. A definite unitary automorphic representation of rank $n$ is unramified at every nonsplit finite place and has the Satake parameters of $\pi$ at all but finitely many split places.
2. There is a finite list of positive-rank cuspidal $\mathrm{GL}$ representations of total rank $n$, containing no copy of $\pi$, for which the union of the infinitesimal parameters at each embedding is a dominant-weight parameter of rank $n$. The union of the Satake multisets agrees with $\pi$ at almost every split place and is invariant under inversion at almost every nonsplit place.

The second alternative is excluded by [split-place rigidity and the normalization argument](split-place-rigidity-upgrades-weak-unitary-descent-to-all-split-places.md). The proof below reconstructs the argument that one alternative must occur from the finite spectral expansions and their geometric transfer identity. [Exact alternative](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/TraceFormula/GaloisFromTraceFormula/EvaluationAtUnitaryOne.lean#L37) · [Main argument](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/TraceFormula/GaloisFromTraceFormula/AsmThOf.lean#L10)

## The trace comparison and the three finite lists

Choose one place of $E$ from every conjugate pair of finite places and include all nonsplit places. Write $S$ for this section. Choose the archimedean weight $\lambda$ read from $\pi$ and the unitary Hecke character $\mu$ restricting to the quadratic character of $E/E^+$. Its archimedean angular exponents $e_v$ are odd, and $\mu(w)=-1$ at the nonsplit places. Fix split test places $Z\subset S$ containing the split pairs where $\pi$ is nonspherical. The chosen local tests $f_w$, $w\in Z$, have nonzero $\pi_w$-trace. At one of these places the tests meet the regular-support conditions required for all parabolic constant terms below. Denote the remaining spherical parameters of $\pi$ by $B(w)$.

At split places outside $Z$ and its conjugates, choose further tests on a finite set $T\subset S$, also using the conjugate coordinate from each split pair. These are polynomials in spherical Hecke generators. For any parameter system $C(w)$, their combined scalar $v(C)$ is a product of their evaluations on $C(w)$ and on the inverse multiset at the conjugate places. Use the $k$-th powers of the chosen tests and hyperspecial units at all other finite places. The scalar factor on a system with parameters $C$ is consequently $v(C)^k$. The trace comparison uses the following data.

- The unitary trace for these tests is an **exact finite sum** $\sum_i A_i v(C_i)^k$. The list is fixed by the initial level, weight and $Z$. Each $C_i$ is the split Satake system of a definite unitary automorphic representation that is unramified at all nonsplit places. The constants $A_i$ depend on the initial tests and the finite multiplicity spaces, independently of $k,T$ and the chosen further polynomials. [Finite unitary evaluation](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/TraceFormula/GlobalTraceComparison/TraceAsymptotics/SplitLineAlt.lean#L78)
- The geometric trace transfer expresses this unitary trace as a signed finite sum of stable geometric terms on pairs of groups of ranks $a_i,b_i$ with $a_i+b_i=n$. The two endpoint terms $(n,0)$ and $(0,n)$ each occur once with sign $+1$, and their geometric terms agree after the weight and constant-term identifications. The infinitesimal multiset for $(a_i,b_i)$ becomes the weight of rank $n$ after shifting the first block by $b_i e_v/2$ and the second by $a_i e_v/2$ at each chosen infinite place. This is the identity `G2s`, evaluated on the tests matched by the parabolic constant term and the quadratic-character twist. [Signed comparison and weights](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/TraceFormula/GlobalTraceComparison/StableBlock.lean#L21) · [Test matching and endpoint comparison](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/TraceFormula/GlobalTraceComparison/TraceAsymptotics/SplitLineAlt.lean#L90)
- The stable spectral estimate for the $(n,0)$ term yields a finite list of competing rank-$n$ cuspidal data $D_j$ omitting $\pi$, with parameters $C_j(w)$. Their weights are those of $\pi$, and at nonsplit places their multisets are inversion-invariant wherever $B(w)$ is. If every competing $|v(C_j)|<r$, the difference between the stable geometric term and $P\,v(B)^k$ is $O((1+k)^N r^k)$ for some $N$. Here $P=c\prod_{w\in Z}\operatorname{tr}_{\pi_w}(f_w)$ for $c\ne0$, so $P\ne0$. The local parabolic averages give the same nonzero traces used to select the $f_w$. [Stable estimate](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/TraceFormula/GaloisFromTraceFormula/EvaluationAtUnitaryOne.lean#L78) · [The nonzero term](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/TraceFormula/GaloisFromTraceFormula/AsmThOf.lean#L65)
- Each interior term, with both ranks positive, has a finite list of competitors made from a pair $D_{1,j},D_{2,j}$ of positive-rank cuspidal data of those respective ranks. Each list is chosen once from the initial test and weights, before $T$, the further polynomials, or $r>0$ are specified. Combine their Satake parameters by multiplying the first multiset by $\mu(w)^{b_i}$ and the second by $\mu(w)^{a_i}$, then taking their union. Match of the local test functions makes the resulting $v(C_j)$ the scalar appearing in the estimate for the interior term. If all $|v(C_j)|<r$ for that term, it is $O((1+k)^N r^k)$. Each combined list omits $\pi$ because all its constituents have smaller rank. Its archimedean multiset is a dominant-weight parameter by the two weight shifts in the comparison identity, and it is inversion-invariant at nonsplit places because the original two lists have that property and the twist values are signs. [Interior estimate](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/TraceFormula/GaloisFromTraceFormula/EvaluationAtUnitaryOne.lean#L68) · [Combined data and local matching](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/TraceFormula/GaloisFromTraceFormula/AsmThOf.lean#L39)

The stability clauses for the two $\mathrm{GL}$ lists come from adding [nonsplit tests that preserve transfer](hecke-polynomials-separate-the-target-and-preserve-unit-transfer.md). For the argument below the important consequence of the four assertions is that the same finite further test will govern all three lists and the $\pi$ term.

## Choosing the further test

Suppose neither alternative holds. No unitary system in the first list can agree with $B$ at every split place outside $Z$ and its conjugates, since it would give the first alternative. No stable or interior competitor in the other lists can do so either. Their infinitesimal and nonsplit conditions just established would then give the second alternative. Thus for every member of these finitely many lists there is one split place, away from both $Z$ and its conjugates, where its multiset differs from $B$.

At the chosen distinguishing place for each member, some coefficient of the Satake polynomial differs from its value on $B$. If the differing coefficient takes values $x$ on $B$ and $y$ on the competitor, use $(X-y)/(x-y)$, which evaluates to $1$ and $0$, respectively. If the chosen place is the other member of the split pair, use the second Hecke coordinate and the inverse multisets. Inversion preserves distinctness. Multiply these tests, keeping the product of all factors at the same place and coordinate. This gives a finite set $T$ and polynomials with $v(B)=1$ and $v(C_j)=0$ for every competitor of all three lists. ## The trace identity at large powers

Take $k\ge1$. The left side of the trace comparison, the finite unitary sum, is zero since each $v(C_i)^k=0$. Set $r=1/2$. The finite competitor conditions for the stable and interior estimates hold. Every interior term therefore tends to zero, because $ (1+k)^N 2^{-k}\to0$. The stable $(n,0)$ term tends to $P$ because $v(B)^k=1$, and the other endpoint term is equal to this one and has sign $+1$. Passing to the limit in the finite sum gives $0=2P$, contradicting $P\ne0$. This proves the alternative. [Limit and contradiction](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/TraceFormula/GaloisFromTraceFormula/AsmThOf.lean#L66)
