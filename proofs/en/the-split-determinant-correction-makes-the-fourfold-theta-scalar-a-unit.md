# The split determinant correction makes the fourfold theta scalar a unit

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

## Statement

Let $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-d})$ with $d\equiv7\bmod16$ prime and the indicated embedding into $\mathbb C$. Use the host CM extension $E/K$ of degree $f$, its conjugation, and the inverse half-different fractional ideal $I$ of $E$, with the trace-Hermitian form $\sum_{a=1}^4\operatorname{Tr}_{E/K}(x_a\bar y_a)$ on $E^4$. Let $\gamma_0:E^4\simeq K^{4f}$ be the chosen $K$-linear isometry into the standard Hermitian form, and $g_0$ its corresponding complex isometry in the host embeddings. Work with the curve period lattice $\Lambda=\delta\mathfrak c$ for a nonzero integral ideal $\mathfrak c\subset\mathcal O_K$ of norm prime to $2d$, normalized by $\delta^{12}=\Delta(\mathfrak c)$. Let $\Gamma$ be the host single-entry lattice generated by scalar products of $\Lambda$ and the vectors $(\tau(x))_\tau$ for $x\in I$, and $S=\Lambda^{4f}$. Assume the host standard theta data of [the fourfold normalization](a-fourfold-average-generates-the-standard-theta-line.md), with the CM sign, full self-dual lattices $S$ and $\Gamma^4$ for their alternating forms, and the fourfold identity
$$
              F^{\boxtimes4}=\kappa K_{g_0}
$$
for its single-entry theta function $F$. Here $K_g$ is the normalized theta average from $S$ to $g(\Gamma^4)$, then evaluated at $g z$, defined in [the finite fourfold expansion](four-theta-copies-as-a-finite-sum-of-one-variable-translates.md).

In the normalization $\kappa$, let $h=|\mathrm{Cl}(K)|$ be the class number. For each rational split prime $p$ choose one prime $\mathfrak p_p\mid p$ and a generator $\pi_p$ of $\mathfrak p_p^h$. Choose $r_p\in\mathbb C$ with $r_p^{2h}=\pi_p/\overline{\pi_p}$ and a $K$-linear Hermitian isometry $\gamma_p:E^4\simeq K^{4f}$ that takes $I^4$ to $\mathcal O_K^{4f}$ after localization at $p$. Put
$$
    y_p=\operatorname{ord}_{\mathfrak p_p}\det(\gamma_p\gamma_0^{-1}),\qquad
    \kappa=\prod_{p\ \mathrm{split}} r_p^{-y_p}.
$$
These choices and the finite support of the exponents are the data of `KapChoice` and its determinant properties. [Arithmetic isometry and local lattice condition](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Formalism/LocalToGlobal/KernelLattices/LateralTheory.lean#L73) · [Normalization choices](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Formalism/LocalToGlobal/KernelLattices/LateralTheory.lean#L95) · [Independence and finite support](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Formalism/LocalToGlobal/KernelLattices/KernelLocalFactors/BaseEx.lean#L246)

Let $q$ be an odd split prime with $q\nmid N(\mathfrak c)$ and $\iota:\mathbb C\simeq\mathbb C_q$. For any unitary complex map $u$ such that $M=u g_0(\Gamma^4)$ is commensurable with $S$ and $[M:M\cap S]$ is prime to $q$, one has
$$
                     |\iota(\kappa)|_q^2\,|\iota(\det u)|_q=1.
\tag{1}
$$
There exists a $u$ as above with $u-I$ invertible. For $g=u g_0$ the standard theta data then hold with $[g(\Gamma^4):g(\Gamma^4)\cap S]$ prime to $q$, and $F^{\boxtimes4}=e K_g$ for an $e$ with $|\iota(e)|_q=1$. [Determinant identity](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/GlobalInterpolation/NormKappaSqMulDetSplitPrime.lean#L10) · [Normalized split standardization](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/GlobalInterpolation/ExistsStandardIsometryFourthPowerSplitPrime.lean#L255)

## Proof

### The local determinant of a standardizing isometry

With the chosen embedding of $K$, the formula for the period generators gives
$$
        g_0(\Gamma^4)=\delta\,\gamma_0((\mathfrak c I)^4),\qquad
        S=\delta\,\mathfrak c^{\,\oplus 4f}.
\tag{2}
$$
Indeed the left lattice consists of sums of $\delta\,\gamma_0(c x)$ for $c\in\mathfrak c$ and $x\in I^4$. This uses the coefficient identity for $g_0$ on the embedded vectors and $K$-linearity of $\gamma_0$. Both lattices have $\mathbb Q$-span $\delta K^{4f}$. By commensurability so does $M$, and $u$ maps this span to itself. As $u$ is complex linear, division by $\delta$ exhibits its restriction $u_K$ to $K^{4f}$ as a $K$-linear automorphism. Its determinant over $K$ maps to $\det u$. It is unitary on $K^{4f}$ because the complex Hermitian form is a positive scalar times the standard one. [Descending the complex isometry and determinant](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/GlobalInterpolation/UnitaryStandardAtQFromIsometryOverField.lean#L29)

The full alternating self-duality of $M$ and $S$ implies their covolumes are equal in the same real space, so their two relative indices are equal. Thus both are prime to $q$. After localization at $q$, the two lattices agree. From (2), because $\mathfrak c$ is prime to $q$, $u_K\gamma_0$ consequently carries $I^4$ to $\mathcal O_K^{4f}$ after localization at $q$. This is precisely `AtP`, the condition that the map and its inverse have denominators prime to $q$ relative to the two lattices. More explicitly both relative quotient orders clear the respective period directions, and $N(\mathfrak c)$ can be used to pass from $\mathfrak c I$ to $I$ and from $\mathfrak c$ to $\mathcal O_K$. [Clearing the forward direction](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/GlobalInterpolation/FourfoldLatticeIsometryDenominatorCoprime.lean#L22) · [Clearing the inverse direction](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/GlobalInterpolation/InverseIsometryFourfoldLatticeDenominatorCoprime.lean#L38)

Put $x=\det u_K$. The local integral automorphism $u_K\gamma_0\gamma_q^{-1}$ has determinant a unit at both primes above $q$, so $\operatorname{ord}_{\mathfrak p_q} x=y_q$. Also $x\bar x=1$ by unitarity, so its valuation at the other prime is $-y_q$. These facts also show independence of each $y_p$ from the particular choice of $\gamma_p$ at the chosen $\mathfrak p_p$. For finite support, $I^4$ and $\gamma_0^{-1}(\mathcal O_K^{4f})$ are both full arithmetic lattices, with a common nonzero integer clearing the two inclusions. Outside primes dividing that integer $\gamma_0$ itself satisfies `AtP`. Then the determinant independence gives $y_p=0$ there.

At the place of $K$ induced by $\iota$, $|\iota(x)|_q=q^{-y_q}$ if it is $\mathfrak p_q$, and $q^{y_q}$ otherwise. In the first case $\pi_q/\overline{\pi_q}$ has norm $q^{-h}$ and in the second case $q^{h}$. Taking its $2h$-th root gives $|\iota(x)|_q=|\iota(r_q)|_q^{2y_q}$ in both cases. For $p\ne q$, $\pi_p$ and $\overline{\pi_p}$ are units at both primes over $q$, so the same root relation gives $|\iota(r_p)|_q=1$. The finite product for $\kappa$ thus gives $|\iota(\kappa)|_q=|\iota(r_q)|_q^{-y_q}$. This proves (1). [Only the chosen rational prime contributes](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/GlobalInterpolation/ComplexPadicNormKappaSplitPrime.lean#L64) · [The two valuations in the determinant identity](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/GlobalInterpolation/NormKappaSqMulDetIsometryEqOne.lean#L138)

### Choosing the isometry and comparing theta kernels

We give the local-standard construction at a split prime. In the $\delta^{-1}$-scaled coordinates of (2) let $L_0=\gamma_0((\mathfrak c I)^4)$. Its completion at $q$ is an $\mathcal O_K\otimes\mathbb Z_q=\mathbb Z_q\times\mathbb Z_q$ module and hence a direct sum $L_+\oplus L_-$. The alternating form in these coordinates is the trace of $\sum x_j\bar y_j/(A\sqrt{-d})$, with $A=N(\mathfrak c)$, by the area $|\delta|^2 A\sqrt d/2$. If the two values of $\sqrt{-d}$ are $s,-s$, it becomes
$$
        (As)^{-1}(x_+\cdot y_- -x_-\cdot y_+).
$$
Here $As$ is a $q$-adic unit. Alternating self-duality of $L_0$ therefore says that $L_-$ is the ordinary dot-product dual of $L_+$, since testing against each half separately gives exactly the two dual tests. If the columns of $A_+$ span $L_+$, the columns of $A_+^{-t}$ span $L_-$. The local unitary map $(A_+^{-1},A_+^t)$ carries both halves to the standard lattices. By the Cayley approximation argument of [the unitary lattice approximation](simultaneous-unitary-approximation-of-hermitian-lattices.md), choose one $K$-rational unitary $u$ sufficiently close to this map. Local lattice equality is preserved in a small open neighborhood. The absence of a fixed vector may be arranged by multiplying by a global norm-one scalar arbitrarily close to $1$ at $q$, avoiding the finitely many roots of $\det(I-Xu)$. For example $(1+q^j\alpha)/(1+q^j\bar\alpha)$ with $\alpha\in K\setminus\mathbb Q$ gives infinitely many distinct choices as $j\to\infty$. Thus $u-I$ is invertible. By (2) the image period lattice is commensurable with $S$, and their local equality gives a prime-to-$q$ relative index. [Local standardization](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Formalism/LocalToGlobal/TorsionIntegrality/QuadraticSemicharacters/QuadraticSemicharactersFactAltForm.lean#L61) · [Split standardization with the host CM signs](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/GlobalInterpolation/ExistsStandardIsometryFourthPowerSplitPrime.lean#L196)

Both finite theta kernels now satisfy the standard data with the same half-period, since $u$ is an isometry and the sign is the quadratic sign $\exp(\pi i(\sqrt d/2)H(z,z))$ on the appropriate lattices. In particular it is preserved by changing $g_0$ to $g=u g_0$, and its value on $S$ is the product of the prescribed scalar theta signs. The needed semicharacter and duality follow from [the standard theta sign proof](the-unkilled-half-period-gives-the-standard-theta-sign.md). The project first writes the same curve lattice in its standard binary quadratic basis. Changing the half-period representative by a period preserves the scalar sign and normalized theta function, so this has no effect on either kernel. [The fourfold kernel comparison](comparing-two-fourfold-theta-kernels.md) applies because $g z=g_0 z$ implies $z=0$ and gives a scalar $c$ with
$$
           K_{g_0}=c K_g,\qquad c^8=(\det u)^4.
$$
Taking norms yields $|\iota(c)|_q^2=|\iota(\det u)|_q$. By (1), $|\iota(\kappa c)|_q=1$. Taking $e=\kappa c$ in the original fourth tensor identity gives the final assertion.
