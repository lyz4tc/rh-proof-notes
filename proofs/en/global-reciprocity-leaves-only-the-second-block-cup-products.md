# Global reciprocity leaves only the second-block cup products

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $F$ be the CM host field, $k$ a field of characteristic $\ell$, and $T^D$ the cyclotomic dual of a residual representation $T$ on $k^r$. The pairing $T\times T^D\to k(1)$ is the coordinate dot product with cyclotomic action on the target. Fix the auxiliary support $Q$ of two blocks and their conjugates. For $x\in S_T(Q)$ and $y\in S_{T^D}$, use cocycles representing their classes. At the places above $\ell$ suppose that the two prescribed local conditions are orthogonal under the local cup pairing. At the other exceptional places and at the first block suppose that the local $H^1$ for $T$ vanishes. At the second block and its conjugates suppose both residual actions and the cyclotomic action are trivial. Write $\gamma_j,\phi_j$ for the selected inertia and Frobenius lifts and $\gamma_j^c,\phi_j^c$ for their conjugates. Assume the auxiliary character takes value one at $\gamma_j$ in its own cyclic coordinate and that the norms are $1\bmod\ell$. The representations are unramified outside the exceptional set and the places above $\ell$. The unrelaxed class $y$ has zero inertia restriction outside this set.

**Proposition.** There are $c_j\in k^\times$, depending on the local choices and independent of $x,y$, such that

$$
\sum_j c_j\bigl(\langle x(\gamma_j),y(\phi_j)\rangle-
               \langle x(\gamma_j^c),y(\phi_j^c)\rangle\bigr)=0.
$$

**Proof.** Fix a global primitive $\ell$-th root $\zeta$, so the twist $k(1)$ is described in exponent coordinates. For any $\mathbb F_\ell$-linear functional $\theta:k\to\mathbb F_\ell$, apply it to the cup cocycle
$(g,h)\mapsto\langle x(g),T^D(g)y(h)\rangle$. The pairing transforms by the cyclotomic character, which is valued in $\mathbb F_\ell^\times$, so application of $\theta$ is equivariant and gives a $\mu_\ell$-valued cocycle in these coordinates. Both original classes descend across the closed subgroup generated by inertia outside the finite set of allowed places and $Q$, by [unramified descent](unramified-selmer-classes-descend-to-one-finite-set-of-places.md). Their representations descend as well. Consequently the cup cocycle descends with them. Global reciprocity for this degree-two class says that the sum of its local invariants over the finite set is zero. In the source this is the Poitou–Tate reciprocity law applied with the chosen root of unity.

At the places over $\ell$, orthogonality of the local conditions makes the class zero. At the other exceptional places and the first block, the restriction of $x$ has zero class since the local $H^1$ for $T$ is zero. At a second-block place, $y$ vanishes on inertia as a cocycle: its restriction class is zero and the action is trivial. Thus the [tame invariant calculation](one-tame-invariant-pairs-inertia-with-unramified-frobenius.md) gives the local invariant
$u_j\theta(\langle x(\gamma_j),y(\phi_j)\rangle)$, $u_j\in\mathbb F_\ell^\times$.

At the conjugate prime, pull back both cocycles along the conjugation of decomposition groups. The tame calculation at the original place uses the same $u_j$ and the conjugate evaluations. In comparing the local invariants one must also transport the coefficient identification. Conjugation sends $\zeta$ to $\zeta^{-1}$, so a class written in exponent coordinates transports with a minus sign. Consequently the invariant at the conjugate prime is
$-u_j\theta(\langle x(\gamma_j^c),y(\phi_j^c)\rangle)$. This is the change-of-place law for the local invariant in the chosen common coordinates.

The surviving sum therefore equals the application of $\theta$ to the asserted expression with $c_j$ the image of $u_j$ in $k$. Every $\mathbb F_\ell$-linear functional annihilates the expression. Linear functionals separate points of any vector space, so the expression itself is zero.

**Lean correspondence.** `cupZ` constructs the cup cocycle after applying the scalar functional. `local_pair` computes the two local invariants with the common coefficient and opposite signs. `reciprocity` descends to a finite ramification set, chooses the conjugate local places consistently, removes the zero contributions, and uses all linear functionals to obtain the identity in $k$. The orthogonality over $\ell$ is a separate input. Source-based reconstruction.
