# A single nonzero theta projection keeps the local types

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Fix the CM base, definite Weil pair, model, character pair and group identifications of the [stable-range lift](a-definite-theta-lift-in-the-stable-range.md), with $n\ge1$. Let $U$ be an irreducible automorphic representation of the source, take $\phi\in U$, and take a pure theta test function $\Phi=f_\infty\otimes\bigotimes_v\Phi_v$. Here $f_\infty$ lies in the polynomial Schwartz space, each $\Phi_v$ is Schwartz at its finite place, and the finite factors are the model vacua at almost every place. Suppose $\Theta(\Phi,\phi)\ne0$. Then an equivariant projection of this lift is nonzero in one irreducible automorphic representation $U'$ of the target, and this same $U'$ has both of the following properties. At every infinite place at which $f_\infty$ belongs to the image of a compact-type intertwiner of weight $\lambda'$ for the target factor, $U'$ has that weight. At every finite split place, for every pair of smooth irreducible components $\tau$ of $U$ and $\tau'$ of $U'$ at the chosen split embedding, the local map $\beta$ in the stable-range lift statement exists. ## Automorphy and projection

The lift integral is linear in its Schwartz function and conjugate-linear in its source form. The theta kernel is continuous and left rationally invariant in each variable. The compact source quotient permits integration there, giving a continuous target function, left invariant under the rational target group. For $g,h$ in the source and target adelic groups write $R_G(g),R_H(h)$ for the respective right translations. Pull them through the integral:
$$
\Theta(\omega(g,h)\Psi,R_G(g)\phi)=R_H(h)\Theta(\Psi,\phi).
$$
Indeed the Weil action changes the kernel integrand at $(x,y)$ to that at $(xg,yh)$, and right translation by $g$ preserves the source quotient measure. At finite level an open group of target finite adeles fixes the Schwartz vector, giving the same stabilizer of the lift via this identity. At infinity the compact target orbit of a polynomial Schwartz function spans a finite-dimensional space, so the span of the orbit of the lift is contained in the image of that space under the linear Schwartz-to-lift map. These properties give automorphy in the definite model used here. [Equivariance of the integral](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ThetaParseval/ThetaKernels/ThetaLiftWeilTranslateEquivariant.lean#L14) · [Automorphy tests](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ThetaParseval/ThetaKernels/TPGLiftAut.lean#L114)

Every translate $\omega(g,h)\Psi$ of a polynomial Schwartz vector is still in the polynomial Schwartz sector. The local actions at infinity and at one finite place preserve this sector. For a general adelic element, keep the archimedean factors and the factors in a finite set of places, and replace the others by the identity. The resulting element tends to the original one as the set grows. The ratio is in a finite-adelic stabilizer of $\Psi$ for a sufficiently large set, so the finite product of local actions already gives its full translate. Thus the covariance equation holds on the same space of polynomial Schwartz vectors. [Stability under arbitrary adelic translates](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ThetaParseval/ThetaKernels/TPGLiftAut.lean#L63) · [Sesquilinear lift with covariance](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ThetaParseval/ThetaKernels/TPGLiftAut.lean#L155)

The definite automorphic space is a sum of irreducible invariant subspaces in the algebraic smooth and compact-type-finite sense. The source proves this using the invariant positive Petersson form, then extracts an equivariant projection $P$ on the automorphic space onto a single irreducible subspace $U'$, with $P\Theta(\Phi,\phi)\ne0$. To see the projection step from the sum property, select a maximal independent family of irreducible summands. If another irreducible subspace intersects the family's sum nontrivially it lies in the sum, since the intersection is invariant. If the intersection is zero, maximality adds it to the family. Thus the whole space is a direct sum and the original vector has a nonzero component in one summand. The coordinate projection commutes with the right action. The algebraic sum property for this automorphic space is an internal input to this step. ## Reading the infinite types in the selected representation

Fix one real place. Suppose $i:V_{\lambda'}\to\mathcal S_\infty$ is an equivariant map into the polynomial Schwartz space, for that target compact factor, with $i(z_0)=f_\infty$. Keep all the finite factors and the source form fixed. Then
$$
z\longmapsto P\Theta\left(i(z)\otimes\bigotimes_v\Phi_v,\phi\right)
$$
is a linear map into $U'$, and at $z=z_0$ it gives the selected nonzero projection. The covariance equation for the target factor and the equivariance of $i,P$ make it an intertwiner. Since $V_{\lambda'}$ is irreducible, its kernel is zero. Thus $U'$ has the specified weight at that place. This applies to each place with the same $P$ and $U'$. [Infinite-place argument](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ThetaParseval/ThetaKernels/TPGReadLift.lean#L96)

## Reading each split local intertwiner

Fix a finite split place $v$ of $F$, and choose the embedding $t:E\to F_v$ and the place of $E$ it specifies. The model identifies the source and target factors there with $\mathrm{GL}_n(F_v)$, $\mathrm{GL}_d(F_v)$ via this embedding and the resulting completion isomorphism. Let $\tau,\tau'$ be any smooth irreducible components in the sense of occurring as equivariant embeddings into $U,U'$ under these local subgroups. The restriction of $U$ to the source factor is an algebraic direct sum of copies of $\tau$. In fact the translates of a given copy by all other adelic factors are still copies of $\tau$, and their span is invariant and nonzero for the full group. Irreducibility of $U$ gives the sum property, and the same invariant-intersection argument gives a direct sum. The same statements hold for the restriction of $U'$ and $\tau'$. [Local groups and restriction argument](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ThetaParseval/BlockDeterminants/AbsIso.lean#L6) · [Identification through the chosen split embedding](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ThetaParseval/ThetaKernels/TPGReadLift.lean#L138)

By writing $\phi$ as a sum in the local decomposition of $U$, there is an equivariant map $j:V_\tau\to U$ and a vector $x_0$ for which $P\Theta(\Phi,j(x_0))\ne0$. The map in $\phi$ is additive, so at least one source summand contributes. Take an equivariant local projection $p:U'\to V_{\tau'}$ that remains nonzero on this new vector, using its component in the local decomposition of $U'$. Identify $\mathcal S(M_{n,d}(F_v))$ with the model's local Schwartz space using its split-place linear isomorphism. For $\Psi$ in that matrix Schwartz space denote by $\Phi[\Psi]$ the global function obtained by replacing just the $v$-factor by the model image of $\Psi$. Define
$$
\beta(\Psi,x)=pP\Theta(\Phi[\Psi],j(x)).
$$
This is linear in $\Psi$ and conjugate-linear in $x$. The original $v$-factor at $x=x_0$ gives a nonzero value. The split-place calculation identifies the model action with $\omega_t(g,h)$ in the stable-range statement. The two local group elements act only on the varied place. Apply covariance and the intertwining identities for $j,P,p$, obtaining
$$
\beta(\omega_t(g,h)\Psi,\tau(g)x)=\tau'(h)\beta(\Psi,x).
$$
This proves the split-place assertion in exactly the same $U'$ that supplied the archimedean weights.
