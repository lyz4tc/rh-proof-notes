# A half-integral norm twist vanishes under strict Satake bounds

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $E$ be a CM field, $a,n\ge1$, and $\rho$ a normalized cuspidal automorphic representation of $\mathrm{GL}_a(\mathbb A_E)$. Normalized means its split central real one-parameter subgroup acts trivially. Such a cuspidal representation is unitary. For $z\in\mathbb C$, form $\rho(z)=\rho\otimes|\det|^z$ in the project's idelic norm convention. Assume:

- At every complex embedding of $E$, every infinitesimal parameter of $\rho(z)$ belongs to $(n-1)/2+\mathbb Z$.
- At one finite place $w$, the representation $\rho(z)$ is spherical and each normalized Satake root $b$ satisfies $q^{-1/2}<|b|<q^{1/2}$, where $q=|\mathcal O_E/w|>1$.

Then $z=0$. In the trace-formula alternative of [definite unitary descent](split-place-rigidity-upgrades-weak-unitary-descent-to-all-split-places.md), this forces each apparently nonunitary block to be normalized and unitary. ## The archimedean bounds make $z$ a real half-integer

At each embedding $\tau$, the infinitesimal multiset of $\rho(z)$ is obtained by adding $z$ to every parameter of $\rho$. Since the assumed coset of $\mathbb Z$ is real, every original parameter has imaginary part $-\Im z$. The sum of all original parameters over all embeddings is zero. Indeed, differentiating the split central action gives the sum of the trace elements of the archimedean Lie algebras. The trace element at each embedding acts by the sum of that embedding's infinitesimal parameters, and the combined split central action is trivial by normalization. There are $a[E:\mathbb Q]$ parameters counted with multiplicity, so the imaginary part of their sum forces $\Im z=0$. The original parameters are now all real. [Sum of central eigenvalues](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/TraceFormula/GaloisFromTraceFormula/PartNorm.lean#L26) · [Twist effect](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/TraceFormula/GaloisFromTraceFormula/DescWeakOfRunTh.lean#L47)

For a normalized cuspidal representation the multiset at a conjugate embedding is the multiset of negatives of the complex conjugates of the original parameters, by its unitary contragredient symmetry. Thus if $x$ occurs at $\tau$, $-x$ occurs at $\bar\tau$, since $x$ is real. Pick any $x$, possible as $a\ge1$. Both $x+z$ and $-x+z$ belong to $(n-1)/2+\mathbb Z$. Adding gives $2z\in\mathbb Z$. [Conjugate embeddings in the argument](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/TraceFormula/GaloisFromTraceFormula/PartNorm.lean#L82)

## The strict finite-place bound rules out every nonzero half-integer

The untwisted $\rho$ is spherical at $w$. Its Satake product has modulus $1$ because it is the value of its unitary central character on a uniformizer. The local twist $|\det|^z$ multiplies every Satake root by $q^{-z}$. Hence for the $a$ roots of $\rho(z)$,
$$
 \prod_b |b|=q^{-az}.
$$
Multiplying the $a$ strict inequalities gives $q^{-a/2}<q^{-az}<q^{a/2}$. Since $q>1$ and $a>0$, this means $-1/2<z<1/2$. Together with $2z\in\mathbb Z$, it proves $z=0$. [Satake determinant calculation](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/TraceFormula/GaloisFromTraceFormula/PartNorm.lean#L104)

## Application to `PartNorm`

Suppose $\pi$ is conjugate self-dual cuspidal of rank $n$, and a finite list of positive-rank cuspidal classes $P_i$ has combined infinitesimal multiset equal, at every embedding, to the parameters of a dominant integral weight of rank $n$. Suppose its Satake multisets have union equal to those of $\pi$ at all but finitely many split places. Every cuspidal class can be expressed as an absolute-value twist of a normalized cuspidal representation. To obtain the normalizing twist, take the central character on the split central one-parameter subgroup, write its continuous character as $t\mapsto e^{ct}$ under a logarithmic parametrization, and choose the norm exponent canceling $c$ on the center. The rank is positive, so the determinant norm is nontrivial on that subgroup. Write $P_i=\rho_i(z_i)$ with $\rho_i$ normalized. Each archimedean parameter of $P_i$ belongs to $(n-1)/2+\mathbb Z$ because it belongs to the combined weight multiset. Choose a split place in the cofinite matching set. All roots of each $P_i$ there occur in the Satake multiset of $\pi$. The conjugate self-dual $\pi$ is unitary, so its Jacquet–Shalika inequalities give the second hypothesis for each $P_i$. The preceding argument makes $z_i=0$ for every $i$, giving the normalized unitary representatives claimed by `PartNorm`.
