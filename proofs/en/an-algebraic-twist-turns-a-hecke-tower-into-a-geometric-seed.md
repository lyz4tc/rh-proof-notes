# An algebraic twist turns a Hecke tower into a geometric seed

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Use the CM field, coefficient prime, rank, and definite-unitary automorphic datum of the [odd-rank seed](conjugate-reference-places-give-one-odd-rank-geometric-seed.md). In particular $\iota\colon\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\simeq\mathbb C$ is fixed, and $v\mid\ell$ is a spherical reference place with a lacunary weight. The geometric datum used here has four parts: a smooth projective Hecke tower with finite étale correspondences, a common system $\theta$ of Hecke eigenvalues, an algebraic Hecke character $\kappa$ of $E$, and its integer exponents $m_\tau$ at complex embeddings. In the project's convention its rank-one $\ell$-adic realization has jump $-m_\tau$ at an embedding over an unramified coefficient-prime place, and Frobenius eigenvalue $\kappa(\varpi_w)$ at the inverse Frobenius. [The geometric datum](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/GeometricFlagBases/HodgeTrAt.lean#L58) · [The representation construction](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/GeometricFlagBases/MapInvMul.lean#L43)

There are two tests for a place attached to this datum.

- The Hodge test requires that $\kappa$ be unramified at the place, that the geometric covers there supply the required crystalline comparison, and that the Hecke system occur in Hodge degree $j+m_\tau$ whenever $j$ is a required automorphic jump at $\tau$. In Lean this occurrence is allowed after conjugating the complex coefficient map and the embedding by an automorphism of $\mathbb C$.
- The Frobenius test for a Satake multiset $B$ requires that $\kappa$ be unramified, and that the characteristic polynomial specified by the tower's Hecke operators have, up to a nonzero scalar, the roots
  $$
  q_w^{(a-1)/2}B\cdot\kappa(\varpi_w)^{-1}.
  $$
  The tower congruence relates these Hecke operators to Frobenius at the place. By assumption the Frobenius test is available for every spherical $B$ at usable places away from $\ell$ outside a finite exceptional set.

Assume the Hodge test at the reference place $v$. Then a single continuous semisimple Galois representation $\rho$ has the geometric-model properties described in the [rank extraction](a-common-rank-is-extracted-from-each-geometric-model.md). It supplies the unramified inverse-Frobenius annihilator at any $w\nmid\ell$ where the Frobenius test holds, is crystalline with all required jumps at any $w\mid\ell$ passing the Hodge test, and at any $w\mid\ell$ passing the Frobenius test is crystalline with its degree-$-1$ Weil characteristic polynomial dividing a power of the normalized Satake polynomial. Each local condition holds in every compatible bridge. [Precise tests and conclusion](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/GeometricFlagBases/HodgeTrAt.lean#L54) · [Representation with all the comparisons](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/GeometricFlagBases/MapInvMul.lean#L43)

## Proof

Take the semisimplification $\rho_0$ of the Hecke eigenspace by the [étale representation construction](the-hecke-eigenspace-gives-a-continuous-semisimple-geometric-model.md). Let $\kappa_\ell$ be the rank-one realization of $\kappa$. Define $\rho=\rho_0\otimes\kappa_\ell$. Tensoring by a character preserves continuity and semisimplicity.

At a place away from $\ell$ passing the Frobenius test, the geometric comparison reads the indicated Hecke polynomial as an annihilator of inverse Frobenius on $\rho_0$, and the geometric finite-cover condition gives unramifiedness. Tensoring by the unramified line $\kappa_\ell$ multiplies all inverse-Frobenius eigenvalues by $\kappa(\varpi_w)$. The same change transports the annihilating polynomial, since for a monic degree-$a$ polynomial $P$, a scalar $c\ne0$, and an operator $A$ with $P(A)=0$, the polynomial $c^aP(X/c)$ annihilates $cA$. Thus the inverse-Frobenius annihilator has precisely the normalized Satake roots. The scalar leading coefficient of the original Hecke polynomial is nonzero and can be canceled. Doing this outside the finite exceptional set gives the almost-everywhere part of the geometric-model definition. [Unramified local comparison from the tower](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/BasicOpenSections/EsStepHolds.lean#L18) · [Twist of the annihilator](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/GeometricFlagBases/MapInvMul.lean#L53)

At a place above $\ell$ passing the Hodge test, the tower comparison first makes $\rho_0$ crystalline. The appearance of an eigenspace in Hodge degree $j+m_\tau$, via the Hodge–Tate comparison for the smooth proper tower, puts a jump of that value in $\rho_0$. The automorphism of $\mathbb C$ appearing in the test relabels the same $p$-adic embedding, since both the coefficient identification $\iota$ and the complex embedding are conjugated together. Thus the resulting jump is the one at the original induced $p$-adic embedding. The crystalline line $\kappa_\ell$ shifts all those jumps by $-m_\tau$. Its tensor product with $\rho_0$ remains crystalline, so $\rho$ has the required jump values. In particular at $v$ the required $a$ values include the lacunary set, and this proves the Hodge part of the geometric-model definition. [Crystalline and Hodge comparison inputs](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/GeometricFlagBases/GeometricRealizationOfWeightOne.lean#L19) · [Weight shifts in Lean](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/GeometricFlagBases/MapInvMul.lean#L61)

At a place above $\ell$ passing the Frobenius test, the crystalline congruence gives a degree-$-1$ Weil element where the polynomial of $\rho_0$ divides a power of the twisted Satake polynomial. Multiplying the Weil eigenvalues by the eigenvalue of the crystalline character removes the same scalar twist. The divisibility is preserved because $X\mapsto X/c$ transfers it on both sides. Thus the divisor condition is now with respect to the normalized Satake roots of $U$. [Crystalline polynomial and twist](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/GeometricFlagBases/MapInvMul.lean#L81)

## From these comparisons to the required geometric models

The input supplying the geometric data chooses one tower with the Hodge test at $v$. It can also choose a tower that passes that reference test and a Frobenius test at any one requested spherical place away from $\ell$. At an eligible target $w\mid\ell$, it supplies a tower passing the Hodge tests at $v$ and $w$, and another passing the Hodge test at $v$ and the Frobenius test at $w$. Applying the preceding construction to these choices proves each of the three geometric clauses of `GeoOne` used in the odd-rank seed. The towers and large representations can depend on the test at $w$; their eventual rank-$a$ constituent is common by the rank extraction.
