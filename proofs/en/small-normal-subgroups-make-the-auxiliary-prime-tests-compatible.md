# Small normal subgroups make the auxiliary prime tests compatible

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Fix the twist coefficient ring $O_0$ of residual characteristic $\ell$, with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$, and the index parameter $\nu$ defined by the cyclotomic determinant. Let $G_F$ be the host Galois group, $\sigma$ its conjugation automorphism, and $H$ the common open normal subgroup of the auxiliary character input. Fix the finite family of good signatures $s$, with integral representations $T_s$ of rank $r$, and choose the designated tame inertia element $z$. The inertia hypothesis on $H$ places $z$ and $\sigma z$ in $H$. Moreover $T_s(z)$ and $T_s(\sigma z)$ are scalars for all the signatures. Fix $M\ge1$.

**Small subgroup.** There is an open normal $L\subseteq H$, stable under $\sigma$, with

$$
[g,z]\in L\quad(g\in G_F),\qquad
L|_{\mu_{2\ell^M}}=1,\qquad
\bar\epsilon|_L=1,\qquad
 T_s(g)\equiv I\pmod{\ell^\nu\mathfrak m}\quad(g\in L).
$$

Here $\epsilon$ is the cyclotomic character and the congruence is entrywise. To construct $L$, take the intersection $L_0$ of $H$ with the kernels of the action on $\mu_{2\ell^M}$, of the residual cyclotomic character, and of all the finite congruence representations modulo $\ell^\nu\mathfrak m$. The open normal kernels exist because the actions are continuous and the quotients are finite. Every commutator with $z$ belongs to $H$ by normality. The cyclotomic targets are abelian and $T_s(z)$ is scalar, so the same commutator lies in each remaining kernel. This applies also to $\sigma z$. Thus $L=L_0\cap \sigma^{-1}(L_0)$ remains open normal, is $\sigma$-stable, and contains all the required commutators. Its residual congruence in particular makes $\bar T_s|_L=1$.

**Zero-corank coefficient bound.** Assume $\nu=0$ and that the ordinary and dual residual Selmer spaces have equal dimensions at each signature. Choose the [common Frobenius tests](one-list-of-residual-tests-works-for-all-good-signatures.md) to get $R$, then their list $h_a\in L$ and a subgroup $U\subseteq L$ realizing corank zero. A further open normal subgroup $U'$ and a finite exclusion list may be specified in advance of the prime selection. Use the [two-block construction](two-chebotarev-blocks-realize-inertia-and-frobenius-coordinates.md) with the smaller subgroup $U\cap U'$. The first block has the prescribed tame Frobenius classes modulo both subgroups. On the second block, Frobenius lies in $L$ because the $h_a$ do, and inertia lies in $L$ by the prime selection. Thus each such decomposition group lies in $L$, and both the residual representation and the residual cyclotomic character are trivial there. This remains true at the conjugate prime by stability of $L$.

The second-block Frobenius classes meet the chosen rank tests simultaneously for all signatures, and their first-block coordinates are the required Kronecker values. Assume the local vanishing on the first block and the mixed reciprocity identity for these places. For $M\ge4rR+2$ one has $\ell^M>4rR+2$. The [residual line argument](a-dual-number-line-gives-the-residual-selmer-coefficient-bound.md) gives, at each good signature, a unit coefficient of degree at most $4rR$ in every polynomial lift of the Selmer determinant. The choices of $R$ and the degree bound precede $M$ and the additional subgroup and exclusions. This supplies the odd-case input to the common twist.

**Lean correspondence.** `exists_small` constructs the common congruence subgroup and verifies the commutator closure. `dec_le_of_frob_mem` checks the decomposition group, and `OddBound.residual_bound_of_parts` packages the successive choices and applies the zero-corank coefficient bound. `residual_bound_of_parts` in the odd-prime assembly provides its inputs from the cohomological results.
