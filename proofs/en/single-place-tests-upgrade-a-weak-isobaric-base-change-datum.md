# Single-place tests upgrade a weak isobaric base-change datum

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $E/E^+$ be a CM extension unramified at every finite place. Let $n\ge1$ and $U$ be an irreducible automorphic representation of the standard totally definite unitary group of rank $n$. A datum $D$ consists of a finite list of positive ranks $N_i$ with $\sum_i N_i=n$, irreducible discrete automorphic representations $V_i$ of $\mathrm{GL}_{N_i}(\mathbb A_E)$, local components $\sigma_{i,w}$, and infinitesimal multisets $m_i(\tau)$. Each $V_i$ is in the conjugate self-dual ascending part of the discrete spectrum. In the project this means a discrete representation admitting an equivariant embedding into an ascending induction built from a normalized unitary conjugate self-dual cusp form of rank $a$, repeated $d$ times with specified norm twists and $ad=N_i$. [Data of the base-change theorem](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/WeilBound/RepresentationTheoryGL/ArchimedeanBaseChange/ArchimedeanBaseChangeProof/BaseChangeIsobaricNOf.lean#L21) · [Weak datum](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/WeilBound/RepresentationTheoryGL/ArchimedeanBaseChange/ArchimedeanBaseChangeProof/BaseChangeIsobaricNOf.lean#L70)

For each finite $w$ write $I_w(D)$ for the normalized parabolic induction of the product of the $\sigma_{i,w}$. At a split place the local unitary group is identified with $\mathrm{GL}_n(E_w)$. Say that $D$ matches $U$ at that place if the identified component of $U$ is an irreducible subquotient of $I_w(D)$. Say $D$ is weak for $U$ if it matches $U$ at all but finitely many split places and
$$
 \bigsqcup_i m_i(\tau)=\operatorname{infOfWeight}(\lambda_\tau)
$$
at every embedding $\tau$, where $\lambda_\tau$ is the weight of $U$. The goal here is to fix **one** of these weak data and establish all the split and nonsplit local comparisons for it. [Exact globalization theorem](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/WeilBound/RepresentationTheoryGL/ArchimedeanBaseChange/ArchimedeanBaseChangeProof/BaseChangeIsobaricNOf.lean#L39)

## Three inputs from the finite-family argument

The argument uses the following internal inputs, with $E,n,U$ as above.

- `RunSplit` gives a weak $D$ matching $U$ at any explicitly nominated one split place, and with each $\sigma_{i,w}$ spherical whenever $w$ is nonsplit and $U$ is unramified there. It may also be applied with no nominated place. [Statement](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/WeilBound/RepresentationTheoryGL/ArchimedeanBaseChange/ArchimedeanBaseChangeProof/BaseChangeIsobaricNOf.lean#L77)
- `RunInert` gives, at any nominated nonsplit $w$ where $U$ is unramified, a weak $D'$ with a stronger condition. Whenever all its factors are spherical at $w$, their Satake multisets have union equal to the unramified unitary base-change Satake multiset of $U_w$. The comparison is for every choice of the local conjugation map, uniformizer and residue size inducing the unramified unitary model and for every Satake parameter of $U_w$. [Statement](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/WeilBound/RepresentationTheoryGL/ArchimedeanBaseChange/ArchimedeanBaseChangeProof/BaseChangeIsobaricNOf.lean#L82)
- `Rigid` compares any two weak data $D,D'$ for the same $U$. At every $w$, each irreducible subquotient of $I_w(D')$ is a subquotient of $I_w(D)$. If every factor of $D$ is spherical at $w$ with multisets $B_i$, every factor of $D'$ is also spherical there with multisets $B'_j$, and $\bigsqcup_i B_i=\bigsqcup_j B'_j$. [Statement](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/WeilBound/RepresentationTheoryGL/ArchimedeanBaseChange/ArchimedeanBaseChangeProof/BaseChangeIsobaricNOf.lean#L88)

The source obtains these inputs from the frame and finite-candidate argument under `RunFin`, and derives rigidity from the unramified Satake identities and their archimedean weight strings. [Exact assembly of the inputs](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/WeilBound/RepresentationTheoryGL/ArchimedeanBaseChange/ArchimedeanBaseChangeProof/SatakeExponents/SatakeRigidity/FullThetaHolds.lean#L31)

## Fixing a single datum

Apply `RunSplit` with no nominated place to obtain $D$. In particular it has the desired weight identity and its factors are spherical at all the nonsplit unramified places of $U$. Fix any split place $w$, and apply `RunSplit` again with $w$ nominated. The corresponding $D'$ matches the local component of $U$ at $w$ by construction. It is only used to witness that this component occurs as an irreducible subquotient of $I_w(D')$. By `Rigid` that same component is a subquotient of $I_w(D)$. This argument works for every split $w$ with $D$ fixed.

Now fix a nonsplit $w$ where $U$ is unramified. All factors of the original $D$ are spherical there. Apply `RunInert` to choose $D'$ for that $w$. The second part of `Rigid` shows that all factors of $D'$ are spherical at $w$ and that the union of their Satake multisets is also the union for $D$. Thus the parameter comparison guaranteed by `RunInert` for $D'$ applies to this union for $D$ at $w$. This recovers the expected unramified unitary base-change parameters for $D$ at every such place. [Fixing $D$ and testing with $D'$](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/WeilBound/RepresentationTheoryGL/ArchimedeanBaseChange/ArchimedeanBaseChangeProof/BaseChangeIsobaricNOf.lean#L94)

Finally choose one irreducible subquotient $P_w$ of $I_w(D)$ at each $w$. At a split $w$, choose the already identified local component of $U$. At a nonsplit unramified $w$, choose a spherical constituent, which has the union of the spherical factor parameters by the spherical parabolic induction rule. At other places choose any irreducible subquotient. The statement `BaseChangeIsobaricN` imposes only the subquotient condition there. With these choices $D$ and $P_w$ have the weight, split, and nonsplit unramified comparisons required by the theorem. [Constructing the local subquotients](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/WeilBound/RepresentationTheoryGL/ArchimedeanBaseChange/ArchimedeanBaseChangeProof/BaseChangeIsobaricNOf.lean#L113)
