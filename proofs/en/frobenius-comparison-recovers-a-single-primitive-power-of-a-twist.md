# Frobenius comparison recovers a single primitive power of a twist

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Fix one twist index of residue characteristic $\ell$, one finite support $Q$, and $k\ge1$, and write $m=\ell^k$. Let $G_Q$ be the finite ray quotient used at that index, equipped with a surjective continuous Artin map $\mathrm{art}$ from the source Galois group. Let $O$ be the integral coefficient ring for the index and root level, containing a primitive $m$-th root of unity, and let $\psi:G_Q\to\mu_m(O)$ be the transported seed character. A finite-order complex Hecke character $\chi$ was obtained from it in the [retwisting construction](the-first-pass-selects-disjoint-twist-supports-by-fixed-field-place.md), using an isomorphism $\iota:\mu_m(O)\simeq\mu_m(\mathbb C)$. The complex Frobenius value, with the source convention, is
$$
 \chi(w)=\iota\bigl(\psi(\mathrm{art}(\mathrm{Frob}_w))\bigr)^{-1}
 \tag{1}
$$
for the selected Frobenius outside $Q$.

Suppose the rebuilt local packet uses the same index, support and root level, and reads the same Hecke character. Write $\psi'$ for its actual integral character. Let $\varepsilon:L\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$ be the enlarged coefficient field's complex embedding, with $\mu_m(\mathbb C)\subseteq\varepsilon(L)$, and let $e:L\hookrightarrow M$ be its map into the comparison field of the rebuilt local packet. Write $j:O\hookrightarrow M$ for the injective map of the integral root ring. The local compatibility in the source is the equation
$$
 j\bigl(\psi'(\mathrm{art}(\mathrm{Frob}_w))\bigr)=e(x)
 \quad\text{if}\quad \varepsilon(x)=\chi(w)^{-1},
 \tag{2}
$$
for Frobenius at a prime $w$ away from both $Q$ and the primes over $\ell$. Then there is a single $a\in\mathbb N$ with $\gcd(a,\ell)=1$ for which $\psi'=\psi^a$ on $G_Q$. The packet equality retains the same index, support and root level.

Indeed let $u\in\mu_m(O)$ be a primitive root, and let $x_0\in L$ be the unique lift of $\iota(u)$. Injectivity shows that $e(x_0)$ has exact order $m$. The group of $m$-th roots of unity in $M$ is generated by $j(u)$, since $j(u)$ is primitive and the polynomial $X^m-1$ has at most $m$ roots. Thus $e(x_0)=j(u)^a$ for some $a$ prime to $m$. For any $u^b$, its unique lift under $\varepsilon$ is $x_0^b$, and its image under $e$ is $j(u^b)^a$. The resulting comparison exponent depends only on the two embeddings and $\iota$.

Now take arbitrary $\gamma\in G_Q$. By the prescribed Chebotarev input, choose $w\nmid\ell$ outside $Q$, a place over it and a Frobenius element mapping to $\gamma$. In fact the kernel of $\mathrm{art}$ is open and normal, and surjectivity gives one nonempty coset mapping to $\gamma$; Chebotarev supplies a representative of this coset with the finitely many exclusions. Equation (1) identifies the lift in (2) as the lift of $\iota(\psi(\gamma))$. The fixed comparison exponent shows that its image under $e$ is $j(\psi(\gamma)^a)$, so injectivity of $j$ gives $\psi'(\gamma)=\psi(\gamma)^a$. As $\gamma$ was arbitrary this proves the character identity.

In the application, the matching Galois and twist data identify the two ray quotients and the root rings before this calculation. The old seed was chosen to pass the good-twist tests for every power prime to $\ell$, so the power just found preserves them at the rebuilt place. The support is unchanged, hence disjointness between different selected indices is preserved. This is why the first pass exports a test uniform in the primitive power.
