# The Hecke eigenspace gives a continuous semisimple geometric model

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $E$ be a number field with algebraic closure $\Omega$, and $\ell$ a prime. Let $H$ be a geometric Hecke tower in the project's sense, with smooth projective base-level variety $Z/E$ and a family of correspondences indexed by a set $\Gamma$. Assume the first leg of every correspondence is finite étale, as required by `Nice`. It is the map used for the trace. Fix scalars $\theta_j\in\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ for $j\in\Gamma$. In all-degree étale cohomology of $Z_\Omega$ with coefficients in $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$, take the common eigenspace on which the Hecke operators act as $\theta_j$. Then this common eigenspace is stable under $G_E$, and its semisimplification is a continuous semisimple representation over $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$. The output also includes an invariant flag realizing it as the associated graded of the original eigenspace representation. This is the geometric representation input `RepEx`. ## From the correspondences to a Galois subspace

Let $V=\bigoplus_i H^i_{\mathrm{\acute et}}(Z_\Omega,\mathbb Q_\ell)$. Smooth projective étale cohomology makes this direct sum finite-dimensional and gives a continuous $G_E$-action. In the Lean proof, finiteness comes from finite-dimensionality in each degree and vanishing outside the degree bound. Continuity in each degree comes from finite-level descent of cohomology classes and the inverse limit of the coefficient groups. The total action is continuous because the direct sum is finite. Extend scalars to $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$. Each Hecke operator is the specified pullback followed by trace for a correspondence over $E$. Pullback and trace commute with the change of geometric point by $G_E$, so each Hecke operator commutes with the Galois action. Hence
$$
V_\theta=\bigcap_{j\in\Gamma}\ker(T_j-\theta_j I)
$$
is invariant, even when $\Gamma$ is infinite. It is finite-dimensional as a subspace of $V\otimes_{\mathbb Q_\ell}\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$. [Finite-dimensionality and continuity inputs](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/GeometricFlagBases/RepMain.lean#L65) · [Hecke commutation and stability](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/GeometricFlagBases/RepMain.lean#L61)

Choose a basis of $V_\theta$. The action matrices in that basis define the original representation $\rho_0$. The coefficient functions are continuous: each matrix entry is a fixed linear coordinate functional of the image of a fixed vector under the continuous ambient action. A coordinate functional on the subspace extends to one on the whole finite-dimensional vector space by choosing a basis extension. Applying it to an orbit map gives the asserted continuous entry. This is the local proof of continuity used in the Lean assembly. [Finite basis and continuous coefficients](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/GeometricFlagBases/RepAsm.lean#L26)

## Semisimplifying with a flag

If $V_\theta=0$, take the zero-dimensional representation. Otherwise, choose a maximal proper invariant subspace, let the quotient be the last irreducible factor, and apply induction to the subspace. This produces an invariant flag with irreducible successive quotients. In a basis adapted to the flag the matrices are block triangular. The direct sum of the actions on the successive quotients is obtained by retaining the diagonal blocks. It is a representation because diagonal blocks multiply blockwise for block triangular matrices. Each block is irreducible, so their direct sum is semisimple. [Flag and associated graded in Lean](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/GeometricFlagBases/RepMain.lean#L59)

The change of basis uses a single invertible matrix independent of the group element. Conjugation by this matrix and selecting the diagonal blocks preserve entrywise continuity. Inversion in the matrix group is also continuous for the resulting representation because it sends $g$ to the matrix already assigned to $g^{-1}$. This proves continuity of the semisimplification and supplies the flag certificate. The construction keeps the original action and its associated graded linked so that the local annihilator and comparison conditions can be transferred later.
