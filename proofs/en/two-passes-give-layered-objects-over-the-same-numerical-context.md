# Two passes give layered objects over the same numerical context

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

This is the recursion used in [the family design](point-kits-on-the-host-windows-give-a-family-design.md). The preliminary data consist of a scale $s=(x,\varepsilon,J)$, a host and its ranks $f,k,m$, and prescribed weight arrays and word code. Good preliminary data satisfy the numerical inequalities of `ChGood`. A level extension $L_v$ of this record assigns the word primes with their window conditions and carries the positive dimension factors and common adjustment $(d_u,D_*,\Gamma)$. A context $K$ combines those numerical choices with the standard family constants. The arithmetic `Base` adds coefficient fields to the preliminary record, and a frame fixes the rational identifications used to make its columns and rows. The core includes those columns and rows, their divisor and representation data, the chosen twists, and the attached real and local models. A layered object $Y$ is the resulting compatible collection of spaces, lattices, operators and twists at the successive ranks of the column.

Fix the base-and-frame predicate $KP$, two core predicates $Q,Q'$, and a point predicate $Q_O(\kappa,D,O)$. Assume the `BaseCond`, `FirstG`, `ExportF` and `SecondG` hypotheses from [the linked bundle selection](one-family-class-supports-all-the-linked-bundle-tests.md), with the following meaning for the quantifiers. On sufficiently late good contexts, the base existence works over the prescribed preliminary data and with the required algebraic scalars in its coefficient fields. The first, export, and second passes each apply after a large-base condition given by finitely many coefficient inclusions, forced primes and permitted exclusions. Enlarging the coefficient fields with the same frame preserves $KP$. The [arithmetic field and frame construction](an-exact-arithmetic-field-survives-two-coefficient-enlargements.md) proves this base hypothesis for the exact-field predicate used in the third-place series. Assume also that at each sufficiently late good context for positive $\kappa$, every point from a $Q'$-core satisfies $Q_O$. Finally use the main volume and mass bounds for the host discussed in [the rank-two comparison](the-rank-two-genus-mass-fixes-the-normalized-volume.md).

Then after the eventual parameter choices, for every sufficiently late good preliminary record, there are $d_u\in\mathbb N_{>0}$ for each word and $D_*>0$ such that
$$
m s.L\le
\log\!\left((D_*/d_u)^{1/(fk)}\,\Gamma^{-2/(fk)}\right)
\quad\text{for every word }u.
\tag{1}
$$
The adjustment $\Gamma$ is fixed by the prescribed size constants and ranks. For every level extension with exactly these factors and adjustment, there is a layered object over that extension. It has the good size, weight, and local twist conditions, and every point obtained from it by the prescribed scale, weight, size, and canonical-instance tests has $Q_O$ whenever $\kappa$ is positive. All tuple indices are used, the twist tests hold for the primitive powers of every nontrivial chosen twist, and the point lattices are stable under integral coefficient scalars. This is `RecExP` with `QD` and $c_s=1$.

### The two passes at a fixed context

Intersect the eventual assertions for the base, the three construction passes and the conversion to layers. The intersection uses the smaller upper threshold on $\varepsilon$ and maxima of the succeeding lower thresholds. At the chosen context combine the finite coefficient and prime requirements of the passes. Include both generator primes, the discriminant prime, and the primes at most the auxiliary cutoff $X''$ in the required set. These are forced by the first pass. All these sets are finite at this context.

The initial twist datum at this set uses the trivial character on each twist slot. Its associated character set is exactly
$$
\{1,\delta_f,\delta'_f\},
$$
where the last two characters are the base determinant characters attached to the fixed frame $f$. Indeed the extra products in the set become one of the two determinant characters since the attached twists equal $1$. So the required coefficient scalars of the initial pass can be evaluated on this known three-element set. The base assumption chooses a base with the required inclusions and the same preliminary frame. It satisfies both $KP$ and the joint finite conditions. Invoke the first pass to obtain an untwisted core satisfying $Q$, then apply the export condition to that core.

The second pass applies to the exported data with that base, frame and prime set. At this step it is enough to ask for empty additional avoidance sets and empty extra finite lists of algebraic scalars. The theorem still makes the enlargement and chooses the genuine twists at the same prime set. It supplies a core with the prescribed $Q'$ and with the tests for all primitive powers of the chosen local twists. The identification of preliminary data and the frame is part of the enlargement conclusion, so the numerical context has stayed fixed while the coefficient fields and twists were chosen.

The core-to-layers assertion `LayOfCore` applies to this latter core, using its real volume normalization and the resulting mass identifications. It returns a layered object over the prescribed $L_v$ with the required weight and numerical data. Its attached object is the one from the selected $Q'$-core. In particular the local twist data of that core agree with the local twist data in the layered object. The primitive-power condition implies the ordinary twist test by taking exponent $1$. All tuples are used by the layer construction, and integral stability is inherited from the core. Thus at this fixed context one has a layered object, its $Q'$-core witness and the tuple, twist and stable-lattice clauses. This is `RecAtG`, an eventual assertion in the *fixed* context.

### Obtain the contexts from the preliminary records

At a good preliminary record, the positive integral column factors $d_u$ are obtained by the integer rank calculation `exists_du_nat`. The row factor $D_*$ is the value `DsP` of that preliminary record. Its positivity and lower estimate follow from the rank mass and gap bounds. The numerical window lemma `window_lower`, using these column and row factor estimates and the standard mass ratio, gives (1) for all words on the same sufficiently late range. These integer and lower-window estimates are inputs to this assembly. Their thresholds are combined with those for the two passes and for the eventual point property.

Now fix any $L_v$ with the prescribed factors and adjustment. It completes the preliminary data to a context $K$ because the same numerical identities verify the context clauses. Apply the previous construction to this $K$. For every point obtained from the resulting $Y$ with the four stated tests, its core witness makes it a constructed $Q'$-point in that context. Apply the eventual assertion for $Q_O$ there when the parameters are positive. The tuple, twist and stability data pass to this point, including the divisibility choice of the auxiliary prime used in its `QD` condition. As $L_v$ was arbitrary subject to the factor identities, the result has the universal level-extension quantifier needed to choose short-window primes afterwards.
