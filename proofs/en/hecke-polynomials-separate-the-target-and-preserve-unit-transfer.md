# Hecke polynomials separate the target and preserve unit transfer

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

The [trace-formula descent proof](a-finite-hecke-separator-forces-a-term-in-the-unitary-trace-formula.md) uses two sorts of finite tests. At split places they distinguish a chosen Satake multiset from finitely many competitors. At nonsplit places they remove competitors which fail inversion symmetry while leaving the stable twisted orbital integrals of the test function equal to those of the hyperspecial unit. This note gives both polynomial constructions and explains how the second is used in the estimates.

## Split places

Let $S$ be a section of the involution on the finite places of a CM field $E$, and $Z\subset S$ a finite set of split places reserved for fixed local tests. Let $B(w)$ be a system of Satake multisets, and $C_j(w)$ a finite family of competitors. Suppose for each $j$ there is a split place $w_j$ outside $Z$ and its conjugate set where $B(w_j)$ and $C_j(w_j)$ have the same cardinality $n$ and differ. There exist a finite split set $T\subset S\setminus Z$ and pairs of spherical Hecke polynomials at $T$ whose combined value is $1$ on $B$ and $0$ on every $C_j$. The second polynomial at $w\in S$ is evaluated using the inverse multiset of the parameters at the conjugate place. [Exact split separation](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/TraceFormula/GlobalTraceComparison/TraceAsymptotics/SplitLineAlt.lean#L120)

Indeed, the normalized elementary symmetric Satake quantities $s_i(B)=q^{i(n-i)/2}e_i(B)$, $0\le i\le n$, determine the multiset, because the unscaled elementary symmetric coefficients determine its monic polynomial. If $s$ is one coordinate where the two multisets give $x\ne y$, the polynomial $(s-y)/(x-y)$ has the desired values. Choose that polynomial in the first channel if $w_j\in S$. Otherwise the chosen representative is the conjugate place and the second channel sees the two inverse multisets, which are also distinct. Construct the same affine polynomial using these inverse multisets. Put the constant $1$ in the unused channel. Multiply the factors for all $j$ at their chosen coordinates and take the product over the finite set of places used. Every factor is $1$ on $B$. For $C_j$, its $j$-th factor is zero. [Affine separator and product](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/TraceFormula/GlobalTraceComparison/TraceAsymptotics/ConstantTermParabolicAverage.lean#L26)

## Nonsplit places

At a nonsplit place $w$ fix two ranks $a,b\ge0$. Write $s_i=s_i(B_1)$ and $t_j=t_j(B_2)$ for the normalized elementary symmetric Satake quantities of parameter lists $B_1,B_2$ of lengths $a,b$. In particular $s_0=t_0=1$, and $s_a,t_b\ne0$. The basic generator of the two-block Hecke polynomial algebra has value $X_{ij}=s_it_j$. Set
$$
 d_{ij}=X_{ab}X_{ij}-X_{a-i,b-j}.
$$
The algebraic identity $e_i(B^{-1})=(\prod_{\alpha\in B}\alpha)^{-1}e_{n-i}(B)$ follows by taking complements in the $i$-element subsets. The two $q$-normalizing factors are equal for indices $i,n-i$. Thus a list $B$ of length $n$ is inversion-invariant exactly when $s_n(B)s_i(B)=s_{n-i}(B)$ for all $i$. It follows that $d_{ij}=0$ for all $i,j$ if both lists are inversion-invariant. Conversely, if all the $d_{ij}$ vanish, take $j=0$ and divide by $t_b$ to obtain the inversion relations for $B_1$. Take $i=0$ and divide by $s_a$ to obtain those for $B_2$. This includes a rank-zero list, whose only coordinate is $1$. [Allowed unit tests](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/TraceFormula/GaloisFromTraceFormula/EvaluationAtUnitaryOne.lean#L25) · [Inversion detection](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/TraceFormula/GaloisFromTraceFormula/SeparatesUnitaryOne.lean#L20)

Fix a pair failing inversion invariance. Choose $i,j$ with $d_{ij}$ having a nonzero value $d_*$ there. The polynomial $1-d_{ij}/d_*$ vanishes on that pair and equals $1$ on every inversion-invariant pair. The class `U1` of unit tests consists of $1$, the polynomials $1+c\,d_{ij}$, and finite products of such polynomials. It is closed under positive powers and every member has eigenvalue $1$ on the inversion-invariant pairs.

For the geometric side the separate theorem `StUnit` states the following. Over a CM field with $E/E^+$ unramified at all finite places, at any nonsplit place $w$ and any ranks $a,b$, for every `U1` polynomial the associated spherical test function has the **same stable twisted orbital integral** as the indicator of the hyperspecial subgroup of $\mathrm{GL}_a(E_w)\times\mathrm{GL}_b(E_w)$, at each pair of separable characteristic polynomials. This equality is the extra geometric input here. The eigenvalue identity is a consequence of the polynomial formula above. [Geometric equality used here](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/TraceFormula/GaloisFromTraceFormula/EvaluationAtUnitaryOne.lean#L105) · [Spectral unit property](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/TraceFormula/GaloisFromTraceFormula/HeckeEigenvaluesUnitaryOneFactor.lean#L20)

## Enlarging a finite test while keeping its geometric value

Start with any finite split test set, disjoint from the fixed split places $Z$, and any test polynomials there. Suppose for each of finitely many competing pairs of parameter systems one has chosen a nonsplit place where at least one list fails inversion invariance. At each chosen place multiply the above unit tests for all competitors assigned there. Adjoin the resulting finite set of nonsplit places to the test. The combined eigenvalue on each designated competitor is zero. For a system inversion-invariant at all the newly chosen places, the combined eigenvalue stays equal to its previous value because each extra factor is $1$. Every newly inserted test remains in `U1` after taking its $k$-th power. At each new place its stable twisted orbital integrals are those of the original hyperspecial unit by `StUnit`. The stable geometric sum uses products of those local orbital integrals on pairs of separable polynomials, so the entire geometric value is identical to its value before enlargement, for every $k$. [Precise enlargement](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/TraceFormula/GaloisFromTraceFormula/Enlarge.lean#L33)

In an interior spectral estimate, choose one witnessing nonsplit place for each finite competitor whose two parameter lists fail inversion invariance somewhere. The enlarged test kills those competitors identically. The smaller remaining list is inversion-invariant at all nonsplit places, so its scalar evaluations on the enlarged tests are unchanged. Thus assuming the small-eigenvalue bound for that smaller list is enough to use the original estimate. For the endpoint estimate, retain competitors whenever they are inversion-invariant at every nonsplit place where the target $B$ is inversion-invariant. Kill each of the other finitely many competitors at a place where it fails and $B$ passes. The main term from $B$ then keeps exactly its original scalar. These are the strengthened estimates used in `RunTh`.
