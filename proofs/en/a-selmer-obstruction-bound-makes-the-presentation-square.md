# A Selmer obstruction bound makes the presentation square

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $(\Lambda,\mathfrak m,k)$ be a Noetherian local ring with finite residue field. Here a torsion module means a $\Lambda$-module in which every element is killed by some power of $\mathfrak m$. Let $F$ be a linear functor on torsion modules naturally isomorphic to $\operatorname{Hom}_{\Lambda}(\Lambda^a/N,-)$, where $N\subseteq\mathfrak m\Lambda^a$. Suppose for every short exact sequence of torsion modules $0\to k\to B\to C\to0$ that

$$
 \#\operatorname{coker}\bigl(F(B)\to F(C)\bigr)\le \# F(k),
$$

with both cardinalities finite. Then $F$ is naturally the kernel of a single square $a\times a$ matrix over $\Lambda$, acting on the $a$-fold power of the coefficient module.

**Proof.** The first step is to choose a sequence in which all maps $N\to k$ can be tested together. Embed $k$ into an injective module $J$, and let $B$ be the submodule of $J$ formed by its elements killed by powers of $\mathfrak m$. It contains $k$. Let $A=\Lambda^a$. By the Artin–Rees lemma there is an integer $t$ such that $N\cap\mathfrak m^t A\subseteq\mathfrak m N$. Every $\phi:N\to k$ vanishes on this intersection. Thus its composite $N\to k\hookrightarrow J$ descends to the image of $N$ in $A/\mathfrak m^t A$. Injectivity of $J$ extends that map to $A/\mathfrak m^t A$. The image of the extension lies in $B$, as it is killed by $\mathfrak m^t$. Composing with $A\to A/\mathfrak m^t A$ gives $u_\phi:A\to B$ restricting to $\phi$ along $k\hookrightarrow B$.

Take $C=B/k$ and let $\pi:B\to C$ be the quotient. The map $\pi u_\phi$ kills $N$, so defines $z_\phi\in F(C)$ through the given natural isomorphism. Its class in $F(C)/\operatorname{im}F(\pi)$ is independent of the extension: the difference of two extensions kills $N$ and so is an element of $F(B)$. The resulting assignment is injective on $\operatorname{Hom}_{\Lambda}(N,k)$. In fact, if two classes agree, then $\pi(u_\phi-u_{\phi'})=\pi v$ for some $v:A\to B$ killing $N$. Hence $u_\phi-u_{\phi'}-v$ takes values in $k$ and is a homomorphism from $A$ to $k$. Every such homomorphism vanishes on $\mathfrak m A$, and $N\subseteq\mathfrak m A$. Restricting to $N$ therefore gives $\phi=\phi'$.

The right side of the assumed cardinality inequality is $(\# k)^a$, since every map $A\to k$ kills $N$. The left side bounds $\#\operatorname{Hom}_\Lambda(N,k)=(\# k)^{\dim_k(N/\mathfrak m N)}$ from below by the injection. Hence the dimension of $N/\mathfrak m N$ is at most $a$. The submodule $N$ is finitely generated by Noetherianity. Nakayama's lemma now gives a list of at most $a$ generators of $N$, which can be padded with zeros to length $a$. Take their coordinates as the rows of a matrix $\Phi$. For any coefficient module $B'$, a vector in $(B')^a$ lies in the kernel precisely when the corresponding homomorphism $A\to B'$ kills those rows, hence kills $N$. This identification commutes with all homomorphisms of coefficient modules.
