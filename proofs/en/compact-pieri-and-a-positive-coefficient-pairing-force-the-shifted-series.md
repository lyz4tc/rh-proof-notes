# Compact Pieri and a positive coefficient pairing force the shifted series

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Use $G,F,M,\sigma$ from [the tensor-module setup](weighted-l2-coefficients-give-real-spectral-projections-in-the-tensor-module.md). Let $\lambda'=\lambda+e_s$ for the defining action, or $\lambda'=\lambda-e_s$ for the dual action, and suppose both parameters are regular of sufficiently large gap for the source's discrete-series models. Write $M'$ for the irreducible admissible module of the shifted model $\sigma'$. Let $V,V'$ be their lowest compact types. Use their compressed coefficients $\Psi,\Psi'$ from [the compact coefficient model](schur-orthogonality-sandwiches-the-formal-degree-between-two-integrals.md). In particular $\Psi(1)=1_V$, $\Psi'(1)=1_{V'}$, both transform under the compact actions on the left and right, and both are positive operators on $e^X$ for $X$ in the polar space. The tensor coefficient of the first model is assumed to satisfy the weighted $L^2$ condition on $M$ from that setup. Use also the source's realization of the shifted model inside the right regular representation, with functions $\langle v_0',\Psi'(g)v'\rangle$ in its lowest compact type for a fixed nonzero $v_0'\in V'$. Under these inputs there is a $(\mathfrak{gl}_N,K)$-embedding $M'\to F\otimes M$ into the eigenspace of $A$ with eigenvalue $\lambda_s+(1-N)/2$ in the defining case or $-\lambda_s+(1-N)/2$ in the dual case. ## A compact summand gives a positive trace integral

The lowest compact highest weights are $\lambda+\rho_n-\rho_c$ and $\lambda'+\rho_n-\rho_c$. They differ in just the coordinate $s$, and the shift is dominant on the two compact blocks since $\lambda'$ is integral and strictly decreasing. The compact Pieri rule gives a nonzero map $\iota_0:V'\to F\otimes V$ intertwining $K$. To see the rule in this case, multiply the Weyl alternant for the relevant $U(p)$ or $U(q)$ factor by $\sum_i x_i^{\pm1}$. Distributing through its alternating sum gives the sum of alternants with the corresponding one exponent raised or lowered by one. Singular alternants are zero. Every remaining shifted dominant weight occurs, and the other compact factor is unchanged. [Compact Pieri embedding](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/ReciprocityTheory/ReductiveGroups/DiscreteSeries/TensorRepresentations/ExistsKEquivariantMap.lean#L64) · [Lowest-weight shift](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/ReciprocityTheory/ReductiveGroups/DiscreteSeries/TensorRepresentations/OccBLowKShiftF.lean#L25)

Form operators on $V'$
$$
 A_g=\iota_0^*(F(g)\otimes\Psi(g))\iota_0,\qquad B_g=\Psi'(g).
$$
For $g=k e^X$ the compact covariance and the intertwining give $A_g=V'(k) A_{e^X}$, $B_g=V'(k) B_{e^X}$, where $V'(k)$ denotes the compact action. Here $F(e^X)$ is positive for the defining action, and it is $e^{-X^{\mathsf T}}$, also positive, for the dual. The compressed $A_{e^X}$ and $B_{e^X}$ are therefore positive. Hence
$$
 \operatorname{tr}(A_g^*B_g)=\operatorname{tr}(A_{e^X}B_{e^X})\ge0,
$$
by inserting the positive square root of either factor under the trace. At $g=1$ this trace is $\operatorname{tr}(\iota_0^*\iota_0)>0$. Continuity gives positivity on an open neighborhood of positive Haar measure. Each matrix-entry pairing is integrable by Cauchy–Schwarz, since the tensor coefficients are in $L^2$ by the weighted bound and the shifted coefficients are in $L^2$ by square integrability. Thus the trace integral is positive, and at least one matrix coefficient of $F\otimes\Psi$ has nonzero pairing with a coefficient of $\Psi'$.

One can take the first vector of the $\Psi'$-coefficient to be the fixed $v_0'$. If all such pairings were zero, translate on the left by an element of $K$. This changes the second model's first vector by the compact action and leaves the first model's coefficient in the same space of test coefficients. The compact orbit of $v_0'$ spans $V'$ by irreducibility, so all the original pairings would be zero, contradicting the positive trace. ## Project a smooth coefficient and take its adjoint

Fix a first vector $\xi$ for the tensor coefficient that gives a nonzero pairing. Sending $f\in F\otimes M$ to its coefficient $g\mapsto\langle \xi,(F(g)\otimes\sigma(g))f\rangle$ gives a right-intertwining map into $L^2(G)$ on the compact and derived actions. This follows by differentiating and translating, using weighted $L^2$ for all derivatives as in the tensor-module setup. Project orthogonally onto the shifted subrepresentation of the right regular representation. The projection commutes with right translation, hence with the derived action on these smooth vectors. The projected vectors are $K$-finite because the map is $K$-equivariant, so they lie in $M'$. The fixed-vector pairing above makes the resulting map $T:F\otimes M\to M'$ nonzero. [Construct the nonzero projection](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/ReciprocityTheory/ReductiveGroups/DiscreteSeries/TensorRepresentations/OccCExistsPs.lean#L83) · [Projected vectors in the algebraic module](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/ReciprocityTheory/ReductiveGroups/DiscreteSeries/TensorRepresentations/OccurrencePhiKEquivariant.lean#L67)

Use the invariant signed form $B$ on $F\otimes M$ and the invariant positive form on $M'$ to take the algebraic adjoint $\iota:M'\to F\otimes M$. It exists on each $K$-finite vector. Indeed the functional tested against $T f$ only sees the finite sum of compact isotypic spaces in the given vector's support. Each matching space of $F\otimes M$ is finite dimensional by admissibility. The signed form is nondegenerate there since $J$ commutes with the compact action and is invertible. Thus the adjoint is represented by a vector in that finite sum. Invariance gives both intertwining properties, and the adjoint is nonzero since $T\ne0$. Its kernel is a proper invariant submodule of $M'$, hence zero. [Take the signed adjoint](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/ReciprocityTheory/ReductiveGroups/DiscreteSeries/TensorRepresentations/OccDAdj.lean#L60) · [Obtain the embedding](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/ReciprocityTheory/ReductiveGroups/DiscreteSeries/TensorRepresentations/CoefEmbed.lean#L49)

Finally the tensor Casimir is the old Casimir plus $N+2A$. Its difference from the old Casimir on the embedded $M'$ is $\|\lambda'\|^2-\|\lambda\|^2=1\pm2\lambda_s$, where the sign is that of the shift. Solving $N+2A=1\pm2\lambda_s$ there gives the specified eigenvalue.
