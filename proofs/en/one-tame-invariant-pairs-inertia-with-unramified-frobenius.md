# One tame invariant pairs inertia with unramified Frobenius

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $F_v$ be a nonarchimedean local field of residue cardinality $q$, of residue characteristic different from $\ell$, with $\ell\mid q-1$. Let $D_v,I_v$ denote its Galois group and inertia subgroup. Suppose $d:D_v\to\mathbb Z/\ell^M\mathbb Z$ is continuous with $M\ge1$ and $d(\gamma)=1$ for some $\gamma\in I_v$. Let $\phi$ be an arithmetic Frobenius lift. Fix a primitive root of order $\ell$ to identify $\mu_\ell$ with $\mathbb F_\ell$ and an isomorphism $\operatorname{inv}:H^2(D_v,\mu_\ell)\to\mathbb F_\ell$. Here the action on $\mu_\ell$ is trivial since $q\equiv1\pmod\ell$.

**Proposition.** There is $u\in\mathbb F_\ell^\times$, independent of the cocycles, with the following property. Let $x:D_v\to V$, $y:D_v\to W$ be continuous additive maps to discrete additive groups, with $V$ killed by $\ell$ and $y|_{I_v}=0$. For any biadditive $\beta:V\times W\to\mathbb F_\ell$, write $\beta(x\cup y)$ for the two-cocycle $(g,h)\mapsto\beta(x(g),y(h))$ in the chosen coordinates. Then

$$
 \operatorname{inv}[\beta(x\cup y)]=u\,\beta(x(\gamma),y(\phi)).
$$

**Proof.** The prime-to-$\ell$ part of inertia contributes no additive characters into $\mathbb F_\ell$. The remaining quotient is topologically generated by one element, as in the [tame Frobenius calculation](a-tame-frobenius-relation-tests-dual-number-cocycles.md). Since $d(\gamma)=1$, evaluation at $\gamma$ detects all its order-$\ell$ characters. An unramified character into $\mathbb F_\ell$ is determined by its value on $\phi$, and the space of such characters is one-dimensional. Let $b$ be the unramified character with $b(\phi)=1$, and let $a$ be the reduction of $d$, so $a(\gamma)=1$. For any scalar character $f$, the difference $f-f(\gamma)a$ is unramified, hence a multiple of $b$. Thus $a,b$ are a basis of all scalar characters.

The cup product of two unramified characters is zero. Their cup factors through the procyclic unramified quotient, which has zero second continuous cohomology with trivial $\mathbb F_\ell$ coefficients. One way to see this is that every continuous cocycle factors through some finite cyclic quotient. In a central extension representing its class, a lift of the cyclic generator has its order-th power in a group killed by $\ell$. Pulling back along the cyclic quotient of order multiplied by $\ell$ gives a lift of that generator with the required order, splitting the pullback. Therefore the class vanishes in the direct limit. This argument also covers $\ell=2$. By the perfect local Tate pairing, the pairing with the nonzero character $b$ is nonzero. Since $\operatorname{inv}[b\cup b]=0$, it follows that $u=\operatorname{inv}[a\cup b]\ne0$. Writing any scalar characters $f,g$ with $g$ unramified in the $a,b$ basis now gives $\operatorname{inv}[f\cup g]=u f(\gamma)g(\phi)$.

For the stated vector formula, the span of the finite image of $x$ is finite-dimensional over $\mathbb F_\ell$. Take a basis $v_i$ there and write $x(g)=\sum_i f_i(g)v_i$, with scalar additive characters $f_i$. Each function $g_i(h)=\beta(v_i,y(h))$ is a scalar unramified character. Hence $\beta(x\cup y)=\sum_i f_i\cup g_i$, and linearity of the invariant gives the desired formula.

**Lean correspondence.** `tame_pairing` proves the scalar formula by using a Kummer root of a uniformizer and rescaling its tame character by its nonzero value on $\gamma$. The argument above gives the same nonzero scalar through the perfect local pairing and the unramified quotient. `exists_sum_mul_of_finite_torsion` and `inv_cup_pairing_of_inv_cup_characters` give the finite coordinate expansion.
