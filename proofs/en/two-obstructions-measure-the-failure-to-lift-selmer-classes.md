# Two obstructions measure the failure to lift Selmer classes

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $\Gamma$ be a profinite group, and let $0\to A\xrightarrow{u}B\xrightarrow{w}C\to0$ be a short exact sequence of discrete modules with continuous $\Gamma$-actions. Fix finitely many continuous group maps $\Gamma_v\to\Gamma$. Choose additive subgroups $L_v(A)\subseteq H^1(\Gamma_v,A)$, $L_v(B)\subseteq H^1(\Gamma_v,B)$, and $L_v(C)\subseteq H^1(\Gamma_v,C)$. Assume $u_*$ sends $L_v(A)$ into $L_v(B)$ and $w_*$ maps $L_v(B)$ onto $L_v(C)$ at every $v$. Let $\operatorname{Sel}(D)$ consist of the classes in $H^1(\Gamma,D)$ whose restrictions satisfy all the chosen conditions, for $D=A,B,C$.

Define the two groups

$$
 S^2(A)=\ker\!\left(H^2(\Gamma,A)\longrightarrow\prod_v H^2(\Gamma_v,A)\right),\qquad
 Q^1(A)=\operatorname{coker}\!\left(H^1(\Gamma,A)\longrightarrow
                      \prod_v H^1(\Gamma_v,A)/L_v(A)\right).
$$

**Proposition.** If both groups are finite, the quotient of $\operatorname{Sel}(C)$ by the image of $\operatorname{Sel}(B)$ is finite of cardinality at most $\# S^2(A)\,\#Q^1(A)$.

**Proof.** A class $x\in H^1(\Gamma,C)$ represented by the cocycle $z$ admits a continuous lift $c:\Gamma\to B$ as a function. Choose a section of $w$ on the discrete target and compose it with $z$. The coboundary $dc(g,h)=g c(h)-c(gh)+c(g)$ has image zero in $C$. It therefore comes from an $A$-valued continuous two-cocycle under the injective map $u$. Denote its class by $\partial x\in H^2(\Gamma,A)$. Changing the lift adds an $A$-valued coboundary to this two-cocycle. Changing the representative of $x$ by the coboundary of an element of $C$ can be matched by adding the coboundary of a lift of that element in $B$. Hence the class is well-defined. It commutes with restrictions and differences. Its vanishing is precisely the criterion for $x$ to lift to $H^1(\Gamma,B)$: if the $A$-valued two-cocycle is $db$, subtract $u(b)$ from $c$ to obtain a cocycle. If the class already lifts, a lifting cocycle can be used as $c$, after altering the original representative by a coboundary if needed.

For $x\in\operatorname{Sel}(C)$, the local condition on $w_*$ gives a lift at every $v$, so $\partial x\in S^2(A)$. In each nonempty fibre of $\partial$ on $\operatorname{Sel}(C)$ choose a base point $x_0$. The difference $x-x_0$ lifts globally to some $a_x\in H^1(\Gamma,B)$. At $v$ it also has a local lift $\ell_{x,v}\in L_v(B)$. The difference $\operatorname{res}_v a_x-\ell_{x,v}$ has image zero in $H^1(\Gamma_v,C)$, so choose $b_{x,v}\in H^1(\Gamma_v,A)$ mapping to it. For this exactness at $H^1(\Gamma_v,B)$, represent the difference by a cocycle. Its image in $C$ is a coboundary. Lift the element giving that coboundary to $B$ and subtract its coboundary. The resulting cocycle has values in $u(A)$, so comes from $A$.

Assign $x$ the pair $(\partial x,[ (b_{x,v})_v])$ in $S^2(A)\times Q^1(A)$, with the preceding choices fixed. If two classes $x,x'$ give the same pair, they have the same base point, and there is $b\in H^1(\Gamma,A)$ such that $b_{x,v}-b_{x',v}-\operatorname{res}_v b$ lies in $L_v(A)$ for every $v$. Then $a_x-a_{x'}-u_*b$ has local restrictions in $L_v(B)$. Indeed its restriction differs from $\ell_{x,v}-\ell_{x',v}$ by the $u_*$-image of that element of $L_v(A)$. Its image in $H^1(\Gamma,C)$ is $x-x'$. Choosing one representative of every coset modulo the image of $\operatorname{Sel}(B)$ thus gives an injection of the quotient into the finite product. This yields the bound.
