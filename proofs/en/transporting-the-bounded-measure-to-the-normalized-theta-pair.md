# Transporting the bounded measure to the normalized theta pair

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Use the project's CM curve ideal $\mathfrak c$, a twelfth root $\delta$ of the discriminant of its period lattice, and an admitted pair of period lattices with normalized theta vector $e$. Let $p\ne2$ split in the same imaginary quadratic base and be prime to $\mathrm N\mathfrak c$. Fix an embedding $\lambda:\overline{\mathbb Q}\to\mathbb C_p$ and a prime-to-$p$ torsion translate $t$ of $e$ modulo the pair's period lattice $\Lambda$. Take $N_0>0$ prime to $p$ with $N_0t\in\Lambda$. Then there is a unit period $\Omega_{\mathfrak c}$, independent of the pair and $t$, with the following property. For an admitted lattice list $b_1,\ldots,b_r$ giving the integral moment coordinates at this split place, the translated theta admits a linear functional on $C(\mathbb Z_p^r,\mathbb C_p)$ of norm at most one. For each level $n$, good lift $y$, and finite list of algebraic directions $D_1,\ldots,D_k$, the normalized jet of the successive torsion translates is its moment on the continuous function specified at $a\in\mathbb N^r$ by
$$
\lambda\!\left(\kappa\!\left(\delta\,\mathrm{emb}\left(\sum_i a_i b_i\right),y\right)\right)
\prod_{\ell=1}^k \operatorname{pair}_{\Omega_{\mathfrak c}}(D_\ell,b;a).
$$
Here $\kappa$ is the character of the alternating period pairing, $\mathrm{emb}$ is the complex embedding of the CM rational matrix directions, and each $\operatorname{pair}_{\Omega_{\mathfrak c}}$ is the linear form in $a$ from the scaled lattice pairing. A good lift means an element of the additive subgroup generated by $N_0\gamma x/p^n$, where $x\in\Lambda$, $\gamma$ is integral in the base imaginary quadratic field and $|\lambda(\bar\gamma)|_p\le p^{-2n}$. The assertion includes algebraicity of the Taylor coefficients of these successive translates.

### Period choice and measure norm

Extend $\lambda$ to a field isomorphism $\iota:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C_p$ as in [the coefficient embedding comparison](matching-complex-and-p-adic-coefficients-over-the-number-field.md). For the unit ideal period lattice choose the discriminant scale $\delta_0$ and the [unit period](a-height-one-formal-group-gives-the-unit-theta-period.md) $\Omega$. The ratio $\delta/\delta_0$ is algebraic and has norm one at the split prime when $p\nmid\mathrm N\mathfrak c$, by [the discriminant-ratio proof](an-odd-prime-to-q-superlattice-preserves-the-discriminant-norm.md). Put
$$
\Omega_{\mathfrak c}=\Omega\;(\mathrm N\mathfrak c)\;\lambda(\delta/\delta_0).
$$
All three factors have norm one, as required.

The analytic setup gives a complex linear isometry $E$ from the period space to the standard complex coordinate space, carrying $\Lambda$ to its model lattice and carrying $\delta\,\mathrm{emb}(b_i)$ to admitted basis directions. It matches the Hermitian pairings and the Heisenberg operators. The image of $e$ is a normalized theta in these coordinates. Its derivative data in algebraic directions are algebraic, using Taylor algebraicity in the original lattice and the algebraic scale ratio. The moment construction in this setup gives a functional realizing all the jets by [the product measure](multiplicative-shifts-turn-theta-jets-into-moments.md) and the integral coordinate comparison. [The integral theta theorem for the normalized pair](unitary-pullback-gives-integral-moments-for-a-normalized-theta-translate.md) gives bounded lattice moments along all lists of period directions after translation by $t$. Transport that assertion through $E$. The equivalence between integral lattice moments and the norm condition from [the dual direction argument](prime-to-p-translation-preserves-theta-measure-norm.md) then gives norm at most one for the constructed functional.

### Transporting each moment

[The good-lift comparison](a-split-cm-idempotent-represents-every-good-theta-lift.md) writes $E(y)$, for the purpose of both its torsion translation and its bracket with $E(\Lambda)$, as a lift in the integral moment coordinates. In those coordinates it comes from the appropriate idempotent at the split places, with the lift of order a power of $p$ and the congruence prescribed by $N_0$. The comparative identity is for both the translated theta and its bracket, so the character in the model test function is the $\kappa$ in the displayed formula. This is the good-lift input to the moment identity. The rational Heisenberg multiplier and the finite Taylor coefficient calculation give [algebraic jets at the allowed lifts](good-lift-weyl-phases-preserve-taylor-algebraicity.md).

For each original direction $D_\ell$ the standard direction supplied to the model is $(\delta_0/\delta)\,E(\delta\,\mathrm{emb}(D_\ell))$. Its division by $\delta_0$ in the model derivative thus gives $E(\mathrm{emb}(D_\ell))$, as required by the original jet. The model linear forms for these directions equal $\operatorname{pair}_{\Omega_{\mathfrak c}}(D_\ell,b;a)$, by the pairing compatibility and the definition of $\Omega_{\mathfrak c}$. Equality of the test functions on the dense set $\mathbb N^r$ gives equality everywhere. The chain rule for the linear isometry then identifies the model moment with the actual jet. This proves the unit-period moment assertion used in [the bracket-twisted point tables](bounded-p-adic-measures-give-integral-point-tables.md).

[The pairing scale calculation](the-curve-ideal-norm-fixes-the-moment-pairing-scale.md) proves the asserted equality of linear forms.
